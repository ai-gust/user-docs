Every financial report in aiGust is built to be shaped around the question you're trying to answer. Whether you're reviewing a single month, benchmarking against a prior year, or preparing a PDF for a client meeting, the view controls sit directly above each report so you can adjust the lens without leaving the page.

## Period Selector

The period selector controls the date range the report covers. You'll find it in the top-left toolbar of any report view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the period dropdown">
    Click the date range button — it defaults to the current calendar month on first load. The dropdown shows preset options and a custom range picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a preset or set a custom range">
    Select **Month**, **Quarter**, or **Year** to snap to the nearest complete period. For anything else — a rolling 13 weeks, a specific project window, or a financial year that doesn't align to the calendar — choose **Custom Range** and set the start and end dates manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the selection">
    Click **Apply**. The report reloads immediately with data for the selected period. The period label in the column header updates to reflect your choice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  aiGust uses the period end date to determine which transactions are included. Transactions dated after the period end are excluded even if they have been coded in the bookkeeping file.
</Note>

## Comparison Modes

Comparison columns let you place the current period alongside a reference point. This is where the analytical value of aiGust reports really opens up.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="vs Prior Period" icon="arrow-left-right">
    Shows the same period length immediately before the selected range. A March quarter is compared to the December quarter. Useful for spotting seasonal patterns or one-off movements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="vs Budget" icon="chart-bar">
    Pulls the budget figures you or your client have loaded into aiGust and places them alongside actuals. Variance columns (absolute and percentage) appear automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="vs Benchmark" icon="chart-line">
    Compares the client's figures to an industry benchmark dataset. Benchmarks are updated quarterly and can be filtered by industry code and entity size.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To activate a comparison, click **Compare** in the report toolbar and select one of the three modes. You can only run one comparison at a time. To remove it, click **Compare** again and choose **None**.

<Tip>
  When using **vs Budget**, make sure the budget has been loaded for the same period you're viewing. If budget data is missing for any month in the range, aiGust shows a dash rather than a zero so you can distinguish between a nil budget and a data gap.
</Tip>

## Display Modes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Absolute Values">
    The default view. Every line shows a dollar (or local currency) amount. Positive values are income or assets; negative values are expenses, liabilities, or losses, shown in red with parentheses. Use absolute values when you need to communicate actual magnitudes to a client.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Percentages">
    Switches the primary column to show each line as a percentage of a base figure. On the P&L, the base is total revenue. On the balance sheet, the base is total assets. Toggle to percentages when you want to highlight structural shifts — for example, whether cost of goods sold has crept up as a share of revenue.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Both">
    Shows dollars and percentages side by side. This mode is most useful when exporting to Excel for further analysis or when presenting to a client who needs both the "how much" and the "how significant" in one view.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Switch display modes using the **Values / %** toggle in the toolbar. Your selection is preserved if you navigate between report types within the same session.

## Expanding and Collapsing Account Groups

aiGust organises every report into a hierarchy of account groups. The top level shows summary lines; deeper levels reveal the individual accounts that feed into each summary.

- Click the **›** arrow next to any group name to expand it one level.
- Click **Expand All** in the toolbar to reveal every account in the report at once.
- Click **Collapse All** to return to the summary view.
- Right-click any group header for options to expand only that branch.

<Note>
  The account group structure mirrors the chart of accounts in the connected bookkeeping file. If a client's chart of accounts is unusually granular, **Collapse All** is a good starting point for client presentations — you can expand specific sections in response to questions.
</Note>

## Exporting Reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export menu">
    Click the **Export** button in the top-right corner of any report. A dropdown appears with format options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your format">
    Select **PDF** or **Excel (.xlsx)**. PDF is best for sharing a fixed, printable snapshot. Excel is best when you or your client want to manipulate the data further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure export options">
    Before downloading, you can:
    - Include or exclude the comparison column
    - Choose whether to export the summary view, the detailed view, or both on separate tabs (Excel only)
    - Add a cover page with the client name, report period, and your firm's logo (PDF only)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the file">
    Click **Download**. The file generates in the background and downloads to your browser's default downloads folder. Large reports with many accounts typically generate in under ten seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you export to PDF with the cover page enabled, the firm logo is pulled from your workspace branding settings. Update it under **Settings → Branding** to ensure client-facing exports always carry your firm's identity.
</Tip>

## Sharing a Report with a Client

aiGust lets you send a live, read-only report link directly to a client without them needing a full aiGust login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    Select the **Share** button in the report toolbar. A sharing panel slides in from the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the access level">
    Choose between:
    - **Summary only** — the client sees top-level group totals, not individual account lines.
    - **Full detail** — all account lines are visible.

    For most client-facing shares, Summary only is the safer default until you've confirmed with the client how much detail they want to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an expiry (optional)">
    Shared links expire after 30 days by default. You can extend this to 90 days or set a custom expiry date. Once a link expires, it shows a "Report no longer available" message rather than a login prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and send the link">
    Click **Copy Link** and paste it into your email, client portal message, or practice management system. The link opens directly to the report period you had selected when you created the share — the client cannot change the period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Shared report links do not grant the recipient access to any other part of aiGust. They cannot navigate to other clients or other reports. If you need a client to have ongoing interactive access, invite them as a **Client User** from the entity's settings page instead.
</Note>
