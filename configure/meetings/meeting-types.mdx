title: "Meeting types"

---

A meeting type is a category for your meetings, such as *annual review* or *intermediate review*. Using them is optional. aiGust doesn't provide any types: your firm creates its own.

<Note>
Only key users can manage meeting types.
</Note>

## Why use meeting types

Link each [meeting flow](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows) to one or more meeting types, and to company types. When an accountant creates a meeting, aiGust then proposes the most specific flow. For example:

| Meeting flow | Meeting type | Company type |
|---|---|---|
| Annual review, management company | Annual review | Management company |
| Annual review, operating company | Annual review | Operating company |
| Intermediate review | Intermediate review | Operating company |

Accountants can always choose any other flow. The meeting type also shows on each meeting's tile on the **Meetings** page.

## Create a meeting type

1. Click **Meeting flows** in the firm menu, and go to the **Meeting types** tab.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Enter a **Name** and a **Description**. Use **nl**, **en** and **fr** to enter them in each language. The sparkle button translates the name for you.
4. Click **Create**.

You can edit a meeting type later on the same tab. You can only delete a meeting type once no flow is linked to it anymore. Unlink it from its flows first.
