aiGust puts every tool you need for deep client work in one place — financial reports, benchmarking, KPI dashboards, and structured meeting workflows. Whether you're reviewing a client's P&L before a quarterly call or tracking trends across administrations, this guide walks you through the core tasks you'll return to every day.

## What aiGust Does for Accountants

aiGust connects to your firm's accounting data sources and surfaces the numbers that matter. Instead of exporting spreadsheets and building decks manually, you work inside a single platform that automatically generates reports, highlights anomalies, and prepares meeting agendas based on live financial data. Your job shifts from data gathering to insight delivery.

## Onboarding Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In and Accept Your Invitation">
    Open the invitation email sent by your firm administrator and click **Accept Invitation**. You'll be prompted to set a password and complete your profile — add your name, job title, and a profile photo so colleagues and clients recognise you in shared meeting views.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Familiarise Yourself With the Home Screen">
    After logging in, you land on the **Home** screen. The left sidebar contains your primary navigation:

    - **Administrations** — all client companies you have access to
    - **Reports** — generated financial statements and custom reports
    - **Meetings** — scheduled and past client meetings
    - **Insights** — AI-generated observations and benchmarks
    - **Settings** — your personal preferences and notification options

    Spend a moment exploring the sidebar before moving to a specific client.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to a Client Administration">
    Select **Administrations** in the sidebar. You'll see a list of every client company your firm administrator has assigned to you. Use the search bar at the top to filter by company name, or sort by last activity to find clients with recent data updates.

    Click a client name to open that administration. The administration overview shows the company's current financial period, connected data source, and a summary of recent activity.

    <Tip>
      Pin your most active clients by clicking the star icon next to their name in the Administrations list. Pinned clients appear at the top of the list and on your Home screen for faster access.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read a Financial Report">
    Inside an administration, select the **Reports** tab. aiGust pre-generates standard statements — Profit & Loss (P&L), Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow — based on the connected data source. To open a report:

    1. Click the report name (for example, **Profit & Loss**).
    2. Use the **Period** selector at the top right to choose a month, quarter, or custom date range.
    3. Toggle **Comparison** to add a prior-period or budget column side by side.

    Each line item in the P&L shows the current value, the comparison value, and the variance. Lines with significant variances are highlighted automatically. Click any line item to drill down into the underlying transactions.

    <Tip>
      Use the **Annotate** button (pencil icon) on any line item to leave a note for yourself or a colleague before a client meeting. Annotations carry over into the meeting agenda.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor the KPI Dashboard">
    Select the **Dashboard** tab inside an administration to view the KPI panel. aiGust calculates key ratios and metrics automatically from the connected financial data:

    - **Gross margin** and **net margin** — profitability at a glance
    - **Current ratio** and **quick ratio** — liquidity position
    - **Revenue growth** — period-over-period trend
    - **Debtor days** and **creditor days** — working capital efficiency

    Each KPI tile shows the current value, a trend arrow, and a sparkline for the last twelve periods. Click a tile to open a detailed chart with period-by-period history and an AI-generated comment explaining the trend.

    <Note>
      KPI values update automatically each time the connected accounting software syncs. The timestamp of the last sync is shown in the top right corner of the Dashboard tab.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch a Client Meeting">
    When you're ready to run a client session, open the **Meetings** tab inside the administration and click **New Meeting**. aiGust pre-populates an agenda based on the client's current financial period, outstanding KPI movements, and any report annotations you've added.

    Review the agenda items, reorder or remove topics as needed, and set the meeting date and time. Click **Start Meeting** to enter the live meeting view, which presents each agenda item in sequence alongside the relevant report data and charts. You can take notes directly in the meeting view — they're saved automatically and attached to the meeting record.

    <Tip>
      Share a read-only meeting summary with your client after the session by clicking **Send Summary** at the end of the meeting. The summary includes agreed action items, key figures, and your notes, formatted with your firm's branding.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  Review a client's Dashboard KPIs before opening any detailed report. The KPI tiles give you a thirty-second health check and flag where the conversation should focus — saving time on report review.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Use the **Insights** section regularly. aiGust surfaces AI-generated observations — such as an unusual spike in operating expenses or a declining gross margin trend — that you might not spot during a time-pressured report review.
</Tip>
