As a firm administrator, you control how aiGust is configured for your entire practice — from the accounting software connections that feed financial data into the platform, to the team members who access it and the branding your clients see. This guide takes you through every setup step in the order that makes sense, so your accountants can start delivering value to clients as quickly as possible.

## Before You Begin

Make sure you have the following ready before starting setup:

- Administrator credentials for the accounting software you plan to connect (for example, Xero, QuickBooks, or Exact)
- A list of client companies you want to add as administrations
- Email addresses for each team member you intend to invite
- Your firm logo file (PNG or SVG, minimum 400 × 400 px recommended)

<Note>
  Only users with the **Firm Administrator** role can access Firm Settings, manage data connectors, and invite new team members. If you do not see the **Firm Settings** option in the sidebar, contact your aiGust account manager to confirm your role.
</Note>

## Setup Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete Firm Onboarding">
    When you first log in as a firm administrator, aiGust launches a one-time onboarding checklist. Work through each item in sequence:

    1. Confirm your firm's legal name and country of operation.
    2. Set the default currency and fiscal year start month for your firm — these become the defaults for every new administration you create.
    3. Choose the default reporting language.
    4. Accept the data processing agreement if prompted by your region's requirements.

    Click **Complete Setup** to save your choices and dismiss the checklist. You can revisit any of these settings later under **Firm Settings → General**.

    <Tip>
      Take a moment to set the fiscal year correctly. Changing it after administrations are active can affect how historical comparisons are displayed in reports.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Your First Accounting Software">
    Navigate to **Firm Settings → Data Connectors** and click **Add Connector**. aiGust supports connections to the most widely used accounting platforms. Select your software from the list, then follow the authentication flow:

    - **OAuth-based software** (for example, Xero or QuickBooks): you'll be redirected to your accounting provider's login page to authorise the connection. Once authorised, you're returned to aiGust automatically.
    - **API key-based software**: enter the API key and any required identifiers from your accounting platform's developer or integration settings.

    After a successful connection, aiGust performs an initial data sync. Depending on the volume of historical data, this can take a few minutes to several hours. A status indicator on the Data Connectors page shows **Syncing**, **Active**, or **Error**.

    <Note>
      You can connect multiple accounting software instances — for example, if different clients use different platforms. Each connector is managed independently under Data Connectors and can be assigned to one or more administrations.
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      If a connector shows an **Error** status after the initial sync, check that the authorised account has read permissions for the organisation data in your accounting software. Restricted permissions are the most common cause of sync failures.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Client Administrations">
    An administration in aiGust represents one client company. To create your first administration, go to **Administrations** in the sidebar and click **New Administration**.

    Fill in the required details:

    - **Company name** — as it should appear in reports and meeting summaries
    - **Industry** — used for benchmarking comparisons in Insights
    - **Data connector** — select the connected accounting software that holds this client's data
    - **Organisation** — if your connector is linked to a multi-company account, choose the specific company from the dropdown
    - **Fiscal year override** — leave blank to inherit the firm default, or set a custom start month for this client

    Click **Create Administration**. aiGust immediately begins pulling financial data for this client from the connected source. Repeat this process for each client company.

    <Tip>
      Use consistent naming conventions across administrations (for example, including the legal entity type in the name) to make the Administrations list easier to scan as your client base grows.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Team Members and Set Roles">
    Navigate to **Firm Settings → Team Members** and click **Invite Member**. Enter the team member's email address and select their role before sending the invitation:

    | Role | What They Can Do |
    |---|---|
    | **Firm Administrator** | Full access to Firm Settings, connectors, all administrations, and team management |
    | **Senior Accountant** | Access to all assigned administrations, reports, meetings, and insights |
    | **Accountant** | Access to assigned administrations; cannot modify firm-level settings |
    | **Viewer** | Read-only access to assigned administrations; cannot create or edit content |

    After selecting a role, optionally assign the new member to specific administrations in the same dialog. Click **Send Invitation** — the team member receives an email prompting them to create a password and complete their profile.

    <Note>
      You can change a team member's role or administration assignments at any time from the Team Members page. Role changes take effect immediately; the user does not need to log out and back in.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Firm Branding">
    Navigate to **Firm Settings → Branding** to configure how aiGust presents your firm to clients in reports and meeting summaries.

    Upload your firm logo and, optionally, set a primary brand colour using the hex code field — this colour is applied to report headers, chart accents, and meeting summary documents. Preview your changes in the live preview panel on the right before saving.

    Additional branding options on this page include:

    - **Report footer text** — add your firm's registration number, disclaimer, or contact details
    - **Email signature** — customise the sign-off appended to post-meeting summary emails sent to clients
    - **Custom domain for client links** — if your firm has a white-label domain configured, enter it here so client-facing links use your domain rather than aigust.io

    Click **Save Branding** to apply your settings. All existing and future reports and meeting summaries will use the updated branding.

    <Tip>
      After saving, open any existing report in a test administration and click **Export PDF** to confirm the logo, colour, and footer appear exactly as intended before your team starts sharing documents with clients.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're Ready to Go

With your data connector active, administrations created, team invited, and branding in place, your firm is fully set up in aiGust. Share the **Accountant Quickstart** guide with your team so they can get up to speed on day-to-day workflows.
