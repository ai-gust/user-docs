aiGust uses a role-based access model to ensure every team member can do their job without being exposed to data or settings they don't need. You invite colleagues into your workspace, assign them a role that matches their responsibilities, and optionally scope their access to specific client administrations. This page explains how to invite users, what each role can do, and how to manage access over time.

## Inviting a new user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Users & Roles">
    In the left sidebar, click **Settings**, then select **Users & Roles**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Invite user">
    Select **+ Invite user** in the top-right corner. An invitation panel opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the user's details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | **Email address** | The address where the invitation will be sent |
    | **First and last name** | Pre-fills the user's profile; they can update it on first login |
    | **Role** | Select a role from the dropdown (see [Role descriptions](#available-roles) below) |
    | **Administration access** | Choose **All administrations** or select specific ones from the list |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send invitation**. aiGust emails the invitation with a secure sign-up link that expires after **72 hours**. If the invite expires, return to **Users & Roles**, find the pending invite, and click **Resend**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The invited user must complete their account setup — choosing a password and accepting the terms — before they appear as active in the Users list. Pending invitations are shown separately under the **Pending** tab.
</Note>

## Available roles

aiGust provides two built-in roles. Each role grants a fixed set of permissions across the workspace.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-halved">
    Full access to all workspace settings, all client administrations, user management, billing, and branding. Admins can invite, edit, and deactivate other users — including other Admins.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accountant" icon="calculator">
    Access to assigned client administrations only. Accountants can import data, generate reports, run benchmarks, manage meetings, and grant external access to clients. They cannot access workspace-level settings, billing, or user management.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Permissions at a glance

| Action | Admin | Accountant |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Access all administrations | ✅ | ❌ (assigned only) |
| Create / delete administrations | ✅ | ❌ |
| Import financial data | ✅ | ✅ |
| Generate and export reports | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage client meetings | ✅ | ✅ |
| Grant external access to clients | ✅ | ✅ |
| Edit firm branding & email templates | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite and manage users | ✅ | ❌ |
| View billing and subscription | ✅ | ❌ |
| View the audit trail | ✅ | ❌ |

## Changing a user's role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user's profile">
    Go to **Settings → Users & Roles** and click the name of the user you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the role">
    In the **Role** dropdown, select the new role. The change is applied immediately — no email is sent to the user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust administration access if needed">
    If you are downgrading a user from Admin to Accountant, review the **Administration access** field and confirm which administrations they should retain access to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save changes** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deactivating or removing a user

### Deactivating a user

Deactivation prevents a user from logging in but preserves their activity history in the audit trail. Use this when a team member is on extended leave or their role has temporarily changed.

1. Go to **Settings → Users & Roles** and open the user's profile.
2. Click **Deactivate user** and confirm in the dialogue.
3. The user is immediately signed out and cannot sign in again until reactivated.

To reactivate, open the deactivated user's profile (visible under the **Inactive** tab) and click **Reactivate user**.

### Removing a user

Removing a user permanently severs their access to the workspace. Their audit trail entries are retained, but the account cannot be recovered.

<Warning>
  Removing a user is irreversible. If the person might rejoin the firm in future, deactivate them instead so you can simply reactivate their account without issuing a new invitation.
</Warning>

1. Go to **Settings → Users & Roles** and open the user's profile.
2. Click **Remove user** at the bottom of the profile panel.
3. Confirm by typing the user's email address, then click **Permanently remove**.

Removal can only be performed by a user with the **Admin** role.

## Best practices for access control

<Accordion title="Apply the principle of least privilege">
  Assign the Accountant role as the default for all non-administrative staff. Only users who genuinely need to manage billing, branding, or other users should hold the Admin role. Limiting Admin accounts reduces the risk of accidental configuration changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Scope administration access explicitly">
  When inviting Accountants, grant access only to the specific client administrations they will work on rather than selecting "All administrations". Adjust access as the accountant takes on new clients or hands over existing ones.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Review the user list quarterly">
  Schedule a quarterly review of **Settings → Users & Roles** to check for staff who have left the firm and still have active accounts, or Admins whose responsibilities no longer require elevated access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Deactivate before removing">
  Default to deactivating rather than removing departing users. Deactivation preserves the full audit trail linked to that account, which may be valuable for compliance reviews or client handovers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Use the audit trail to monitor privileged actions">
  Admin-level actions — such as creating or deleting administrations, changing user roles, and modifying branding — are all logged in the audit trail. Review it regularly to detect any unexpected changes. See [Usage & Audit Trail](/firm-setup/usage-and-audit-trail) for details.
</Accordion>
