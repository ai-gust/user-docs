When you first sign in to aiGust, the onboarding wizard guides you through every step needed to configure your workspace before you start adding clients. Completing the wizard fully ensures that reports, emails, and exported documents all carry accurate firm information from day one.

## What happens during onboarding

The onboarding wizard is a one-time, linear flow that collects your firm's identity details, sets default preferences, and confirms your subscription. You can pause and return at any point — aiGust saves your progress automatically. Once you submit the final step, your workspace becomes active and you can begin creating client administrations.

<Note>
  Only the account owner who completed the initial sign-up can run the onboarding wizard. Other team members can be invited after onboarding is finished.
</Note>

## Step-by-step walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome screen">
    Read the overview of what the wizard covers, then click **Get started** to begin. The progress bar at the top of each screen shows how far through the wizard you are.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your firm details">
    Fill in the fields that identify your firm. Every field marked with an asterisk is required before you can advance.

    | Field | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | **Firm name** | Appears on all reports and client-facing emails |
    | **Trading name** | Optional — use if your public-facing name differs from the legal name |
    | **Street address** | Full postal address including postcode |
    | **Country** | Sets the default locale for currency and date formats |
    | **VAT / tax number** | Printed on formal report footers where required |
    | **Chamber of Commerce number** | Optional — relevant for Dutch and EU firms |
    | **Website URL** | Linked from client email footers |
    | **Main contact email** | Used for system notifications and support correspondence |

    Click **Save and continue** when all required fields are complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure firm-level preferences">
    Set the defaults that apply across every client administration in your workspace. You can override most of these at the individual administration level later.

    - **Default currency** — the currency used in financial summaries unless overridden per client.
    - **Default financial year start** — the month your clients' fiscal years typically begin.
    - **Number format** — choose between period (1.000,00) or comma (1,000.00) separators.
    - **Date format** — DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, or YYYY-MM-DD.
    - **Language** — the language used in generated reports and client-facing content.
    - **Time zone** — affects scheduled report delivery and audit log timestamps.

    <Tip>
      If your firm serves clients across multiple countries, set preferences to match your most common client profile. You can always override them for individual administrations.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your firm logo">
    Upload a logo that will appear on reports, dashboards, and client emails. Accepted formats are PNG, JPG, and SVG. The recommended minimum size is 400 × 400 px with a transparent background.

    You can skip this step and add a logo later under **Settings → Branding**. See [Branding & Emails](/firm-setup/branding-and-emails) for full guidance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    The final screen shows a summary of everything you have entered. Review each section and click **Edit** next to any section you want to change. When everything is correct, click **Complete onboarding** to activate your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revisiting your firm details

You can update any information entered during onboarding at any time by going to **Settings → Firm details**. Changes take effect immediately on all newly generated reports; previously exported documents are not retroactively updated.

<Warning>
  Changing your firm name or VAT number updates the information on all future reports but does not alter historical exports already delivered to clients. If a correction is critical, re-export and re-send the affected reports.
</Warning>
