Connecting Exact Online to aiGust lets you pull client financial data directly into your workspace without any manual exports. Once the connection is active, aiGust syncs your chart of accounts, transactions, and period balances on an automatic schedule, so your reports and dashboards always reflect the latest figures from Exact Online.

<Note>
  **Before you begin, confirm you have the following:**
  - An active Exact Online subscription (any edition that includes API access)
  - Administrator or IT Manager rights in the Exact Online division you want to connect
  - An aiGust account with the **Firm Admin** role
</Note>

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connectors page in aiGust">
    Log in to aiGust and go to **Settings → Connectors**. Click **Add connector** and select **Exact Online** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your Exact Online region">
    Select the region that matches your Exact Online environment — **Netherlands**, **Belgium**, **United Kingdom**, **Germany**, **Spain**, or **United States**. The region must match the country your Exact Online subscription is registered in. Selecting the wrong region will cause the OAuth flow to fail.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initiate the OAuth authorisation">
    Click **Connect with Exact Online**. aiGust redirects you to the Exact Online login page. Sign in with the Exact Online account that has administrator access to the divisions you want to sync.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorise aiGust in Exact Online">
    After signing in, Exact Online displays a permissions consent screen listing the data aiGust is requesting access to. Review the listed permissions — aiGust requests read-only access to financial data. Click **Authorise** to grant access and return to aiGust.

    <Warning>
      You must authorise with an Exact Online account that has **IT Manager** or **Administrator** rights. Authorising with a standard user account will result in incomplete data access and sync errors later.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select administrations to sync">
    After authorisation, aiGust loads the list of Exact Online divisions (administrations) available under the connected account. Toggle on each division you want to sync into aiGust. For each division, choose or create the corresponding administration in aiGust using the dropdown on the right.

    <Tip>
      You can connect additional divisions later by returning to **Settings → Connectors** and clicking **Manage administrations** on the Exact Online connector.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the initial sync">
    Click **Save and sync**. aiGust begins the initial full sync for each selected administration. The status indicator changes to **Syncing**. The first sync downloads your full transaction history and chart of accounts, which may take several minutes per administration depending on data volume.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verifying the Connection

Once the initial sync completes, the status for each administration changes to **Up to date** with a timestamp. To confirm the data is correct:

1. Open an administration in aiGust and navigate to **Financial data → Chart of accounts**. Verify that the account codes and names match those in Exact Online.
2. Open **Financial data → Transactions** and check that recent postings from Exact Online appear with the correct dates and amounts.
3. If you notice missing accounts or transactions, check the sync log under **Settings → Connectors** for any warnings.

<Tip>
  After the initial sync completes, aiGust syncs Exact Online data automatically every four hours. To pull in changes immediately — for example, after a period close — go to **Settings → Connectors** and click **Sync now** next to the relevant administration.
</Tip>
