A connector is the secure bridge between aiGust and your accounting software. Once you set up a connector, aiGust reads financial data directly from your accounting package and keeps your workspace current — no manual exports, no copy-pasting figures between systems. Every report, dashboard, and client analysis in aiGust is powered by this live connection, so the accuracy of your insights depends on the health of your connectors.

## What a Connector Does

Each connector authenticates aiGust with a specific accounting package, either through OAuth (where you grant access via your browser) or through API credentials (where you supply a key or username and password). After authentication, aiGust is granted read-only access to the financial data in your accounting environment. aiGust never writes data back to your accounting software.

Connectors are set up at the firm level. Once a connector is active, you map individual client administrations or companies inside that accounting package to the corresponding administrations in aiGust.

## How the Sync Cycle Works

After a connector is established, aiGust syncs data automatically on a scheduled cycle. The default sync frequency is **every four hours**. You can also trigger a manual sync at any time from the connector settings page.

Each sync run is incremental. aiGust does not re-download all historical data on every cycle — it fetches only the records that have changed or been added since the previous successful sync. This keeps sync times short and reduces load on your accounting package's API.

The first sync after connecting a new administration is a **full historical sync**. Depending on the volume of transactions, this initial sync can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours. aiGust shows a progress indicator while the initial sync is running.

<Tip>
  Schedule any bulk data entry or period-end closing work in your accounting software before triggering a manual sync. Running a sync immediately after a large batch of changes ensures aiGust reflects the final state of your books.
</Tip>

## What Data Is Synced

aiGust pulls three categories of financial data from your accounting package:

**Chart of accounts** — The full account structure, including account codes, account names, account types (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses), and any grouping or hierarchy defined in your accounting software. Changes to your chart of accounts — such as adding a new cost centre or renaming an account — are reflected in aiGust at the next sync.

**Transactions** — Individual journal entries and ledger postings, including the posting date, accounting period, debit and credit amounts, account code, description, and any reference numbers. aiGust uses these transactions to calculate period-level figures for all reports.

**Balances** — Opening and closing balances per account per period, as reported by the accounting package. Where your accounting software provides pre-calculated trial balance figures, aiGust uses those as the authoritative source. Where only transactions are available, aiGust derives balances from the transaction history.

<Note>
  Attachments, PDF invoices, and non-financial records (such as CRM contacts or inventory items) are not synced. aiGust only pulls the accounting data needed for financial reporting and analysis.
</Note>

## Sync Status Indicators

Every administration in aiGust displays a sync status badge so you can see at a glance whether data is current. There are three states:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Syncing** | A sync run is currently in progress. Data may be incomplete until the run finishes. |
| **Up to date** | The last sync completed successfully. The timestamp shows when the most recent sync finished. |
| **Error** | The last sync attempt failed. aiGust was unable to retrieve data from the accounting package. |

You can see the sync status for all administrations from **Settings → Connectors**. Click any administration to view the detailed sync log, including the time of the last successful sync and the error message for any failed run.

## Troubleshooting a Failed Sync

When a sync shows an **Error** status, aiGust displays an error message in the sync log that identifies the cause. Work through the following steps to resolve common issues.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the error message">
    Open **Settings → Connectors**, select the affected administration, and read the error message in the sync log. Common messages include expired credentials, revoked access, rate limit exceeded, and administration not found.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Re-authenticate if credentials have expired">
    OAuth tokens and API keys can expire or be revoked. If the error indicates an authentication failure, go to **Settings → Connectors**, select the connector, and click **Reconnect**. Follow the authentication flow again to issue a fresh token or enter updated credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check permissions in your accounting software">
    If you recently changed the user account or API key aiGust uses to connect, verify that the account still has sufficient permissions. aiGust requires read access to the chart of accounts, general ledger, and period balances. Refer to the individual connector guide for the exact permissions required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check for planned maintenance">
    Some accounting packages perform scheduled maintenance that temporarily disables API access. Check your accounting software's status page or support portal. If maintenance is in progress, wait for it to complete, then trigger a manual sync from aiGust.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trigger a manual sync">
    After resolving the underlying issue, go to **Settings → Connectors**, select the administration, and click **Sync now**. Watch the status indicator — if it transitions to **Up to date**, the issue is resolved.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact support">
    If the error persists after re-authenticating and triggering a manual sync, contact aiGust support at support@aigust.io. Include the error message from the sync log and the name of the connector so the support team can investigate quickly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If a sync error is left unresolved, aiGust continues to display the most recently synced data. Reports and dashboards will not reflect any changes made in your accounting software after the last successful sync. Resolve sync errors promptly to keep your client data accurate.
</Warning>
