Connecting Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central to aiGust gives your firm live access to chart of accounts data, journal transactions, and period balances from your Business Central companies. The connection uses Microsoft OAuth, so you authorise aiGust through your existing Microsoft 365 account — no API keys to manage and no passwords to share with aiGust.

<Note>
  **Before you begin, confirm you have the following:**
  - A Microsoft 365 account with access to a Business Central environment
  - The **D365 Finance** or **D365 Business Central** licence assigned to the Microsoft account you will use to authorise the connection
  - **Read** permissions on the General Ledger, Chart of Accounts, and Companies in Business Central
  - An aiGust account with the **Firm Admin** role
</Note>

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connectors page in aiGust">
    Log in to aiGust and go to **Settings → Connectors**. Click **Add connector** and select **Microsoft Business Central** from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initiate the Microsoft OAuth flow">
    Click **Connect with Microsoft**. aiGust redirects you to the Microsoft login page. Sign in with the Microsoft 365 account that has access to the Business Central environment you want to connect.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorise aiGust in Microsoft">
    After signing in, Microsoft displays a permissions consent screen. This screen lists the permissions aiGust is requesting — read-only access to your Business Central financial data. Review the listed permissions and click **Accept** to authorise the connection and return to aiGust.

    <Warning>
      If your Microsoft 365 tenant requires admin consent for third-party app authorisations, a Microsoft tenant administrator must approve the aiGust app before you can complete this step. Ask your IT administrator to grant admin consent in the Azure Active Directory portal under **Enterprise Applications**.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a Business Central environment">
    After authorisation, aiGust loads the list of Business Central environments available under the connected Microsoft account. Select the environment (for example, **Production** or **Sandbox**) that contains the companies you want to sync.

    <Tip>
      Connect to your **Production** environment for live client data. Only use a Sandbox environment for testing the aiGust integration before rolling it out to your full client portfolio.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select companies to sync">
    aiGust loads the list of companies within the selected Business Central environment. Toggle on each company you want to sync. For each company, select or create the corresponding administration in aiGust using the dropdown on the right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the initial sync">
    Click **Save and sync**. aiGust begins the initial full sync for each selected company. The status indicator changes to **Syncing**. The first sync fetches the complete chart of accounts and full transaction history, which may take several minutes per company depending on data volume.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verifying the Connection

Once the initial sync completes, the status for each company changes to **Up to date** with a timestamp. To confirm the data is correct:

1. Open an administration in aiGust and go to **Financial data → Chart of accounts**. Verify that account numbers and names match those in the corresponding Business Central company.
2. Go to **Financial data → Transactions** and check that general ledger entries appear with the correct posting dates, amounts, and account references.
3. If data looks incomplete or you notice missing periods, go to **Settings → Connectors**, select the Business Central connector, and review the sync log for any errors.

<Tip>
  aiGust syncs Business Central data automatically every four hours. To pull in the latest data immediately after a period close or after posting a large batch of entries, go to **Settings → Connectors** and click **Sync now** next to the relevant administration.
</Tip>
