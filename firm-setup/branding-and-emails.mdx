Every report, dashboard, and automated email that aiGust sends to your clients carries your firm's identity. Configuring your branding ensures a consistent, professional appearance across all client-facing output — from the cover page of a management report to the invitation email a new client receives on day one. You control every visible element: logo, colours, and message content.

## Uploading a firm logo

Your logo appears on the header of generated reports, on the client-facing portal, and in the email header of messages sent from aiGust.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Branding settings">
    Go to **Settings → Branding & Emails** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    In the **Logo** section, click **Upload logo** and select your image file. Accepted formats are **PNG**, **JPG**, and **SVG**.

    | Recommendation | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | Minimum size | 400 × 400 px |
    | Maximum file size | 5 MB |
    | Background | Transparent background preferred for reports |
    | Aspect ratio | Square or landscape logos work best |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Crop and position (optional)">
    Use the on-screen crop tool to adjust how the logo is framed. Drag the handles to resize the crop area, then click **Apply crop**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your logo">
    Click **Save** in the Logo section. The new logo takes effect on all reports generated from this point forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Previously exported PDF reports are not retroactively updated when you change your logo. Only future exports will carry the new image.
</Note>

## Setting brand colours

Brand colours are applied to report accents, chart palettes, button highlights in the client portal, and email call-to-action elements.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Colours section">
    On the **Branding & Emails** settings page, scroll down to **Brand colours**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your primary colour">
    Click the colour swatch next to **Primary colour**. Enter a hex code (e.g. `#1A4B8C`) directly into the input field, or use the colour picker to choose visually. The primary colour is used for headings, chart fills, and key highlights.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your secondary colour (optional)">
    The **Secondary colour** is used for accents, sub-headings, and secondary chart series. If left blank, aiGust derives a complementary shade automatically from your primary colour.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the palette">
    Click **Preview** to open a sample report page rendered with your chosen colours. Check that the colour contrast is legible for both screen viewing and PDF printing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save colours">
    Click **Save colours**. The palette applies to all reports and portal views generated going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Aim for a primary colour contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against white backgrounds to meet WCAG AA accessibility standards. The preview panel shows a contrast score to help you check this.
</Tip>

## Customising email templates

aiGust sends several types of automated emails on behalf of your firm — access invitations, report delivery notifications, meeting reminders, and more. You can customise the content of each template while aiGust handles delivery and formatting.

### Editing a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    In **Settings → Branding & Emails**, scroll to the **Email templates** section. Click the name of the template you want to edit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the subject line">
    Update the **Subject** field. You can include dynamic variables that aiGust replaces at send time:

    | Variable | Replaced with |
    |---|---|
    | `{{client_name}}` | The name of the client administration |
    | `{{firm_name}}` | Your firm name from Settings |
    | `{{report_title}}` | The title of the relevant report |
    | `{{meeting_date}}` | The date of the scheduled meeting |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the body content">
    Use the rich-text editor to modify the email body. You can add, remove, or rearrange paragraphs. The same dynamic variables are available in the body.

    <Warning>
      Do not remove the `{{action_button}}` variable from invitation and report notification templates. This variable generates the link button that recipients use to access the platform or document. Removing it will break the email flow.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the template">
    Click **Save template** to store your changes. The updated template is used for all emails of that type sent from this point forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Resetting a template to default

If a template has been edited and you want to restore the original text, open the template and click **Reset to default** in the top-right corner of the editor. Confirm the reset in the dialogue. This cannot be undone, so copy any custom text you want to keep before resetting.

## Previewing and testing branded emails

Before you send branded emails to clients, verify how they look in a real inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template you want to test">
    Navigate to **Settings → Branding & Emails → Email templates** and open any template.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Send test email">
    Click **Send test email** in the top-right corner of the template editor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter a recipient address">
    Type the email address where you want to receive the test — typically your own address or a team inbox. Click **Send**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the result">
    Check the received email for layout, logo rendering, colour accuracy, and that all dynamic variables have resolved correctly. If the email is landing in spam, check that your firm's sending domain has been verified under **Settings → Email domain**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Test emails in both a desktop email client and a mobile email client. Logo and layout rendering can differ significantly between the two environments.
</Tip>
