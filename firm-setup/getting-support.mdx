If you run into an issue or have a question about how aiGust works, several support channels are available to you. Most questions are answered quickly in the documentation or through the in-app help centre. For issues that need direct attention, the aiGust support team is reachable by email and live chat, and will respond based on your subscription tier's service level.

## In-app help and documentation

The fastest way to get an answer is to check the resources built directly into aiGust.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Help centre" icon="circle-question">
    Click the **?** icon in the bottom-right corner of any page in aiGust to open the help centre. Search by keyword to find relevant documentation articles, step-by-step guides, and short video walkthroughs without leaving the platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contextual tips" icon="lightbulb">
    Many fields and settings panels display a small info icon (**ⓘ**) next to them. Hover over it to read a concise explanation of what the field does and how to fill it in correctly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="This documentation" icon="book-open">
    The aiGust documentation site (where you are reading this now) contains full reference guides, feature overviews, and how-to articles. Use the search bar at the top of any page to find what you need.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Release notes" icon="megaphone">
    Find the latest release notes under **Settings → What's new** in the platform, or on the documentation site under **Release notes**. Release notes describe new features, improvements, and resolved issues in each update.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Contacting the support team

If you cannot find the answer in the documentation or the help centre, reach the aiGust support team through the following channels.

### Live chat

For quick questions, use live chat — available directly inside the platform:

1. Click the **?** icon in the bottom-right corner of any aiGust page.
2. Select **Chat with support** at the bottom of the help centre panel.
3. Type your question. A support agent joins the conversation as soon as one is available. During off-hours, the chat automatically converts to an email ticket and a response is sent to your registered email address.

### Email support

For detailed questions, bug reports, or issues that require screenshots or attachments, email the support team at **support@aigust.io**. Tickets are assigned a reference number and tracked through to resolution.

### Response times

| Plan | Live chat | Email |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Next business day | 2 business days |
| **Professional** | Same business day | 1 business day |
| **Enterprise** | Priority queue, same day | 4 business hours |

## What to include in a support request

A well-prepared support request helps the team diagnose and resolve your issue faster. Before reaching out, gather the following information:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you were trying to do">
    Explain the task you were working on when the issue occurred — for example, "I was generating a management report for the administration named Bakker & Zonen BV."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe what happened and what you expected">
    State what aiGust actually did and what you expected it to do instead — for example, "The export produced a blank PDF. I expected a fully formatted report."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Include the exact error message if one appeared">
    Copy the full text of any error message or, if it is easier, take a screenshot and attach it to your email or chat message.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Note the steps to reproduce the issue">
    List the exact steps you took before the problem appeared. This allows the support team to replicate the issue in a test environment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Include relevant identifiers">
    Provide the name of the affected administration, the report title (if applicable), and the date and time when the issue occurred. If you know it, include the browser you are using (e.g. Chrome 124, Safari 17) and your operating system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can find your aiGust **workspace ID** under **Settings → Firm details** at the bottom of the page. Including it in your support request lets the team locate your workspace immediately without asking for it separately.
</Tip>

## Community resources

<Accordion title="aiGust user community">
  The aiGust community forum is a space where accounting professionals using aiGust share tips, workflows, and feature ideas. Browse existing threads or post a question to get input from other practitioners. Access the community at **community.aigust.io** or via the link in the help centre panel under **Community**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Webinars and live training">
  aiGust hosts monthly live webinars covering new features, advanced workflows, and best practices for accounting firms. Register for upcoming sessions under **Settings → Training & events** or on the community site. Recordings of past webinars are available in the same location.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Feature requests">
  If you have an idea for a new feature or an improvement to an existing one, submit it through the **Share feedback** option in the help centre panel. Upvote requests from other users to signal priority to the product team. The most popular requests are reviewed during each quarterly planning cycle.
</Accordion>
