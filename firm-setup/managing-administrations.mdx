An administration in aiGust is the central workspace for a single client entity — it holds that client's financial data, generated reports, benchmark results, and meeting records. Every action you take in aiGust, from importing a ledger to running a performance analysis, happens inside a specific administration. Keeping administrations well-organised makes it easy to navigate your client portfolio and ensures the right data reaches the right people.

## What is an administration?

Each administration represents one legal entity or client file. A single client group with multiple subsidiaries would typically have one administration per entity. Administrations are isolated from one another, so data from one client is never mixed with another's.

## Creating a new administration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Administrations list">
    In the left sidebar, click **Administrations**. The list shows all administrations your account has access to.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click New administration">
    Select **+ New administration** in the top-right corner of the list. A setup panel slides in from the right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the administration details">
    Fill in the fields in the setup panel:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Administration name** | The client's trading or legal name as it should appear in reports |
    | **Client reference** | Your internal client code or number (optional but recommended) |
    | **Sector / industry** | Used for benchmarking against sector peers |
    | **Company type** | e.g. Private limited, Sole trader, Partnership, Foundation |
    | **Financial year start** | The month the client's fiscal year begins |
    | **Currency** | The reporting currency for this administration |
    | **Country** | Determines locale defaults for this client |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the administration">
    Click **Create administration**. aiGust creates the workspace and opens it immediately. You can now connect a data source or import a file to begin populating the administration with financial data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a consistent naming convention across all administrations — for example, including the legal entity type in the name ("Bakker & Zonen BV") — to make bulk searches faster and reduce confusion when multiple clients share similar names.
</Tip>

## Editing administration details

You can update the details of any administration at any time without losing existing data or reports.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the administration">
    Click the administration name in the **Administrations** list to open it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Inside the administration, click **Settings** in the top navigation bar, then select **Administration details**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Update any field — name, sector, company type, financial period, currency, or country — then click **Save changes**. The update applies to all reports generated from this point forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changing the **financial year start** does not reprocess historical data. It affects how future reporting periods are calculated. If you need to reprocess data under a new period structure, re-import your source files after saving the change.
</Note>

## Archiving an administration

Archive an administration when a client engagement ends but you want to retain the historical data for reference. Archived administrations no longer appear in your active list, but remain accessible through the archive view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open administration settings">
    Navigate to the administration and click **Settings → Administration details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the administration">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Archive administration**. Confirm the action in the dialogue that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access archived administrations">
    Return to the **Administrations** list and toggle the **Show archived** switch in the top-right corner to view archived records. Click any archived administration to browse its historical reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To restore an archived administration to active status, open it and click **Settings → Unarchive administration**.

## Deleting an administration

Deletion is permanent. All data — ledger imports, reports, meeting records, and audit entries — is removed and cannot be recovered.

<Warning>
  Only delete an administration if you are certain the data is no longer needed. Consider archiving instead to preserve a complete record of the engagement.
</Warning>

To delete an administration:

1. Go to **Settings → Administration details** inside the administration.
2. Click **Delete administration** at the bottom of the page.
3. Type the administration name exactly as shown to confirm, then click **Permanently delete**.

Deletion can only be performed by users with the **Admin** role.

## Searching and filtering administrations

Use the search and filter tools in the **Administrations** list to find specific client files quickly.

<Accordion title="Searching by name or reference">
  Type any part of the administration name or your client reference code into the search bar at the top of the **Administrations** list. Results update as you type. The search is case-insensitive.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Filtering by sector or company type">
  Click **Filter** to open the filter panel. Select one or more sectors or company types from the checkboxes. Apply the filter to narrow the list. Multiple filters combine with AND logic — the result must match all selected criteria.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sorting the list">
  Click any column header to sort the list by that column. Click again to reverse the sort order. The default sort is alphabetical by administration name.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Showing archived administrations">
  Toggle **Show archived** in the top-right of the list to include archived administrations in the view. Archived records are visually distinguished by a grey label.
</Accordion>
