Key users report bugs and ask for features from within aiGust. Only key users see the bug icon. Other users report problems to a key user at their firm.

1. Click the bug icon at the top right, and choose **Bug or feature request**.
2. Enter a title and describe the problem.
3. Add what helps us understand it:
   - the camera, to take a screenshot of the page. Draw on it to point out the problem, for example with a rectangle or an arrow;
   - the video camera, to record your screen;
   - the paperclip, to attach a file.
4. Click **Send**.

The window follows your language. In the screenshot it's in Dutch, so **Send** shows as **Verzenden**.

A good report tells us:

- where it happens: the administration and the page;
- what you did, what you expected and what you got;
- a screenshot, with the problem marked.

## After you send it

You get a confirmation email, and we get a ticket. We then pick it up. If we have questions, you get an email with a link to answer them. When we close the ticket, you get an update.

To follow up on what you reported, choose **Ticket overview** under the bug icon. See [Follow up your tickets](/setup/support/follow-up-tickets).
