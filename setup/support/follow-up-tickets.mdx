Key users can follow up on the bugs and feature requests they [reported](/setup/support/report-a-bug). You see the tickets of all key users at your firm, not only your own. Click the bug icon at the top right, and choose **Ticket overview**. The **Support tickets** tab of the **Workspace** opens. You can also click **Workspace** at the bottom left of the screen, and open the **Support tickets** tab.

Each ticket shows its type, such as **Idea**, its status, such as **New**, the title, when it was created and last updated, and who reported it.

Narrow the list with the filters:

- **Reporter:** the key user who reported the ticket, for example to see only your own.
- **All types:** the type of ticket: a bug, an idea or feedback. The type is set automatically when you send the report.
- **All open:** the status: **New**, **In progress** or **Resolved**. By default, you see the open tickets, the ones that aren't resolved yet.
- **Search:** find a ticket by its text.

**Feature requests** and **Roadmap** open the ideas and the roadmap of aiGust. See [Ideas and roadmap](/guide/getting-started/help#ideas-and-roadmap).

To see a ticket in full, with its screenshot and comments, click the three dots on it and choose **Open**. There you can also add a comment.

## What happens with a ticket

1. You send a report. You get a confirmation email.
2. We pick it up.
3. If we have questions, you get an email with a link. Follow the link to answer them.
4. When we close the ticket, you get an update by email.
