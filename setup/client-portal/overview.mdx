The client portal gives your clients access to their own figures in aiGust. They see their dashboard, their financial reports, how they compare with their peers, and the meetings you had with them. All in your firm's colours and with your logo.

## Why open it to your clients

- **Your advice keeps working after the meeting.** Your client looks back at the slides and your public notes whenever they want.
- **Your clients follow their own figures.** They don't have to ask you for the latest numbers, and they come to the next meeting prepared.
- **You stay in control.** You decide which tabs they see, how far the figures go, and which peer group they're compared with.

## Client users

The people who use the client portal are client users, also called external users. They're not part of your firm: usually the owner, a manager or the CFO of your client.

A client user:

- only sees the administrations you gave them access to;
- only sees what you share: published meetings, public notes and comments, and the tabs you turned on;
- can't change anything in the figures, except a few view settings.

With the role **External user (admin)**, a client user can also give access to others at their company. See [Client roles](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users#client-roles).

## Set up the client portal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what clients see">
    Turn the tabs of the portal on or off for your whole firm, and decide whether clients can manage users themselves. See [Control what clients see](/setup/client-portal/control-what-clients-see#for-your-whole-firm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand the portal">
    The portal uses your firm's colours and logo, and the welcome email your header and footer. See [Light mode and dark mode](/setup/branding/portal) and [Email templates](/setup/branding/email-templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your clients">
    Give client users access to their administrations, and send them the welcome email. See [Invite and manage client users](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune per administration">
    Where needed, set other tabs, a different up-to-date-until date or another peer group for one client. See [Control what clients see](/guide/client-portal/share).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your meetings">
    Publish a meeting after you've held it, so your client can look back at it. Check that only the notes meant for your client are public. See [Publish a meeting to your client](/guide/meetings/publish).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To check the result, open the administration and click **Client portal**. You then see the portal as your client does. See [What your clients see](/guide/client-portal/client-view#see-the-portal-as-your-client-does).
