Choose which tabs of the client portal your clients see by default, for your whole firm. Accountants can change this for one administration. See [Choose what your client sees](/guide/client-portal/share).

For what the tabs show, see [What your clients see](/guide/client-portal/client-view).

## For your whole firm

1. Click **Workspace** at the bottom left of the screen, and open the **Client portal** tab.
2. Under **Page visibility**, turn off **Use aiGust system defaults**.
3. Turn each tab on or off.1.

Some switches have a slightly different name than the tab in the portal:

| Switch              | Tab in the client portal |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Dashboard**       | **Dashboard**            |
| **Client meetings** | **Meetings**             |
| **P&L**             | **P&L**                  |
| **Balance**         | **Balance**              |
| **Cashflow**        | **Cashflow**             |
| **Clients**         | **Customers**            |
| **Suppliers**       | **Suppliers**            |
| **Users**           | **Users**                |

With **Use aiGust system defaults** on, your clients see all tabs.

### Should clients manage users?

With the **Users** tab, a client with the role **External user (admin)** can give others access to the client portal of their administration. Firms handle this in two ways:

- **Let one person at the client manage access.** Turn on **Users**, and give one person, such as the owner or the CFO, the role **External user (admin)**. They then give access to the others at their company.
- **Keep it with your firm.** Turn off **Users**. Only your firm then grants access. See [Invite and manage client users](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users).

## For one administration

Accountants can change the tabs for one administration, and choose how far the figures go and which peer group the client is compared with. See [Choose what your client sees](/guide/client-portal/share).
