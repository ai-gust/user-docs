---
title: "Invite and manage client users"
description: "Give client users access to the client portal, from an administration or for your whole firm, and manage their accounts."
---

A client user is someone outside your firm, usually at your client, who uses the [client portal](/guide/client-portal/client-view). A client user only sees the administrations you give them access to. By default, that's none.

You can give access in two ways:

- **From the list of all client users,** under **Users** > **Clients** in the firm menu, as described on this page. Use it to give someone access to several administrations or administration groups at once, and to manage all client users of your firm.
- **From one administration,** on its **Client access** tab. See [Give your client access](/guide/client-portal/give-access).

## Client roles

Each client user has a role:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **External user (read only)** | See the client portal of the administrations they have access to. |
| **External user (admin)** | The same, and give others access to those administrations on the **Users** tab of the client portal, if your firm turned that tab on. See [Should clients manage users?](/setup/client-portal/control-what-clients-see#should-clients-manage-users) |

## Add a client user for your firm

1. Click **Users** in the firm menu, and choose **Clients**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. **Client details:** enter the **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Language** and **Role**, and click **Next**.
4. **Link administration to client:** choose the **Access type**:
   - **Administration groups**, to give access to all administrations in one or more [administration groups](/setup/users/administration-groups);
   - **Administrations**, to pick single administrations.
5. **Link administrations:** select the groups or administrations, and click **Next**.
6. **Finish client set up:** choose whether to **Send a welcome email to the user**, and click **Submit**.

### The welcome email

The welcome email lets your client sign in, or set a password. It's the **Client welcome email** in your [email templates](/setup/branding/email-templates). See [Sign in to aiGust](/guide/getting-started/log-in).

If you don't send it, tell your client yourself how to sign in. You can send it later with **Resend welcome email**.

## Manage client users

**Users** > **Clients** lists all client users of your firm, with their email address, role and whether they're active. Filter the list by **Status** or **User Role**, search it, or click **Export** to download it.

Click the three dots next to a client user:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Opens the client user. See below. |
| **Activate/deactivate** | Blocks or allows their access to the client portal. A deactivated user can't sign in, and is greyed out in the list. |
| **Resend welcome email** | Sends the welcome email again, for example when the link expired. |
| **Delete** | For now, the same as deactivating: the client user stays in the list, but can't sign in. |

### Profile

The **Profile** tab holds the name, email address, **Language** and **User Role** of the client user. Change what you need, and click **Save**.

### Administration access

The **Administration access** tab lists the **Administrations** and **Administration groups** the client user can see. Click **+** to add one, or **−** to remove it. The client user only sees what's on this list: all other administrations are blocked.

### Audit log

The **Audit log** tab shows what the client user did, per day and per administration, for example **Viewed meeting**. Choose the period at the top right.
