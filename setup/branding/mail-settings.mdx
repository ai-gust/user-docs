aiGust sends emails on behalf of your firm, for example to welcome new users. Click **Branding** in the firm menu, and open the **Mail settings** tab.

Change what you need, and click **Save** at the top right. Only key users can change the mail settings. **Clear customizations** puts back aiGust's default settings.

## Sender

aiGust sends the emails from an aiGust account. You can't change that address, but you can set:

- **Display name:** the name your recipients see as the sender, such as your firm's name.
- **Reply-to address:** where replies go, such as your firm's general email address.

## Your branding

The preview shows how your emails look.

- **Header and footer:** click the pencil on the header or the footer to choose an image from your [branding assets](/setup/branding/branding-assets).
- **General:** the **Background** around the email, the **Content area color** behind the text, and the **Text color**.
- **Buttons:** the **Background** and the **Label** colour of the buttons, **Normal** and on **Hover**.

Recommended image sizes:

| Image | Width | Height |
|---|---|---|
| Header | 1096 px | 275 px |
| Footer | 1096 px | 10 to 30 px |

The text of each email is set in its template. See [Email templates](/setup/branding/email-templates).
