---
title: "Audit trail"
description: "See what a user at your firm or a client user did in aiGust, per day and per administration."
---

aiGust keeps an audit log of what each user does: which administrations they opened, and what they created or changed there. Key users can read it for every user at your firm and every client user.

aiGust keeps the audit log for at least 12 months.

## Open the audit log of a user

- **A user at your firm:** click **Users** in the firm menu, choose **Employees**, and open the user. Open the **Audit log** tab.
- **A client user:** click **Users** in the firm menu, choose **Clients**, and open the user. Open the **Audit log** tab. See [Audit log](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users#audit-log).
- **From the usage statistics:** click the history icon next to a user in any list. See [Usage statistics](/setup/monitoring/usage-statistics).

Choose the period at the top right, for example **Last 30 days**.

## What the audit log shows

The log is grouped per day, newest first. Within a day, you see:

- **Where** it happened: an administration, or your firm itself for firm-wide settings;
- **What** the user did, such as **Created meeting** or **Mapped GL account**. Similar actions close together are grouped, with a counter that shows how often, such as **×13**. Saving a meeting, for example, happens automatically while you edit it, so it quickly adds up;
- **When**, as the time of day.

aiGust records every action and translates it into a readable description. The table below lists the ones you'll see most.

| Action | What the user did |
|---|---|
| **Viewed meeting** | Opened a meeting. Mostly seen for client users. |
| **Created meeting**, **Updated meeting** | Created or changed a meeting. |
| **Created comment thread** | Added a comment. See [comments](/guide/reports/table/cell-details). |
| **Updated administration** | Changed the settings of an administration. |
| **Mapped GL account** | Changed the [label mapping](/guide/concepts/label-mapping) of an account. |
| **Analyzed result sheet** | Opened the profit and loss. |
| **Updated tenant meeting flow** | Changed one of your firm's [meeting flows](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows). |
| **Updated tenant user** | Changed a user at your firm. |
