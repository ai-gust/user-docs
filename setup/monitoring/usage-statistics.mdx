title: "Usage statistics"
description: "Follow how the users at your firm and your client users adopt aiGust: who signs in, who really uses it, and how many meetings and forecasts you create."
---

See how aiGust is adopted at your firm and by your clients. Click **Usage aiGust** in the firm menu. **Adoption & usage** opens, with three sections: **Users**, **Meetings** and **Forecasts**.

<Note>
Only key users see **Usage aiGust**.
</Note>

Click a card to open its detail page, with a chart and, for most of them, a list you can search and download with **Export Excel**. Click **Back** to return.

## Choose the period

Every card and detail page shows the **Last 30 days** by default. On a detail page:

- click the period at the top right, and choose **Last 7 days** up to **Last 365 days**, or pick a start and end date in the calendar;
- move to the previous or next period with the arrows.

<Frame caption="Choose the period.">
  <img src="/images/monitoring/period-picker.jpg" alt="Period picker opened, with the options Last 7 days, Last 14 days, Last 30 days, Last 60 days, Last 90 days, Last 180 days and Last 365 days on the left, and a calendar for September and October 2026 with 14 to 20 September selected." />
</Frame>

The charts show one bar per week. Click **Weekly** to choose **Daily**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Complete period** instead.

## Users

Use the switch at the top right of **Users** to see the users at your firm (**Tenant**) or your [client users](/setup/client-portal/overview#client-users) (**Client**). The reports are the same. Usage is usually higher at your firm than at your clients.

<Frame caption="The users at your firm.">
  <img src="/images/monitoring/users-tenant.jpg" alt="Users section set to Tenant, with the figures blurred: Unique user logins in the last 30 days with a weekly bar chart, and User activity with active and inactive users and a ring chart." />
</Frame>

### Unique user logins

How many different users signed in during the period, out of all users. A user who signs in ten times counts once.

Click the card to see the evolution. Click a bar in the chart to see who signed in during that period, with their **Most recent login**.

### User activity

How many users were active during the period. Signing in isn't enough:

- **A user at your firm** is active after doing at least one thing, such as creating a meeting.
- **A client user** is active after viewing a meeting.

Click the card to see the evolution, and the list of active users with their most recent activity.

## Meetings

### Meetings created

How many meetings your firm created in the period, whether or not you published them. The list shows each meeting's **Title**, **Administration**, **VAT Number**, when it was **Created** and who created it.

<Frame caption="The meetings your firm created.">

### Administrations that received a meeting

How many of your administrations got a meeting in the period, out of all administrations. The list shows each administration, when its meeting was created and last updated, and the **Meeting Author**.

Use it to see how widely your firm advises its clients: a meeting for most administrations, or many meetings for only a few.

</Frame>

## Forecasts

### Forecasts created

How many forecasts your firm created in the period. The list shows each forecast's **Title**, **Administration**, when it was **Created** and who created it. See [Forecasts](/guide/forecasts/how-forecasts-work).

<Frame caption="The forecasts your firm created.">
  <img src="/images/monitoring/forecasts-created.jpg" alt="Forecasts created page for the last 30 days, with the total blurred and a weekly bar chart from W36 to W40. Below, a Results table with Search and Export Excel, and the columns Title, Administration, Created and Created by. Titles include forecast 30/09/2026, VA 1, VA 3 and VA 10/10/2026 - Aj 2027. Administrations and names are blurred." />
</Frame>

Client users can't open or create forecasts in the client portal. They only see the forecast you activated for the administration, in their charts and reports. The best way to share a forecast with your client is to add it to a meeting.
