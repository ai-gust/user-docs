description: "The difference between an office key user and a standard office user, and what each role can do in aiGust."
---

Each user at your firm has a role. The role decides what the user can do in aiGust. You choose it when you [add the user](/setup/users/manage-users#add-a-user), and change it on the **Profile** of the user.

| Role | In the bulk upload | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Office key user** | `tenant:key_user` | The people who set up and manage aiGust for your firm. Usually one or a few. |
| **Standard office user** | `tenant:full` | Everyone else at your firm: the accountants and advisors who work with the administrations of your clients. |

The list also shows **Tenant service user**. Don't use it: it's not meant for the people at your firm.

## What both roles can do

Both roles work with all features for the administrations they have access to: the dashboard, reports, forecasts, meetings, the client portal, and so on. In the firm menu, both see **Administrations**, **Triggers & alerts**, **Data sources** > **Excel imports** and **Users** > **Clients**. See [Find your way around](/guide/getting-started/navigation#the-firm-menu).

The role doesn't decide which administrations a user sees. That's the administration access of the user. See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access).

## What only key users can do

Key users also manage aiGust for the whole firm:

| Key users can | Where |
|---|---|
| Add and manage the users at your firm | **Users** > **Employees**. See [Add and manage users](/setup/users/manage-users). |
| Link your bookkeeping and social software, and choose which administrations aiGust imports | **Data sources** > **Connectors** and **Black- & Whitelist**. See [Data sources](/setup/data/overview). |
| Set the colours, logos and emails of your firm | **Branding**. See [Light mode and dark mode](/setup/branding/portal) and [Email templates](/setup/branding/email-templates). |
| Manage the KPI dashboards | **Dashboards**. See [KPI dashboard templates](/configure/reporting/kpi-dashboard-templates). |
| Manage the meeting flows | **Meeting flows**. See [Meeting flows](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows). |
| Add triggers | **Triggers & alerts**. See [Custom triggers](/configure/triggers/custom-triggers). |
| Follow how your firm uses aiGust | **Usage aiGust**. See [Usage statistics](/setup/monitoring/usage-statistics). |
| Change firm-wide settings, such as the client portal | **Workspace**. See [Control what clients see](/setup/client-portal/control-what-clients-see). |
| Report a bug or ask for a feature | The bug icon in the top bar. See [Report a bug](/setup/support/report-a-bug). |

## Client roles

The people at your clients don't get one of these roles. They're client users, with the role **External user (read only)** or **External user (admin)**. See [Invite and manage client users](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users#client-roles).
