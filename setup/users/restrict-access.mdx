By default, a user at your firm sees all administrations of your firm. You can limit that per user, for example for a student who only works on a few clients, or to keep the administrations of one office apart.

A user with restricted access only sees the administrations they're allowed to see, everywhere in aiGust: in the list of administrations, in the alerts, and in search.

## Allowed and blocked

Each user has two lists:

| List | Means |
|---|---|
| **Allowed** | The user sees only these administrations. All others are blocked. |
| **Blocked** | The user sees all administrations, except these. |

You can use both together. aiGust first takes the allowed administrations, then removes the blocked ones. For example: 10 administrations are allowed, and 2 of them are also blocked. The user then sees 8 administrations.

An empty **Allowed** list means no limit: the user sees all administrations, except the blocked ones.

In both lists, you can add single **Administrations** or whole **Administration groups**. A group is easier to keep up to date: when you add an administration to the group, every user with that group gets it too. See [Administration groups](/setup/users/administration-groups).

## Restrict a user's access

You restrict access on a user that already exists. Add the user first, then:

1. Click **Users** in the firm menu, and choose **Employees**.
2. Click the three dots next to the user, and choose **Edit**.
3. Open the **Administration access** tab.
4. Choose **Allowed** or **Blocked**.
5. Click **+** next to **Administrations** or **Administration groups**.
6. In **Customize access**, choose the **Access type**: **Administration groups** or **Administrations**. Click **Next**.
7. Select the groups or administrations. Click **Next**.

To remove one from the list, click **−** next to it.

On **Users** > **Employees**, the **Restricted Access** column shows **Restricted** for users with a limit.

<Note>
You can't restrict access when you [add users in bulk](/setup/users/manage-users#add-users-in-bulk). Set it for each user, one by one, after the upload.
</Note>

## Not the same as the Black- & Whitelist

Under **Data sources**, the firm menu also has a **Black- & Whitelist**. That one decides which administrations aiGust imports from your connectors, for the whole firm. The access of a user decides which of the imported administrations that user sees. See [Include or exclude administrations](/setup/data/bookkeeping/include-exclude-administrations).

## Client users

Client users work the other way around: by default, they see no administrations at all. You give them access to the ones they need. See [Invite and manage client users](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users).
