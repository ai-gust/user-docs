Everyone at your firm who works in aiGust needs a user. Only key users can add and manage users. Click **Users** in the firm menu, and choose **Employees**.

This page is about the users at your firm. For the people at your clients, see [Invite and manage client users](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users).

## Add a user

1. Click **Users** in the firm menu, and choose **Employees**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. **User details:** fill in the fields below, and click **Next**.
4. **Finish user set up:** choose whether to **Send a welcome email to the user**, and click **Submit**.

| Field | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| **First name** and **Last name** | The name of your colleague. |
| **Email** | Their work email address. If they sign in with Google or Microsoft, this must be exactly the email address of their Google or Microsoft account, including the domain. Otherwise, they can't sign in. |
| **Language** | The language of aiGust and of the welcome email. It's set to your own language. Change it if your colleague works in another language. |
| **Role** | **Office key user** or **Standard office user**. There's no default: you always choose. See [Roles and permissions](/setup/users/roles). |

The new user has access to all administrations of your firm. To limit that, open the user after you added them. See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access).

### The welcome email

**Send a welcome email to the user** is turned on by default. The email lets your colleague sign in with Google or Microsoft, or set a password. See [Sign in to aiGust](/guide/getting-started/log-in).

Before you add your colleagues, check the text of the **Welcome email** in your [email templates](/setup/branding/email-templates). That's what they'll read first.

If you don't send it, tell your colleague yourself how to sign in. You can send it later with **Resend welcome email**.

## Add users in bulk

To add many users at once, upload them from a CSV or Excel file.

1. Click **Users** in the firm menu, and choose **Employees**.
2. Click **Add in bulk**.
3. Click **Download template** to get an Excel file with the right columns. Fill in one row per user.
4. Drag your file to the dialog, or click **Select a file to upload**. The file can be up to 50 MB. Click **Next**.
5. **Outcome of the bulk job** shows how many users aiGust added. Click the download icon to get the result as an Excel file, then click **Submit**.

The file needs these columns:

| Column | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| `first_name` | The first name. |
| `last_name` | The last name. |
| `email` | The email address. The same rules apply as when you [add one user](#add-a-user). |
| `role` | `tenant:key_user` for an **Office key user**, or `tenant:full` for a **Standard office user**. |
| `locale` | The language: `nl`, `fr` or `en`. |

The dialog also lists `tenant:service_user` as a role. Don't use it.

## Manage users

**Users** > **Employees** lists all users at your firm, with their email address, role, administration access, whether they're beta testers, and whether they're active. Deactivated users are greyed out.

Search the list, or filter it by **Status**, **Bèta testers** or **User Role**. Click **Export** to download it.

The **Restricted Access** column shows **Restricted** for users who only see part of the administrations, and **Full access** for users who see all of them.

Click the three dots next to a user:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Opens the user. See below. |
| **Activate/deactivate** | Blocks or allows their access to aiGust. A deactivated user can't sign in, and is greyed out in the list. |
| **Resend welcome email** | Sends the welcome email again, for example when the link expired. |
| **Delete** | Removes the user. |

### Profile

The **Profile** tab holds the **Personal details**, **Bèta tester** and **Role** of the user. Change what you need, and click **Save**.

- **Personal details:** **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** and **Language**.
- **Bèta tester:** turn on **Is a bèta tester** to let the user try new features before they're released to everyone.
- **Role:** the **User Role**. See [Roles and permissions](/setup/users/roles).

### Administration access

The **Administration access** tab sets which administrations the user can see. See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access).

### Audit log

The **Audit log** tab shows what the user did, per day and per administration. Choose the period at the top right. For the actions of all users together, see [Audit trail](/setup/monitoring/audit-trail).
