---
title: "Administration groups"
description: "Combine administrations into groups, such as per office or for sensitive files, to manage who sees them more easily."
---

An administration group is a set of administrations of your firm that belong together, for example the clients of one office.

For now, groups make it easier to manage access. Instead of adding administrations to each user one by one, you add a group:

- **Per office:** make a group per office, and allow the users of each office to see only their group.
- **For sensitive files:** put the administrations few people may see in one group, and block that group for everyone else. When a new sensitive file comes in, you add it to the group, and it's blocked right away for all those users.

See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access). You can also give a [client user](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users) access to a whole group.

An administration can be in more than one group.

Only key users can create and change groups. Other users can see them.

## The list of groups

Click **Administrations** in the firm menu, and choose **Groups**. Each group shows its name, its description, and how many administrations it holds.

## Create a group

1. Click **Administrations** in the firm menu, and choose **Groups**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Fill in the **Name**, and optionally a **Description**. Click **Confirm**.

In **Select administrations**, type the name of an administration, and select it. It's added to the list above. Repeat for the next one, and click **Confirm** when you have them all.

### In bulk, by VAT number

Use this when you have a list of your clients, for example from your practice management software.

1. In **VAT-numbers**, paste the VAT numbers, one per line. aiGust shows the numbers it doesn't recognise as a VAT number under **Invalid VAT codes**. Correct or remove them.
2. Click **Next**. aiGust shows the administrations it found. If some VAT numbers don't match an administration in aiGust, it says so.
3. Click **Next** to add the administrations to the group.

## Remove an administration from a group

Click the three dots next to the administration, and choose **Delete from group**. This only removes it from the group, not from aiGust.

The same menu has the options of the [list of administrations](/guide/getting-started/navigation#more-options), and **Copy administration ID**.

## Rename or delete a group

Click the three dots of a group, on the list of groups or at the top right of the group:

- **Edit**, to open the group and add or remove administrations, the same as clicking the group;
- **Rename**, to change its name;
- **Used by**, to see which users have the group on their **Allowed** or **Blocked** list;
- **Delete**, to delete the group. The administrations in it stay in aiGust.

### Delete a group that's still in use

When users still have the group on their **Allowed** or **Blocked** list, aiGust warns you before you delete it. Deleting the group removes it from the access lists of all those users. Their lists themselves stay, so their access stays restricted:

- **On an Allowed list,** the user loses access to the administrations of the group, unless they're allowed in another way. If the group was the only thing on the list, the user no longer sees any administration.
- **On a Blocked list,** the administrations of the group are no longer blocked, so the user sees them again.

Client users only have an allowed list. A [client user](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users) with access to the group loses access to its administrations in the client portal.

See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access).

To delete it anyway, turn on **Delete anyway**, and click **Confirm**. To check first who's affected, choose **Used by**.
