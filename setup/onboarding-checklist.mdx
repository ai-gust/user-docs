---
title: "Onboarding checklist"
description: "Set up aiGust for your firm in the right order: connectors, branding, emails, users, report settings and meeting flows."
---

Follow these steps to get your firm up and running. Start with the connectors: aiGust needs data, and the first sync takes about a day. Use that day for the other steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in for the first time">
    Usually, we do the first sign-in together with you, to arrange the approvals, such as the consent for Microsoft. See [First sign-in as administrator](/setup/first-login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your data">
    Add a connector for each bookkeeping or compliance package your firm uses, and for your social secretariat. See [Data sources overview](/setup/data/overview).

    Want to keep some administrations out of aiGust? Set up the [Black & whitelist](/setup/data/bookkeeping/include-exclude-administrations) before the first sync.

    The first administrations and figures appear about 24 hours later. Check that the connectors work on their **Health** tab. See [Check connector status](/setup/data/connector-status).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your branding">
    Add your logo and choose your colours, so aiGust looks like your firm. See [Light mode and dark mode](/setup/branding/portal) and [Branding assets](/setup/branding/branding-assets).

    You need a logo, preferably without a background, and a header for the emails. Not a designer? Just ask us: we usually help with this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the emails">
    Brand the emails aiGust sends with your header and colours. See [Mail settings](/setup/branding/mail-settings).

    Then adjust their content. aiGust comes with a standard text, but you might want to add something about your firm, or a personal message. Check at least the **Welcome email**: your colleagues get it in the next step. See [Email templates](/setup/branding/email-templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your colleagues">
    Add the users of your firm, and choose their role. Give a few colleagues the **Office key user** role, so you're not the only one. See [Add and manage users](/setup/users/manage-users) and [Roles and permissions](/setup/users/roles).

    Should some colleagues only see part of the administrations? Restrict their access. See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access) and [Administration groups](/setup/users/administration-groups).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the report settings">
    Choose how the financial reports look for everyone at your firm: for example three, four or five years next to each other, and the most recent year on the left or on the right. It takes about a minute. Each accountant can still change them for themselves, but it's best to set them to your firm's standard. See [Firm-wide report view settings](/configure/reporting/report-view-defaults).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look around">
    Once the data is in, open a few administrations and get familiar with aiGust. See [Find your way around](/guide/getting-started/navigation) and [First steps in a new administration](/guide/getting-started/first-steps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the meeting flows">
    Adjust the meeting flows to the way you want your accountants to run client meetings. See [Meeting flows](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows) and [Meeting types](/configure/meetings/meeting-types).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When you're ready

These steps can wait until your firm works with aiGust:

- **Triggers:** switch off the built-in triggers your firm doesn't need, or create your own. See [Manage built-in triggers](/configure/triggers/built-in-triggers) and [Create custom triggers](/configure/triggers/custom-triggers).
- **KPI dashboards:** create dashboard templates per sector. See [Firm-wide KPI dashboard templates](/configure/reporting/kpi-dashboard-templates).
- **Client portal:** give your clients access to their own figures. See [Client portal overview](/setup/client-portal/overview).
