---
title: "Social data overview"
description: "Where aiGust gets the social balance sheets of your clients, for personnel reporting."
---

aiGust's personnel KPIs come from the social balance sheet of an administration: the number of employees in FTE, the hours worked, and so on. See [Personnel data sources](/guide/personnel/data-sources).

aiGust gets social balance sheets from two sources:

| Source | What you set up | How up to date |
|---|---|---|
| **Silverfin** | Nothing extra: the [Silverfin connector](/setup/data/bookkeeping/silverfin) also brings the social balance sheets. See [Silverfin social data](/setup/data/social/silverfin). | Once a year, once it's entered in Silverfin. |
| **Partena** | A Partena connector. See [Partena](/setup/data/social/partena). | Every month. |

If an administration has data from both, aiGust uses Partena, because it's the most detailed.

We're working on a third source: the social balance sheets from the National Bank of Belgium.

aiGust links the social balance sheet to the administration by its VAT number.

For all codes of the social balance sheet that aiGust imports, and the KPIs they become, see [Social balance codes](/setup/data/social/social-balance-codes).
