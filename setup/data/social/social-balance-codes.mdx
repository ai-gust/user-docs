This page lists the 162 codes of the social balance sheet that aiGust imports, with the name of the KPI each one becomes in aiGust. It's for reference only: you don't need it to set up a social connector. For where the social balance sheet comes from, see [Social data overview](/setup/data/social/overview).

Not every social balance sheet has all codes. aiGust looks for each of them, and imports the ones it finds.

The codes are those of the National Bank of Belgium. The descriptions are in Dutch, as on the social balance sheet. **Value** says how aiGust calculates a code over a period:

- **Average:** the average over the period, such as the average number of employees.
- **Total:** the total over the period, such as the hours worked.
- **End:** the value at the end of the period, such as the number of employees on the last day.

To find a code quickly, open a category and use your browser's search (Cmd+F or Ctrl+F).
