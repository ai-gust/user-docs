---
title: "Horus"
description: "Connect Horus to aiGust: add the connector, and give aiGust access in Horus."
---

You connect Horus by signing in to Horus and giving aiGust access. You don't need to fill in anything else.

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Horus**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and click **Create connector**.
4. Click **Activate now**. Sign in to Horus, and give aiGust access.

<Frame caption="Horus only needs a name.">
  <img src="/images/data/horus-config.jpg" alt="Enter configuration details dialog for a Horus connector, with the Name Horus connector (2) and the buttons Back and Create connector." />
</Frame>

You come back to aiGust, and get a notification whether the activation succeeded. See [Activate the connector](/setup/data/overview#activate-the-connector).

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported).
