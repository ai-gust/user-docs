---
title: "Include or exclude administrations"
description: "Choose which administrations aiGust syncs from your connectors, with a whitelist or a blacklist."
---

By default, aiGust syncs all active administrations it finds in your connectors. To sync only some of them, or to skip some, use the **Black & whitelist**. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, and choose **Black- & Whitelist**.

| List | Means |
|---|---|
| **Whitelist** | aiGust syncs only these administrations, from all connectors. |
| **Blacklist** | aiGust syncs all administrations, except these. |

The lists apply to all connectors of your firm.

## Use a whitelist

Use a whitelist when aiGust should only show part of your clients, for example during a pilot with a few administrations.

1. Under **Whitelist**, click **Add**.
2. Add the administrations by their VAT number. That's the only way to add them.
3. Check that the whitelist is **Enabled**.

With the switch **Enabled**, you turn the whitelist on or off, without emptying it.

## Use a blacklist

Use a blacklist to keep a few administrations out of aiGust, for example administrations you no longer work for, or that you don't want in aiGust at all.

1. Under **Blacklist**, click **Add**.
2. Add the administrations by their VAT number.

To remove an administration from a list, click the bin icon next to it.

## What happens to administrations already in aiGust

When you blacklist an administration that's already in aiGust, or leave it off the whitelist:

- It disappears right away from the list of administrations and from search.
- Its figures are deleted after the next nightly sync.

What your firm made in aiGust, such as meetings and comments, is kept. See [When an administration becomes inactive](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported#when-an-administration-becomes-inactive).

## Not the same as restricting access

The **Black & whitelist** decides which administrations aiGust syncs, for your whole firm. To decide which of those administrations one user sees, restrict the access of that user. See [Restrict administration access](/setup/users/restrict-access).
