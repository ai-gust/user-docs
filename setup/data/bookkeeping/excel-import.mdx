---
title: "Excel import"
description: "Add an administration from Excel files when aiGust can't connect to its bookkeeping: create it, define its financial years, and upload the starting balances and transactions."
---

When aiGust has no connector for an administration's bookkeeping, you can import it from Excel files. You create the administration in aiGust, and upload its data step by step.

An Excel import always creates a new administration, from scratch. You can't add historic data from Excel to an administration that comes from a connector: aiGust can't combine financial years from two sources.

<Note>
Check your files carefully before you upload them. Today, you can't correct or delete data once it's imported.
</Note>

## The steps

1. [Create the administration.](#step-1-create-the-administration)
2. [Define the financial years and periods.](#step-2-define-the-financial-years)
3. [Upload the starting balances.](#step-3-upload-the-starting-balances)
4. [Upload the transactions.](#step-4-upload-the-transactions)

Steps 2 to 4 are one wizard: **Financial periods and starting balances**.

Each upload step has a template. Download it, and copy your data into the right columns. Your bookkeeping export probably won't match the template right away, so expect some work in Excel to convert it.

## Step 1: Create the administration

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, and choose **Excel imports**.
2. Click **Add new administration**.
3. **Basic information:** fill in the **Name**, the **VAT Number** and the **Legal type**. Click **Next**.
4. **About the company:** search and select the **Economic activity**, and choose the **Company type**. See [Sectors and company types](/guide/concepts/sectors-and-company-types). Click **Next**.
5. **Address:** fill in the address of the company. Click **Next**.
6. **Is this correct?:** check the details, and click **Submit**.

## Step 2: Define the financial years

Open the administration. It doesn't have any figures yet: you land on **Import data**, which shows what still needs to be done.

<Frame caption="Import data: the steps to fill the administration.">
  <img src="/images/data/excel-import-data.jpg" alt="Import data page of the administration FC De Kampioenen, explaining that you import data from Excel files in several steps. Three sections: Administration added, Financial periods and starting balances, open, with a button Financial periods and starting balances, and Transaction import." />
</Frame>

1. Click **Financial periods and starting balances**.
2. Click **Add period** for each financial year you'll upload.
3. For each financial year, fill in:
   - **Period ID:** the ID of the financial year. aiGust proposes the year, such as *2025*. Use the ID your bookkeeping uses for that financial year, because your transactions will refer to it.
   - **Period start date** and **Period end date.**
   - **Booking periods:** how the financial year is split into periods: months, quarters or the whole year.
4. Click **Next**.

The financial years must follow each other, without gaps.

## Step 3: Upload the starting balances

aiGust needs the balance of each GL account at the start of the first financial year you upload. These are mainly the balance sheet accounts: at the start of a financial year, the P&L accounts are zero.

For example: if you start with financial year 2023, give the balances on the first day of financial year 2023.

1. Click **Download beginning balances template file**, and fill it in. The file needs these columns:

   | Column | What to fill in |
   |---|---|
   | `first_day` | The first day of the first financial year you upload, for example `01/01/2024`. |
   | `glaccount_code` | The number of the GL account, for example `400000`. |
   | `glaccount_description` | The name of the GL account, for example *Customers*. |
   | `amount_eur` | The balance in euro: debit positive, credit negative. |

2. Drag the file to the dialog, or click **Select a file to upload**. Upload a clean file, with only these columns and your data.
3. Link each column to the columns of your file, and choose the date format and number format your file uses.
4. Click **Next**.

Every date must match the date format. Otherwise, you can't continue. Check the amounts too: aiGust leaves an amount it can't read empty.

## Step 4: Upload the transactions

The last step of the wizard is the transactions. Upload all transactions of the financial years you defined. Download the template to see the format.

You can give the amount in one column, or split it into debit and credit:

| Column | One amount | Debit and credit |
|---|---|---|
| `date` | The date of the transaction. | The same. |
| `glaccount_code` | The number of the GL account. | The same. |
| `glaccount_description` | The name of the GL account. | The same. |
| `amount_eur` | The amount: debit positive, credit negative. So revenue is negative, and costs are positive. | — |
| `amount_debit` and `amount_credit` | — | The debit and the credit amount. aiGust calculates the amount as debit minus credit. |
| `business_partner` | Optional. The customer or supplier. | The same. |
| `transaction_description` | Optional. The description of the transaction. | The same. |

As with the starting balances, link the columns to your file, and choose the date and number format. Every date must match the date format. An amount aiGust can't read is imported as 0, so check your amounts before you upload.

aiGust places each transaction in the right financial year and period by its date.

## Add data later

To add a new financial year later:

1. Define the new financial year, as in [step 2](#step-2-define-the-financial-years).
2. Upload its transactions, as in [step 4](#step-4-upload-the-transactions). You don't need starting balances again.

Upload all transactions of the period you add. If you upload in the middle of a financial year, make sure you don't miss any transactions.

### Transactions you already uploaded

aiGust only imports the transactions it doesn't have yet. So you can upload a file that partly overlaps with a previous upload.

aiGust recognises a transaction by all its columns, except the amount. If you upload a transaction again with a corrected amount, aiGust sees it as the same transaction, and skips it. You can't correct an amount this way.
