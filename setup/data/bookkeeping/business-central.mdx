---
title: "Business Central"
description: "Connect Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central to aiGust with the details of an app registration in Microsoft."
---

You connect Business Central with an app registration in your Microsoft organisation. aiGust signs in as that app, without a user, and only reads data. You set up its access in two places: in Microsoft Entra, then in each Business Central environment. You need an administrator of your Microsoft organisation and of Business Central for this.

aiGust reads the company information, customers, vendors, GL accounts, accounting periods and GL entries of every company in the environment.

## Step 1: Register aiGust in Microsoft Entra

1. Sign in to the [Azure portal](https://portal.azure.com), and open **Microsoft Entra ID**.
2. On **Overview**, copy the **Tenant ID**.
3. Go to **App registrations**, and click **New registration**. Give it a name, for example *aiGust*, and register it.
4. On the page of the new app, copy the **Application (client) ID**.
5. Under **Certificates & secrets**, create a new client secret, and copy its value right away. Microsoft only shows it once.
6. Under **API permissions**, add a permission: **Dynamics 365 Business Central** › **Application permissions** › **API.ReadWrite.All**. Then click **Grant admin consent**.

Business Central has no read-only permission for apps. That's not a risk: what aiGust can do is limited by the permission set you assign in Business Central, in step 2.

## Step 2: Give the app access in Business Central

Do this in each Business Central environment you want to connect:

1. Open the **Microsoft Entra Applications** page.
2. Add the **Client ID** of the app, set its **State** to **Enabled**, and grant consent.
3. Assign a permission set:
   - **D365 READ**: read access to everything. The simplest option, and the one we recommend.
   - Or a read-only permission set of your own, if you want to give as little access as possible. It needs read access to Company Information, Customer, Vendor, G/L Account, Accounting Period and G/L Entry, and to Cust. Ledger Entry, Vendor Ledger Entry and their detailed entries. Without those, the sync fails.
4. Leave the **Company** field of the permission set empty, so it applies to all companies. aiGust syncs every company in the environment, and a company without permissions gives an error.

The app doesn't need a Business Central user licence, and it doesn't need the SUPER permission set.

Microsoft explains these steps in [Using service-to-service authentication](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/administration/automation-apis-using-s2s-authentication).

## Step 3: Add the connector in aiGust

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and fill in the fields below.
4. Click **Create connector**.

| Field | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| **Microsoft Tenant ID** | The ID of your Microsoft organisation. |
| **Environment** | The name of your Business Central environment, for example *Production*. |
| **Client ID** | The **Application (client) ID** of the app registration. |
| **Client Secret** | The value of the client secret you created. |
| **Scope** | Leave it as it is: `https://api.businesscentral.dynamics.com/.default`. |
