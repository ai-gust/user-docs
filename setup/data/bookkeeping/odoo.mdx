title: "Odoo"
description: "Connect Odoo to aiGust, in the Odoo cloud or on your own server, and choose which databases to sync."
---

In Odoo, each company has its own database. With the Odoo connector, you tell aiGust where your Odoo runs, how to sign in, and which databases to sync.

## Add the connector

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Odoo**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and fill in the fields below.
4. Click **Create connector**.

| Field | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| **Host (URL without http)** | Where your Odoo runs. For the Odoo cloud, that's `odoo.com`, or something similar. If you host Odoo yourself, fill in the address or IP address of your server. |
| **Port** | Usually `443`. |
| **Username** | The Odoo user aiGust signs in with. |
| **Password or API key** | The password or an API key of that user. |
| **Databases** | The names of the databases to sync, one per company. Click **+** to add another one. |
| **Detect databases** | Turn it on to let aiGust find the databases itself, instead of typing them. This only works when you host Odoo yourself, and your server allows it. |

On the Odoo cloud, odoo.com, **Detect databases** doesn't work: Odoo doesn't allow it there. Type the names of the databases instead.

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported).
