---
title: "Octopus"
description: "Connect Octopus to aiGust: add the connector, and give aiGust access in Octopus."
---

You connect Octopus by signing in to Octopus and giving aiGust access. You don't need to fill in anything else.

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Octopus**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and click **Create connector**.
4. Click **Activate now**. Sign in to Octopus, and give aiGust access.

<Frame caption="Octopus only needs a name.">
  <img src="/images/data/octopus-config.jpg" alt="Enter configuration details dialog for an Octopus connector, with the Name Octopus connector and the buttons Back and Create connector." />
</Frame>

You come back to aiGust, and get a notification whether the activation succeeded. See [Activate the connector](/setup/data/overview#activate-the-connector).

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported).
