Wolters Kluwer Expert/M is a bookkeeping connector that works with the [agent](/setup/data/bookkeeping/on-premise-agent): aiGust gets the figures with the transactions, business partners and documents behind them. Every night, aiGust syncs only what changed in the Expert/M database since the last sync.

## Add the connector

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Wolters Kluwer Expert/M**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and click **Create connector**.

4. **Download & install the agent:** click **Download agent**, and install it on a server with access to the Expert/M database. See [Install the on-premise agent](/setup/data/bookkeeping/on-premise-agent).

There's no activation step: the connector works once the agent runs.

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported).
