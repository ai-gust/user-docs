Wolters Kluwer Adsolut is a bookkeeping connector that works with the [agent](/setup/data/bookkeeping/on-premise-agent): aiGust gets the figures with the transactions, business partners and documents behind them. Every night, aiGust syncs only what changed in Adsolut since the last sync.

## Add the connector

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Wolters Kluwer Adsolut**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and click **Create connector**.

<Frame caption="Adsolut only needs a name.">
  <img src="/images/data/adsolut-config.jpg" alt="Enter configuration details dialog for an Adsolut connector, with the Name Adsolut connector (3) and the buttons Back and Create connector." />
</Frame>

4. **Download & install the agent:** click **Download agent**, and install it on a server with access to the Adsolut database. See [Install the on-premise agent](/setup/data/bookkeeping/on-premise-agent).

There's no activation step: the connector works once the agent runs.

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported).

## Fiscal files

Adsolut also has fiscal files (**fiscaal dossier**): the module where your firm closes the financial year and prepares the corporate tax return. The balances of a financial year are exported from the bookkeeping to the fiscal file.

You don't need a separate connector for them: the Adsolut connector brings the fiscal files too. aiGust treats them as a compliance source, with balances only. See [Bookkeeping and compliance connectors](/guide/dashboard/data-sync#bookkeeping-and-compliance-connectors).

- **An administration in the bookkeeping and in the fiscal files:** that's the case for every administration you keep the books of. aiGust uses the bookkeeping.
- **An administration only in the fiscal files:** aiGust uses the fiscal file. You get its balances, without transactions or business partners.
