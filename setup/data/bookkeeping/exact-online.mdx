title: "Exact Online"
description: "Connect Exact Online to aiGust: register aiGust as an app in the Exact Online App Center, and activate the connector."
---

aiGust isn't listed in the Exact Online App Store yet. So you first register aiGust as an app of your own in the Exact App Center. That gives you a client ID and a client secret, which you then enter in aiGust.

You need a login for the Exact Online App Store.

## Step 1: Register aiGust in Exact Online

1. Go to [www.exact.com/en/login](https://www.exact.com/en/login).
2. In the **Exact Online App Store** box, click **Login**. This is the App Store, not the Exact Online you use for the bookkeeping.
3. Once signed in, go to **Manage my apps** (in Dutch: **Mijn apps beheren**). You land on a red page with your apps.
4. Click **Register a production app** (**Een app voor productie registreren**).
5. Fill in the name of the app (**Naam van de app**), for example *aiGust*, and the **Redirect URI**: `https://app.ai-gust.io/callback/connectors/oauth`. aiGust also shows this address when you add the connector.
6. Accept the terms, and click **Register** (**Registreren**).
7. On the page of your new app, copy the **Client ID** and the **Client Secret**.

## Step 2: Add the connector in aiGust

1. Click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
2. Choose **Exact Online**, and click **Next**.
3. Give the connector a **Name**, and paste the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Click **Create connector**.

## Step 3: Activate the connector

1. Click **Activate now**.
2. You go to Exact Online. Sign in, if it asks you to.
3. Approve the connection, so that aiGust can read your data.

You come back to aiGust, and get a notification whether the activation succeeded. See [Activate the connector](/setup/data/overview#activate-the-connector).

### Which administrations aiGust gets

aiGust gets exactly the administrations that the Exact Online user who activates the connector has access to. Use a shared account of your firm, not the personal account of a colleague.

That matters for the two ways firms usually work with Exact Online:

- **Your firm does the bookkeeping, with its own Exact Online licence.** The administrations are in your firm's Exact Online. aiGust gets them if the user who activated has access.
- **Your client does the bookkeeping, with their own Exact Online licence,** and gave your firm access. aiGust only gets the administration if the user who activated the connector also has access to it.

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported).
