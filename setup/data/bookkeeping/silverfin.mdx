title: "Silverfin"
description: "Connect Silverfin to aiGust for trial balances, social balance sheets and documents."
---

Silverfin is a compliance connector. Firms use Silverfin to close the financial year and prepare the tax return. Silverfin itself gets its data from bookkeeping software such as Exact Online or Yuki.

## What aiGust gets from Silverfin

- **Trial balances,** converted into the common aiGust format. aiGust gets no transactions and no business partners. See [Bookkeeping and compliance connectors](/guide/dashboard/data-sync#bookkeeping-and-compliance-connectors).
- **Financial periods,** one per report in Silverfin. See [Compliance connectors](/guide/concepts/financial-periods#compliance-connectors).
- **The social balance sheet,** for personnel reporting. See [Silverfin social data](/setup/data/social/silverfin).
- **Documents** of the administration, in the cloud drive. See [Files from Silverfin](/guide/documents/cloud-drive#files-from-silverfin).

Silverfin doesn't tell aiGust what changed. So aiGust syncs everything from Silverfin, every night.

When an administration is in Silverfin and in a bookkeeping connector, aiGust uses the bookkeeping connector for the figures. See [How administrations are imported](/setup/data/bookkeeping/how-administrations-are-imported#an-administration-in-more-than-one-connector).

## Add the connector

1. Sign in to Silverfin, and look at the address in your browser, for example `https://live.getsilverfin.com/f/5789/854412/ledgers/...`.
2. In aiGust, click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click **Add**.
3. Choose **Silverfin**, and click **Next**.
4. Give the connector a **Name**.
5. Under **Your Silverfin domain & firm ID**, fill in the parts of that address: the domain, usually `live`, and the firm ID: the number after `/f/`, here `5789`. Click **Create connector**.
6. Click **Activate now**. Sign in to Silverfin, and give aiGust access.

The first administrations and figures appear in aiGust about 24 hours later.
