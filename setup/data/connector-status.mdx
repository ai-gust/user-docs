---
title: "Check connector status"
description: "Open a connector to check its health, change its settings, reactivate it, and see which administrations aiGust found in it."
---

The list of connectors doesn't show whether a connector works. To check that, open the connector: click **Data sources** in the firm menu, choose **Connectors**, and click the connector. A panel opens with these tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | Change what you filled in when you added the connector. |
| **Health** | Check whether the connector works. |
| **Activation** | Activate the connector again. Only for connectors you activate by signing in to your software. |
| **Administrations** | See which administrations aiGust found in this connector. |

For the status of one administration, such as its last sync, see the **Data source** widget on its dashboard. See [Data source and sync](/guide/dashboard/data-sync).

## Health

**Health** checks the connector right when you open the tab. What it checks depends on the software. For Exact Online, for example:

- **Has a correct configuration?** Are the settings complete and valid?
- **Has a valid token?** Does aiGust still have access to Exact Online? It also shows until when.
- **Is reachable?** Can aiGust reach Exact Online?

**Global Status** at the top sums it up. When all checks pass, the connector works. A check that fails is marked in red, and tells you what's wrong.

The list of connectors doesn't show failing checks. So when data seems to be missing or out of date, open the connector and look at **Health**.

## Settings

**Settings** shows the same fields as when you added the connector, such as the **Name**, or the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** for Exact Online. To change a secret, click the pencil next to it.

## Activation

For Exact Online, Silverfin, Horus, Octopus and Partena, aiGust has access because someone signed in to that software and gave access. If **Health** says the access is no longer valid, click **Activate now** on **Activation**, and give access again. See [Activate the connector](/setup/data/overview#activate-the-connector).

aiGust renews its access by itself, so you rarely need this. Except for Partena: there, someone has to give access again every 6 months. aiGust doesn't warn you yet, so put it in your calendar.

## Administrations

**Administrations** lists the administrations aiGust found in this connector, with their name and VAT number. Search to check whether a client is in it.

It's only a list: you can't sync an administration here, or see when it was last synced. Use the [Data source widget](/guide/dashboard/data-sync) of the administration for that.

Not seeing an administration you expected? Check that the account that activated the connector has access to it in your software, and that it's not on the [Black & whitelist](/setup/data/bookkeeping/include-exclude-administrations). A new administration also only shows up after the next nightly sync.

## When a sync fails

1. Open the connector, and check **Health**.
2. If the access is no longer valid, activate the connector again on **Activation**.
3. If the configuration isn't correct, fix it on **Settings**. For example, when the client secret of your Business Central app registration expired in Microsoft Entra.
4. Still not working? Report it with the bug icon in the top bar. See [Report a bug](/setup/support/report-a-bug).
