Benchmarking gives you an objective, data-driven view of how your client's business performs relative to comparable businesses in the same sector. Instead of relying on gut feel or anecdotal comparisons, you can walk into every advisory meeting with a clear picture of where your client leads, where they lag, and where the biggest opportunities for improvement lie.

## What benchmarking does

aiGust benchmarking measures a client's key financial ratios and KPIs against a curated peer group drawn from businesses with similar characteristics. The result is a ranked percentile score for each metric, so you can instantly see whether your client is outperforming or underperforming the market — and by how much.

## How aiGust selects the peer group

aiGust builds each client's peer group automatically using three matching criteria applied in combination:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sector classification">
    aiGust maps the client's primary business activity to a standardised industry sector. This is derived from the chart of accounts and any sector tags applied during onboarding. You can review and override the assigned sector from the client's **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Company type">
    The peer group is filtered to businesses of the same legal and structural type — for example, sole traders are compared with sole traders, and limited companies are compared with limited companies. This removes distortions caused by differences in tax treatment and capital structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size band">
    aiGust places the client into a revenue size band and matches peers within the same band. Size bands are recalculated each period as the client's revenue changes, so the peer group stays relevant as the business grows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  aiGust requires a minimum peer group size to produce statistically reliable results. If too few peers match all three criteria, aiGust widens the size band before widening the sector. A banner on the benchmarking report tells you if any criteria were relaxed.
</Note>

## Reading the benchmarking report

Open a client record, navigate to **Insights**, and select **Benchmarking**. The report is divided into sections, one for each KPI category.

Each KPI card shows:

- **Your client's value** — the actual figure for the most recent period.
- **Percentile position** — where the client ranks within the peer group, expressed as a percentage (e.g. 72nd percentile means the client outperforms 72% of peers).
- **Peer median** — the midpoint value across the peer group, useful as a quick reference target.
- **Quartile badge** — a colour-coded label indicating whether the client is in the top quartile (green), second quartile (teal), third quartile (amber), or bottom quartile (red).

### Quartile interpretation guide

| Quartile | Percentile range | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Top quartile | 75th–100th | Outperforming the majority of peers — a genuine competitive strength |
| Second quartile | 50th–74th | Above median but with clear room to improve |
| Third quartile | 25th–49th | Below median — a potential area of focus |
| Bottom quartile | 0th–24th | Significantly underperforming peers — a priority advisory opportunity |

<Tip>
  Sort the KPI list by **quartile (ascending)** to surface the bottom-quartile metrics first. These are your highest-priority conversation starters.
</Tip>

## KPIs benchmarked by aiGust

aiGust benchmarks metrics across four categories:

<Accordion title="Profitability">
  - Gross profit margin
  - Net profit margin
  - EBITDA margin
  - Operating expense ratio
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Liquidity and working capital">
  - Current ratio
  - Quick ratio
  - Debtor days (days sales outstanding)
  - Creditor days (days payable outstanding)
  - Inventory days (where applicable)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Growth">
  - Year-on-year revenue growth
  - Year-on-year gross profit growth
  - Headcount growth rate
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Efficiency">
  - Revenue per employee
  - Staff cost as a percentage of revenue
  - Overhead as a percentage of revenue
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Not all KPIs are available for every sector or company type. KPIs that cannot be calculated from the connected data are hidden from the report rather than shown as zero.
</Note>

## Using benchmarking to start advisory conversations

Benchmarking is most powerful when it shifts the conversation from reporting the past to planning for the future. Use the following approaches to make benchmarking a regular part of your client engagement:

**Open with the headline score.** Share the overall percentile rank at the start of a meeting to frame the discussion. Clients respond well to knowing where they stand relative to the market before diving into details.

**Focus on one or two bottom-quartile KPIs.** Rather than overwhelming clients with every metric, pick the one or two lowest-ranked KPIs and build the advisory narrative around those. Agree on a target percentile band (for example, moving from the bottom quartile to the median) and revisit progress each quarter.

**Celebrate top-quartile performance.** Benchmarking isn't only about problems. Pointing out that a client's debtor days are in the top quartile builds trust and shows the value you bring as an advisor.

**Use peer median as a target.** When clients ask "what should we be aiming for?", the peer median is a concrete, sector-relevant answer. It avoids prescribing an arbitrary target and grounds the goal in market reality.

<Tip>
  Export the benchmarking report as a PDF from the **Export** menu in the top-right corner of the Benchmarking tab. The PDF is formatted for sharing directly with clients.
</Tip>
