Forecasting transforms aiGust from a tool that describes the past into one that helps you and your clients plan for the future. By projecting revenue, costs, and profit forward based on historical trends and configurable assumptions, you can identify cash shortfalls before they happen, set realistic targets, and have substantive forward-looking conversations in every client meeting. Prepayment analysis complements this by surfacing deferred costs and income that distort the current-period picture — giving you a cleaner baseline to forecast from.

## What the forecast module shows

The aiGust forecast module produces a rolling 12-month forward projection across three core financial dimensions:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Revenue" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot" href="/reports/profit-and-loss">
    Projected total income, broken down by revenue stream where the chart of accounts supports it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Costs" icon="receipt" href="/reports/profit-and-loss">
    Projected cost of goods sold and operating expenditure, segmented by major cost categories.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Profit" icon="chart-line" href="/reports/dashboard">
    Projected gross profit, operating profit, and net profit, with margin percentages shown alongside absolute values.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each projected period is displayed alongside the actual figure for the equivalent period in the prior year, so you can immediately see whether the forecast represents growth, decline, or stability relative to history.

## How aiGust generates forecasts

aiGust's forecasts are trend-based, meaning they are derived from patterns in the client's own historical financial data rather than external market models. The core algorithm works as follows:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Historical baseline">
    aiGust analyses the last 24 months of transaction data (or the full available history if the client has been connected for less than 24 months). Seasonality patterns, growth trajectories, and cost ratios are extracted from this baseline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trend projection">
    The identified trends are extended forward across the forecast horizon. For revenue, aiGust calculates a compound monthly growth rate from the historical data. For costs, aiGust projects based on their historical relationship to revenue, adjusted for any fixed cost components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assumption overlay">
    Configurable assumptions (described in the next section) are applied on top of the trend projection. These allow you to model scenarios that deviate from pure historical extrapolation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Forecasts are regenerated automatically each time new data syncs from the connected accounting system. This means the forecast always reflects the most recent actuals as its starting point.
</Note>

## Adjusting forecast inputs

The default trend-based forecast is a useful starting point, but most client situations benefit from adjustment. Open **Insights → Forecasts** for any administration and select **Edit assumptions** to access the assumption controls.

The following inputs can be adjusted:

| Assumption | Description |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth rate | Override the calculated growth rate with a custom annual or monthly rate |
| Cost of sales % | Fix the cost of sales as a percentage of revenue rather than projecting from trend |
| Staff cost growth | Set a specific growth rate for staff costs, for example to reflect a planned headcount increase |
| One-off items | Add or remove one-off income or cost events in specific forecast months |
| Forecast horizon | Extend or shorten the forecast window (3, 6, or 12 months) |

After adjusting assumptions, click **Recalculate** to see the updated projection immediately. All assumption changes are saved per administration and persist across sessions.

<Tip>
  Use the **Scenario comparison** feature to save up to three named assumption sets (for example, "Base case", "Optimistic", and "Downside") and toggle between them on the forecast chart. This is particularly effective for client meetings where you want to walk through multiple outcomes.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Forecast accuracy depends directly on the quality and completeness of the underlying accounting data. If a client's books have significant uncategorised transactions, reconciliation gaps, or irregular coding, communicate this to your client before presenting the forecast as a planning tool.
</Warning>

## Prepayment analysis

### What it is

Prepayments are payments made in the current period for expenses that relate to future periods — for example, an annual insurance premium paid in January that covers the full calendar year. Deferred income is the mirror image: revenue received in the current period that has not yet been earned. When these items are not correctly accounted for, both the profit figure and the forecast baseline can be materially distorted.

### How aiGust identifies prepaid expenses and deferred income

aiGust scans the balance sheet for movements in prepayment and deferred income accounts. It also applies pattern recognition to the profit and loss, flagging line items that show large, irregular spikes typical of lump-sum prepayment behaviour.

Identified prepayments and deferred income items are listed in the **Prepayment analysis** sub-tab within the Forecasts module. For each item, aiGust shows:

- **Account name** — the nominal account where the item is coded.
- **Identified amount** — the balance or movement aiGust has flagged.
- **Suggested amortisation period** — aiGust's estimate of the period over which the cost or income should be recognised, based on the amount and typical patterns for that account type.
- **Impact on forecast** — the adjustment that would be made to each forecast month if the prepayment were spread correctly.

<Accordion title="How to apply a prepayment adjustment">
  Review each flagged item in the Prepayment analysis list. If you agree with aiGust's suggested amortisation, click **Apply adjustment**. The forecast is immediately recalculated to spread the identified amount across the suggested period. If you want to use a different amortisation period, click **Edit** and enter your preferred number of months before applying.

  Applied adjustments are shown as a separate line in the forecast waterfall chart, clearly labelled as prepayment adjustments, so you can always distinguish between the underlying trend and the restatement effect.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Prepayment adjustments in aiGust are for forecasting and advisory purposes only. They do not post any entries to the connected accounting system. If a formal prepayment restatement is required in the client's books, this must be made directly in the accounting software.
</Note>

## Using forecasts in client meetings

Forecasts are most impactful when they are used to frame decisions, not just report numbers. Here are practical ways to bring the forecast module into your client meetings:

**Lead with the 12-month profit trajectory.** Open the Forecasts tab and share the headline profit chart as your starting point. Ask the client whether the projected trajectory matches their expectations. Discrepancies between the model and the client's own expectations often reveal important context — a major contract win, a planned hire, or a market concern — that should feed back into the assumptions.

**Use scenario comparison for planning conversations.** Walk through the base case, optimistic, and downside scenarios together. Identifying the conditions under which the downside scenario becomes reality is a powerful way to help clients think about risk management.

**Connect forecasts to cash flow conversations.** Even when profit looks healthy, the forecast can reveal months where cost timing creates a cash squeeze. Flagging these in advance positions you to discuss facilities, payment timing, or other mitigations before the client faces the pressure.

**Reference prepayment adjustments to explain profit fluctuations.** If a client is puzzled by why their profit looks lower or higher than expected in a particular month, the prepayment analysis is often the answer. Walking through the adjustment builds the client's financial literacy and demonstrates the depth of your analysis.

<Tip>
  Export any forecast view as a PDF or Excel file using the **Export** button. The Excel export includes the full monthly data table, which clients find useful for incorporating aiGust projections into their own business planning documents.
</Tip>
