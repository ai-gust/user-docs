aiGust is a SaaS platform built for accounting firms. It connects directly to your accounting software, transforms raw bookkeeping data into clear financial reports and KPIs, and gives you everything you need to run productive, insight-driven client meetings. Whether you are an accountant preparing for a quarterly review or a firm administrator setting up your team, this documentation will guide you every step of the way.

## How aiGust Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Accounting Software">
    Link aiGust to your existing accounting package — Exact Online, Yuki, Odoo, Business Central, or any of the other supported connectors. aiGust syncs your clients' financial data automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Your Administrations">
    Organise client files into administrations. Assign sectors, company types, and financial periods so aiGust can benchmark and analyse each client in the right context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Reports & Insights">
    View up-to-date P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow reports. Use AI-powered benchmarking, triggers, and forecasts to spot opportunities and risks before your next client conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Productive Client Meetings">
    Launch a meeting flow directly from aiGust. Walk through financial highlights, discuss alerts, and capture notes — all without switching tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>
