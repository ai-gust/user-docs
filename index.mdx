Accounting is changing. With AI, more and more of the compliance work gets automated, and the value of an accountancy firm moves to advice. Clients no longer only expect their accountant to keep their books in order. They expect someone who thinks with them.

Every step up that mountain adds value for your clients, and keeps your margins high:

- **Number cruncher:** you make sure your client is in line with all financial regulations.
- **Alerter:** you warn your client proactively when something needs attention.
- **Advisor:** you think with your client and help them move forward.
- **Trusted advisor:** your client sees you as their designated advisor, like a lawyer or a doctor.

Today, that advice often depends on a few senior people at your firm. aiGust makes it scalable, so that everyone at your firm can take part in advising clients. It gives your whole team the data, the signals and the tools to prepare and hold a good conversation with every client.

## The aiGust platform

aiGust brings together the financial and personnel data of all your clients, shows what's going on, and supports you from the first signal to the conversation with your client and the follow-up. It's built in four layers.

### 1. The data

aiGust connects to your firm's accounting software and social secretariats. It imports the financial data and the personnel data of your clients, and keeps them up to date. See [Data sources overview](/setup/data/overview).

### 2. The advisory cycle

Around that data, aiGust supports the way your firm advises its clients:

- **Data:** the figures of each client, translated to one chart of accounts with [labels](/guide/concepts/labels).
- **Context:** the client's [sector and company type](/guide/concepts/sectors-and-company-types), and how it compares with its peers.
- **Signals:** [triggers and alerts](/guide/triggers/how-triggers-work) tell you when something needs your attention.
- **Conversation:** you discuss the figures with your client in a [meeting](/guide/meetings/basics).
- **Actions:** you agree on what to do.
- **Follow-up:** you follow up on those actions, and the cycle starts again with new data.

We're working on an inbox, where you'll find all your actions and follow-up in one place.

### 3. Clear insights

aiGust turns the data into views you can search, compare, report and present:

- the [dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard) and the [reports](/guide/reports/overview) of an administration;
- [benchmarks](/guide/benchmarking/how-it-works) against a peer group;
- [forecasts](/guide/forecasts/how-forecasts-work);
- [alerts](/guide/triggers/alerts);
- slides for your [meetings](/guide/meetings/basics);
- the [client portal](/setup/client-portal/overview), where your clients see their own figures.

### 4. AI at every step

AI will support each step and assist you proactively. That's work in progress: we add AI to aiGust step by step, and announce each addition in the [announcements](/guide/getting-started/help#announcements).
