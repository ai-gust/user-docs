---
title: "Read benchmarks"
description: "Select a peer group for an administration and read its benchmarks in charts, KPI cards and the Performance vs benchmark widget."
---

Once an administration has a peer group, aiGust shows benchmarks next to its own figures: in charts, in KPI cards and in the **Performance vs benchmark** widget.

## Select the peer group

Open the administration, click **Data settings** at the top of the page and expand **Benchmark group**. The list shows the administration's default and custom [peer groups](/guide/benchmarking/peer-groups). Select the one to compare with. All charts and KPI cards then use that peer group.

<Frame caption="Selecting the peer group under Data settings. Inactive peer groups are greyed out.">
  <img src="/images/benchmarking/select-peer-group.jpg" alt="Administration dashboard with the Data settings panel open and the Benchmark group list highlighted, showing a selected custom peer group, two default peer groups, a greyed-out inactive peer group and the option Disable Benchmarking" />
</Frame>

- **Inactive peer groups,** for example with fewer than 10 peers, are greyed out and can't be selected.
- **To hide all benchmarks,** for example when there's no relevant peer group, select **Disable Benchmarking**.

The peer group you select becomes the administration's [default peer group](/guide/benchmarking/peer-groups#the-default-peer-group). Everyone who opens the administration sees the same peer group, and the **Performance vs benchmark** score uses it.

## Benchmarks in charts

Charts show the administration's own figures together with the benchmark of its peer group.

<Frame caption="EBITDA as a percentage of turnover, with the band from Q25 to Q75 of the peer group behind it, and the table with the values below.">
  <img src="/images/concepts/kpi-types.jpg" alt="Line chart of EBITDA as a percentage of turnover from 2023 to 2026 with a shaded benchmark band, and below it a table with rows Your company, Bench 75% quartile, Bench median, Bench 25% quartile and Sample size" />
</Frame>

- **Your own figures** are shown in your firm's main brand colour: as bars for absolute values, and as a line for percentages and margins.
- **The benchmark** is shown in your firm's secondary brand colour, as a band from Q25 to Q75. The middle half of the peers falls inside the band.
- **The median** is hidden by default, to keep the chart readable.

### The table below the chart

The table repeats the figures from the chart:

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Your company** | The administration's own value |
| **Bench 75% quartile** | Q75: three quarters of the peers have a lower value |
| **Bench median** | Q50: half of the peers have a lower value |
| **Bench 25% quartile** | Q25: a quarter of the peers have a lower value |
| **Sample size** | The number of peers the benchmark is based on, in ranges such as 10-25, 25-50 and 50+ |

Click the eye icon next to a benchmark row to show or hide it in the chart. Showing the median adds it as a line. Hiding one of the quartiles removes the band.

## Benchmarks in KPI cards

The KPI cards on the [administration dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard) can show the benchmark as a small boxplot.

<Frame caption="A KPI card with its benchmark: the band, the median (square) and the administration's own value (dot).">
  <img src="/images/benchmarking/kpi-card-boxplot.jpg" alt="KPI card Turnover 2025 with value 7.35 million euro and plus 60 percent, with below it a bar from 3M to 7M showing the benchmark band, a square for the median and a dot for the company's own value" />
</Frame>

- **The band** runs from Q25 to Q75 of the peer group.
- **The square** marks the median.
- **The dot** marks the administration's own value.

The line doesn't run from the lowest to the highest peer. It covers the band, and extends to the administration's own value when that falls outside the band.

The benchmark doesn't need the same KPI type as the main number. A card can show the absolute turnover, with the benchmark of the turnover growth below it. Your firm sets this per template. See [Firm-wide KPI dashboard templates](/configure/reporting/kpi-dashboard-templates#choose-what-each-kpi-card-shows).

KPI cards follow the **Year-to-date view** setting under **Data settings**. With the setting off, cards always show the last closed financial year, never the open one.

## Performance vs benchmark

The **Performance vs benchmark** widget sums up in one score how the administration performed compared with its default peer group. See [Performance scores](/guide/dashboard/scores).
