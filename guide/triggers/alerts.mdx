---
title: "Work with alerts"
description: "See which triggers apply to an administration and why an alert is active, and snooze or disable alerts that don't need action."
---

An administration's dashboard shows which [triggers](/guide/triggers/how-triggers-work) apply to it, and which of them have raised an alert.

## See the alerts of an administration

The **Active alerts** card on the [administration dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard) shows two numbers: the active alerts, out of the triggers that apply. *2 / 6* means 2 active alerts out of 6 applicable triggers.

<Frame caption="The Active alerts card on the administration dashboard.">
  <img src="/images/triggers/active-alerts-card.jpg" alt="Administration dashboard with the Active alerts card highlighted in the right column, showing 2 / 6." />
</Frame>

Click the card to open **Triggers & alerts** in a side panel. The **Alerts** tab shows the active alerts, and the number next to it is how many there are. Turn off **Only active** to see all applicable triggers, including those without an alert.

For each trigger, you see:

- **Its name,** and a badge that shows whether it's a built-in trigger (**aiGust**) or one of your firm's own (**Tenant**). An **alert** badge marks an active alert.
- **Its description,** which explains what the trigger looks for and what to check.
- **Each criterion,** with the administration's actual value next to the condition. That tells you why the alert is active, or why it isn't.

<Frame caption="Two active alerts. The Operational cash flow of −€141,786 meets the condition ≤ €0.">

It shows Weak liquidity (<1) with a current ratio of 0.15 against the condition < 1.00, and Profit without cash generation with an EBITDA of 55.60% against > 5.00% and an operational cash flow of −€141,786 against ≤ €0." />
</Frame>

## Snooze or disable an alert

Not every alert needs action. A profit with a negative cash flow is usually worth looking into, but for a management company that has just bought a car, it's easy to explain. Click the three dots on the alert, and choose:

| Option | What it does | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Snooze until...** | Hides the alert until the end of the period you choose. After that, it's shown again if the administration still meets the trigger. | The situation is explained for now, but you want to check again later, for example next financial year |
| **Disable alert** | Hides the alert for this administration until you enable it again. | The trigger doesn't make sense for this administration |

<Frame caption="The options of an alert.">
  <img src="/images/triggers/alert-menu.jpg" alt="The three-dots menu of the Profit without cash generation alert, with the options Snooze until..., Disable alert and a greyed-out Enable alert." />
</Frame>

When you snooze an alert, you choose a financial year, quarter or month. The alert stays hidden until the last day of that period.

<Frame caption="Choose the period until which the alert is snoozed.">
  <img src="/images/triggers/snooze-dialog.jpg" alt="Snooze until... dialog with a period picker listing the year 2030, the quarter 2030 Q4, and the months 12/2030, 11/2030 and 10/2030 with their start and end dates." />
</Frame>

Snoozing and disabling apply to the administration, so everyone at your firm who opens it sees the same.

To switch off a trigger for all administrations, a key user can disable it at firm level. See [Create custom triggers](/configure/triggers/custom-triggers#rename-disable-or-delete-a-trigger) and [Manage built-in triggers](/configure/triggers/built-in-triggers).

## Enable an alert again

The **Disabled triggers** tab of the side panel lists the disabled and snoozed alerts. A snoozed alert shows the date until which it's snoozed.

To show an alert again, click the three dots on it and choose **Enable alert**. This works for disabled and snoozed alerts: it ends a snooze early.

To see all administrations that snoozed or disabled a trigger, open the trigger and click the crossed-out bell. See [Open a trigger](/guide/triggers/how-triggers-work#open-a-trigger).

## Focus on the administrations you manage

Click **Follow** on the dashboard of the administrations you manage. On the [triggers overview](/guide/triggers/how-triggers-work#the-triggers-overview), turn on **Following only** to see the alerts for just those administrations, rather than for the whole firm.
