title: "Personnel KPIs and ratios"
description: "Which figures aiGust takes from the social balance sheet, and how the ratios per FTE are calculated."
---

The personnel KPIs combine two sources:

- the [social balance sheet](/guide/personnel/data-sources), for the number of employees in FTE;
- the [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss), for turnover, added value, EBITDA and personnel costs.

## FTE

aiGust uses the average number of employees in FTE, code 1003 of the social balance sheet. That's the **Total nb of FTEs** on the [personnel dashboard](/guide/personnel/dashboard#revenue-vs-personnel-cost-vs-fte).

## FTE Division

The [FTE Division](/guide/personnel/dashboard#fte-division) uses the number of employees in FTE per category, on the closing date of the period:

| Category | Code |
|---|---|
| **Management** | 1303 |
| **Office workers** | 1343 |
| **Labour workers** | 1323 |
| **Other** | 1333 |

Each category is shown as a share of the total of the four.

## Ratios per FTE

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Turnover / FTE** | (Turnover ÷ FTE) × (365 ÷ days in the period) |
| **Added value / FTE** | (Added value ÷ FTE) × (365 ÷ days in the period) |
| **EBITDA / FTE** | (EBITDA ÷ FTE) × (365 ÷ days in the period) |
| **Costs / FTE** | (Personnel costs ÷ FTE) × (365 ÷ days in the period) |

The factor 365 ÷ days in the period converts a period shorter or longer than a year to a full year. For a regular financial year of 365 days, it's 1.

**Example:** in 2025, an administration had a turnover of € 7.352.621,59 and 9,90 FTE. Its turnover per FTE is € 7.352.621,59 ÷ 9,90 = € 742.689,05.

## Personnel costs

**Personnel costs** as a KPI card is the personnel costs as a percentage of turnover:

```
personnel costs % = personnel costs ÷ turnover × 100%
```

Its change compared with the previous year is in percentage points. See [Vertical delta](/guide/concepts/kpis#vertical-delta).

## Where you find them

The ratios per FTE are also part of the [ratios](/guide/reports/ratios), so you can use them in [triggers](/guide/triggers/how-triggers-work), [peer groups](/guide/benchmarking/peer-groups) and dashboards. They're only available for administrations with social data.
