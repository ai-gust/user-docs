aiGust's personnel KPIs come from the social balance sheet of an administration.

## The social balance sheet

The social balance sheet is a report on a company's staff. The social secretariat, such as Partena, SD Worx or Acerta, usually provides it once a year. The accountant then attaches it to the annual accounts that go to the National Bank of Belgium.

It contains, among other things:

- the number of employees in FTE over the period, split into full-time and part-time;
- the split between labour workers, office workers and management, at the end of the period;
- the total number of hours worked;
- other figures about the staff.

For what aiGust calculates from it, see [Personnel KPIs and ratios](/guide/personnel/kpis-and-ratios).

## Where aiGust gets it

| Source | How it works | How up to date |
|---|---|---|
| **Silverfin** | Accountants usually enter the social balance sheet in Silverfin. From there, it goes into the annual accounts (XBRL) for the National Bank. aiGust imports it from Silverfin. | Once a year, and only once it's entered in Silverfin. |
| **Partena** | aiGust has a partnership with Partena. With the Partena connector, you get a social balance sheet every month, from 2026 on. | Every month, so a much more accurate view. |

Your firm sets up these sources. See [Social data overview](/setup/data/social/overview), [Silverfin social data](/setup/data/social/silverfin) and [Partena](/setup/data/social/partena).

If an administration has data from both, aiGust uses Partena, because it's the most detailed.

We're working on a third source: the social balance sheets from the National Bank of Belgium.

## When an administration has no personnel data

Without a social balance sheet, aiGust can't calculate anything per FTE. The [personnel dashboard](/guide/personnel/dashboard) and the ratios per FTE are then not available: they're disabled for that administration.
