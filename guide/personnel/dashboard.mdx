The personnel dashboard shows how productive an administration's staff is, and how the staff and its cost evolve compared with the business. Click **Reports** in the administration menu, and choose **Personnel**.

The dashboard needs personnel data. See [Personnel data sources](/guide/personnel/data-sources).

## Choose the period

At the top right, choose the year with the arrows, or click it to pick another. Click the button next to it to switch between:

- **Financial year:** full financial years.
- **Quarterly figures:** quarters. See [Quarters](/guide/concepts/financial-periods#quarters).
- **Per period:** the financial periods of the administration.

Social balance sheets rarely match the quarters or periods of an administration. See [Social periods and financial years](/guide/personnel/social-periods) for how aiGust makes them fit, and how precise the result is.

## KPI cards

The cards at the top show the main personnel KPIs of the chosen year, with their change compared with the previous year:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Turnover / FTE** | Turnover per full-time employee. |
| **Added value / FTE** | Added value per full-time employee. |
| **Costs / FTE** | Personnel costs per full-time employee. |
| **EBITDA / FTE** | EBITDA per full-time employee. |
| **Personnel costs** | Personnel costs as a percentage of turnover. Its change is in percentage points. |

A green badge means the change is good news, a red badge bad news. Rising costs per FTE, for example, show in red.

For the formulas, see [Personnel KPIs and ratios](/guide/personnel/kpis-and-ratios).

## FTE Division

How the staff is divided between **Management**, **Labour workers**, office workers and **Other**, in FTE, on the closing date of the financial year. See [FTE Division](/guide/personnel/kpis-and-ratios#fte-division) for the codes of the social balance sheet behind it.

## Revenue vs Personnel Cost vs FTE

Shows whether the staff and its cost grow in line with the business:

- the bars show the number of FTEs, on the right axis;
- the lines show turnover and personnel costs, on the left axis.

The table below the chart shows the values. Click the eye next to a line to hide it from the chart, or to show it again.

Use the [KPI type selector](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types) above the chart to see the values as they are (<KpiTypeIcon type="currency" />) or as growth compared with the previous year (<KpiTypeIcon type="horizontal" />). The percentage views aren't available for this chart.

## Personnel ratio evolution

Shows how the ratios per FTE evolved over the years. **Overview** shows them all together. Click a tab to see one of them on its own: **Revenue/FTE**, **Added Value/FTE**, **EBITDA/FTE** or **Personnel Cost/FTE**.
