---
title: "First steps in a new administration"
sidebarTitle: "First steps"
description: "What to check the first time you open an administration, so its figures, KPIs and benchmarks are right from the start."
---

aiGust fills in most of a new administration by itself: its figures, its sector and company type, and a label for each account. Those are suggestions. Check them once, the first time you open the administration. Your reports, KPIs, benchmarks and meetings are then right from the start.

It takes a few minutes for most administrations. The label mapping can take longer for a large chart of accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the sector and company type">
    The first time you open the administration, the **Please verify the sector and company type** pop-up opens. Check the economic activity and the company type, change them if needed, and confirm. They decide which peers aiGust compares the administration with.

    <Frame caption="The pop-up to verify the sector and company type.">
      <img src="/images/concepts/sector-verification.jpg" alt="Pop-up titled Please verify the sector and company type, showing economic activity RIZIV-10-S07 Ophtalmologist in sector Healthcare and subsector Specialist Medical Practice, and company type Operating Company, each with a confirm and an edit button" />
    </Frame>

    See [Verify and change the sector and company type](/guide/concepts/sectors-and-company-types#verify-and-change-the-sector-and-company-type). To verify many administrations at once, use the [list of administrations](/guide/getting-started/navigation#verify-the-sector-and-company-type-of-many-administrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the data source">
    On the dashboard, the **Data source** widget shows where the figures come from and when they were last synced. Check that it's the right source. A bookkeeping connector gives you more than a compliance file: monthly figures, business partners and transactions.

    See [Data source and sync](/guide/dashboard/data-sync).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the label mapping">
    Go to **Settings** › **Mapping**. The **Work to do!** banner shows how many accounts still need checking. Click **Resolve** to go through them one group at a time. Pay most attention to the **Uncertain cases** and the **Unlabeled** accounts. Wrong labels skew the label KPIs, such as energy costs, and the benchmarks.

    <Frame caption="The Work to do! banner shows what's left to check.">
      <img src="/images/concepts/mapping-work-to-do.jpg" alt="Mapping tab of the administration settings with the Work to do! banner: aiGust did an initial automatic mapping of the chart of accounts and asks you to review it. To check: 8 fairly certain, 1 uncertain cases and 5 unlabeled, with a Resolve button on the right." />
    </Frame>

    See [Map accounts to labels](/guide/concepts/label-mapping).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Split costs into fixed and variable, if you use the contribution margin">
    To show a contribution margin in the profit and loss, set the variable part of each cost account on the same **Mapping** tab. Skip this step if you don't use it.

    See [Contribution margin](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss#contribution-margin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a relevant peer group">
    aiGust compares the administration with a default peer group: companies in the same subsector, or the same subsector and company type. Check whether those peers are really comparable. A small family business and a large group in the same subsector often aren't.

    If they aren't, create a custom peer group, for example with a size range on turnover or employees. Keep a margin above the minimum of 10 peers. See [Tips for a good peer group](/guide/benchmarking/peer-groups#tips-for-a-good-peer-group).

    Then click **Data settings** at the top of the page, and choose the peer group under **Benchmark group**. Everyone who opens the administration, and your client in the client portal, sees the same peer group. See [Select the peer group](/guide/benchmarking/reading-benchmarks#select-the-peer-group).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the administration, if you work on it">
    Click **Follow** at the top of the dashboard. You then find the administration quickly with **Following only**, in the list of administrations and in the alerts. See [Follow the administrations you work on](/guide/getting-started/navigation#follow-the-administrations-you-work-on).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your client access, if you want">
    Add the people at your client who should see their figures and meetings in the client portal, on the **Client access** tab.

    See [Give your client access](/guide/client-portal/give-access).
  </Step>
</Steps>
