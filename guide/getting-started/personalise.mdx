## Your name and language

1. Click **Settings** in your user menu, and open the **Profile** tab.
2. Change your **First name** and **Last name**, or choose a **Language**: **nl**, **en** or **fr**.
3. Click **Save**.

<Frame caption="Your name and language.">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/profile.jpg" alt="Settings dialog on the Profile tab, with First name Balthazar, Last name Boma, Language with the options nl, en (selected) and fr, and a Save button." />
</Frame>

The language applies to aiGust, the report labels and the emails aiGust sends you.

## How reports look

Your firm sets how financial reports look by default, such as the column order and how many years you see. You can override that for yourself.

1. Click **Settings** in your user menu, and open the **Report view settings** tab.
2. Turn on **Use your custom reporting view instead of the default settings**.
3. Change the settings, and click **Save**.

While the switch is off, you see your firm's settings, greyed out.

The settings in **Default report view** apply to every report:

| Group | Setting | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Value formatting** | **Compact value notation** | Shows thousands as K and millions as M: € 4,23M instead of € 4.230.000. |
| | **Hide empty rows** | Hides lines that are empty in every year shown. |
| **Column definition** | **Column order** | **Most recent first** puts the most recent year on the left. **Most recent last** puts it on the right. |
| | **# Periods to show** | How many financial years the table shows at once. |
| | **# Periods to display in quarter view** | How many quarters or periods the table shows at once. |

To use other settings for one report, turn on **These settings deviate from the standard** for **P&L**, **Balance** or **Cashflow**, and set its own values.

Click **Reset** to go back to your firm's settings. If your firm hasn't set any, you get aiGust's defaults.

## Light mode and dark mode

Under **Appearance** in your user menu, choose:

- the moon for dark mode;
- the arrows to follow the setting of your computer. That's the default;
- the sun for light mode.

Your firm's key users set the colours and logo for both modes. See [Light mode and dark mode](/setup/branding/portal).
