The icons at the top right of aiGust take you to help and news.

<Frame caption="The icons at the top right.">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/top-bar-icons.jpg" alt="Three icons: a bug, a megaphone with a yellow dot, and a user icon." />
</Frame>

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| The bug | Report a bug or ask for a feature. Only key users see it. If you're not a key user, report problems to a key user at your firm. See [Report a bug](/setup/support/report-a-bug). |
| The megaphone | Opens the announcements: new features and product news. A yellow dot means there's news you haven't read yet. |
| The user icon | Opens your user menu, with the **Roadmap**, the **Knowledge base** and the **aiGust academy**. See [Personalise aiGust](/guide/getting-started/personalise). |

## Announcements

Click the megaphone to read what's new in aiGust. Each announcement explains a new feature, often with screenshots or a video. The tags group them by month, such as **features-202608** for August 2026.

<Frame caption="The announcements.">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/announcements.jpg" alt="Announcements tab of the aiGust portal, with the tabs Ideas, Roadmap and Announcements at the top. On the left, tags per month from Features-202510 to features-202608 with their number of announcements. The list, sorted Newest first, shows Labeling 3.0: full account mapping and Client user activity & audit trails, each with a screenshot, a text and a button Check out the video here." />
</Frame>

The portal is open to everyone: you don't sign in to it.

## Ideas and roadmap

Click **Roadmap** in your user menu. The same portal opens, with three tabs:

- **Ideas:** suggestions from aiGust users. Vote on the ideas you want, or click **New Idea** to suggest your own.
- **Roadmap:** what we're planning and building.
- **Announcements:** see [Announcements](#announcements).

<Frame caption="The ideas of aiGust users.">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/ideas.jpg" alt="Ideas tab of the aiGust portal. On the left, the statuses New requests, In development, In test, Available and Rejected, and the categories Feature request and Improvement, each with a count. The list, sorted by Latest, shows ideas with their number of votes, a description, a status such as New requests or Available, and a number of comments. A New Idea button at the top right." />
</Frame>

Each idea has a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **New requests** | Suggested, not planned yet. |
| **In development** | We're building it. |
| **In test** | We're testing it. |
| **Available** | You can use it in aiGust. |
| **Rejected** | We won't build it. |

## Knowledge base

Click **Knowledge base** in your user menu to open this documentation.

## aiGust academy

Click **aiGust academy** in your user menu to watch training videos. **Basic training** explains the common parts of aiGust, such as charts and reports. **Advanced training** goes deeper. The videos are in English.
