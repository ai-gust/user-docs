You sign in to aiGust at [app.ai-gust.io](https://app.ai-gust.io). The easiest way is with the Google Workspace or Microsoft account of your firm. You can also set a separate password for aiGust.

## The welcome email

When your firm adds you as a user, you get a welcome email. It has two buttons:

- **Open aiGust** takes you to the sign-in page. Use it to sign in with Google or Microsoft.
- **Wachtwoord instellen** (Set password) lets you set a password, if you don't sign in with Google or Microsoft. See [Sign in with a password](#sign-in-with-a-password).

## Sign in with Google or Microsoft

1. Go to [app.ai-gust.io](https://app.ai-gust.io), or click **Open aiGust** in the welcome email.
2. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with microsoft**.
3. Sign in with your work account, as you usually do.

You then stay signed in for up to 10 hours.

The email address of your Google or Microsoft account has to be the same as the one your firm used to add you in aiGust. If it isn't, aiGust shows an error: "User … authenticated with identity provider google does not exist. Please contact your administrator." Ask the administrator of your firm to check your email address in aiGust.

The first time someone at your firm signs in with Microsoft, Microsoft may ask for consent for aiGust. Only an administrator of your Microsoft organisation can give it. See [First sign-in as administrator](/setup/first-login).

## Sign in with a password

If you don't use Google or Microsoft, set a password first:

1. In the welcome email, click **Wachtwoord instellen**.
2. On the page that opens, click **Stel nu je wachtwoord in** (Set your password now).
3. Enter your new password, and confirm it.

<Frame caption="Set your password, here in Dutch.">
  <img src="/images/sign-in/set-password.jpg" alt="Page with the aiGust logo saying Welkom bij aiGust! Je bent er bijna! (Welcome to aiGust! You're almost there!) and a button Stel nu je wachtwoord in." />
</Frame>

The link in the welcome email is valid for 5 days. If it has expired, you land on a page with a button to send a new link. Click it, and you get an email to reset your password: follow the link in it to set your password.

Your password needs:

- at least 8 characters;
- at least one letter, one digit and one uppercase letter.

A password is valid for 180 days. After that, aiGust asks you to choose a new one when you sign in.

aiGust then takes you back to the sign-in page. To sign in:

1. Click **Login with aiGust credentials**.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

Select **Remember me** to stay signed in on this device.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Login with aiGust credentials**, and then **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your **Email**.
3. Click **Request reset password link**.
4. Open the email, and follow the link to set a new password.

Don't see the email? Check your spam folder.

## Problems signing in

Click **Problems signing in?** on the sign-in page to email aiGust support at [support@ai-gust.io](mailto:support@ai-gust.io).

## Two-factor authentication

aiGust doesn't ask for a second code when you sign in with a password. If your firm wants two-factor authentication, sign in with Google or Microsoft: the security settings of your Google Workspace or Microsoft organisation then apply, including two-factor authentication.
