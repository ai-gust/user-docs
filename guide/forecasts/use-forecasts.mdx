---
title: "Use forecasts in reports"
description: "Activate a forecast so the dashboard, charts and financial reports show the expected figures for the rest of the year."
---

A forecast only shows up in the reports once you activate it.

## Activate a forecast

1. Open the administration, and click **Data settings** at the top of the page.
2. Open **Forecast**.
3. Under **Forecast**, choose the forecast. Under **Scenario**, choose the scenario.

To stop showing a forecast, choose **Disable Forecast**.

aiGust adds the forecast to the actual figures. The open financial year then shows the actual figures up to the up-to-date-until date, plus the forecast for the rest of the year. This applies to:

- the [administration dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard) and its charts
- the financial reports, such as the [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss)

The open financial year only appears when **Show open periods** is on. See [Years, quarters and periods](/guide/reports/table/periods).

## How forecast figures are marked

- **In charts,** the bar of the year with a forecast is hatched.
- **In the table below a chart,** the column has a **Forecast** badge.
- **In the financial reports,** the column has a **FCST** badge.

## Forecasts in meetings and the client portal

- **In meetings,** you can use the forecast to discuss the expected result with your client.
- **In the client portal,** your client sees an activated forecast, but can't change it or switch it off.

## What forecasts aren't used for

- **Benchmarks** are always calculated on actual figures, never on forecasts.
- **Actual vs budget comparisons** aren't available.
