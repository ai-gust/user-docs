---
title: "Create and edit a forecast"
description: "Create a forecast, change it in the forecast editor on any level, and save scenarios."
---

Open the administration and click **Forecasts** in the menu. The page shows the administration's forecasts.

## Create a forecast

1. Click **Create**.
2. Check the **Period to forecast**. It runs from the period after the up-to-date-until date to the end of the financial year.
3. Change the name if you want. aiGust proposes one, such as *Forecast end 2026 (september)*.
4. Click **Let's go!**

aiGust calculates the automatic forecast and opens it in the forecast editor.

## The forecasts overview

Each forecast is shown as a tile, with:

- **Scenarios,** with the number of scenarios and a badge for each one.
- **Period,** the period the forecast covers.
- **Created,** the date and time the forecast was created.

aiGust compares the forecast period with the [up-to-date-until date](/guide/concepts/financial-periods#the-up-to-date-until-date), which it treats as today. A forecast can get one of these badges:

- **Overlapping:** the forecast is still useful, but actual figures are now available for some of its periods. For example, a forecast for 02/2026 to 12/2026, when the figures are up to date until 31/05/2026.
- **Outdated:** the whole forecast period is in the past. For example, a forecast for 09/2025 to 12/2025, when the figures are up to date until 31/05/2026.

Turn on **Hide outdated forecasts** to show only the forecasts you can still use. Switch between **Tiles** and a list at the top right.

Click a forecast to open it in the forecast editor.

## The forecast editor

The editor shows the profit and loss with these columns:

| Column | Example | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Last financial year** | 2025 | The actual figures of the previous financial year |
| **Year to date** | 2026YTD | The actual figures of the current year so far |
| **Forecast** | 2026 end | The forecast for the rest of the year. The yellow cells are the ones you can change. |
| **Change** | △ | The change of the full year compared with last year |
| **Full year** | 2026 | The actual figures plus the forecast |

Click **Open/close all** to open or close all lines. At the lowest level, each account shows its forecast method, such as **copy + trend**. If your firm splits an account into a fixed and a variable part, for example 50/50, the account appears twice, marked **fixed** and **var**. **Show contribution margin** groups those parts into variable and fixed costs, as in the [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss#contribution-margin).

### Show more detail

Three toggles at the top add information:

- **Show previous values** shows the figure of the same period last year below each figure.
- **Show delta** adds the last 12 months and the 12 months before, with the change between them. aiGust uses this growth for the **copy + trend** method.
- **Show periods** shows each period of the year in a separate column. You can then change the forecast period by period.

## Change the forecast

The editor recalculates everything immediately, like a large pivot table. aiGust always stores the forecast per account. A change on a higher level is translated to the accounts below it.

### Type a new amount

Click a yellow cell and type the new amount for the forecast period. Click the check mark to confirm.

If you change a line on a higher level, for example turnover, aiGust spreads the change over the accounts below it and over the periods, in proportion to their current values.

<Frame caption="Changing the forecast of the turnover.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/inline-edit.jpg" alt="The 2026 end cell of Turnover in edit mode, with the amount 2585111,6 and a check mark to confirm." />
</Frame>

### Change the values per period

Click the pencil next to a figure to open **Edit values**. You see each period of last year next to the current year, and you can change the forecast periods. For an account with a trend, you can raise or lower the growth of each period with **−** and **+**. Click **Submit** to apply.

<Frame caption="Edit values: the growth per period compared with last year.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/edit-values-trend.jpg" alt="Edit values dialog for 610200 Rent machinery and equipment, with last year and current year per month. The actual months 01/2026 to 08/2026 are fixed; the months 09/2026 to 12/2026 can be changed, each with a growth of +42.2% and − and + buttons." />
</Frame>

### Change the growth rate

Click the change (△) of a line to open **Change growth rate**. Enter the growth compared with last year, or change it with **−** and **+**, and click **Confirm**.

<Frame caption="Setting the growth rate of a line.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/change-growth-rate.jpg" alt="Change growth rate dialog with the value -7,34, − and + buttons, and a Confirm button." />
</Frame>

### Change the method of an account

Click the three dots next to an account and choose another [method](/guide/forecasts/how-forecasts-work#how-aigust-calculates-the-forecast): **Set to zero**, **Extend**, **copy** or **copy + trend**. aiGust recalculates the account's forecast.

<Frame caption="Choosing the forecast method of an account.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/forecast-methods.jpg" alt="The three-dots menu of account 601000 Waste processing, with the methods Set to zero, Extend, copy and copy + trend. The account shows its current method, copy + trend." />
</Frame>

### Forecast an account without history

To forecast an account that has no figures yet, for example a new rent contract, click **Search GL account**. Search for the account, select it, and click **Next**. Enter the amounts per period in **Edit values**, and click **Submit**.

<Frame caption="Search for the account to forecast.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/select-gl-account.jpg" alt="Select GL account dialog with the search term rent and a list of accounts, such as 610000 Rent land and buildings and 610200 Rent machinery and equipment, and a Next button." />
</Frame>

### Add a note

Add a note to a figure to explain an assumption, for example why you expect turnover to drop. Click the note icon to the left of a yellow cell, type the note, and click **Save**. **Clear** removes it.

The note is only shown on that cell in the forecast editor. It doesn't appear in the reports or the client portal.

## Save and manage scenarios

Your changes aren't saved automatically. Click the save icon at the top right, or use the three dots next to it:

- **Save scenario** saves your changes in the current scenario.
- **Save as new scenario** saves your changes as a new scenario. Enter a name, for example *Best case*, and click **Confirm**.
- **Delete scenario** removes the current scenario.
- **Edit name & description** renames the scenario.

<Frame caption="The options of a scenario.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/scenario-menu.jpg" alt="Menu next to the save icon, with Save scenario, Save as new scenario, Delete scenario and Edit name & description." />
</Frame>

To switch to another scenario, choose it next to **Scenario** at the top of the editor.

Click **+** next to it to start a new scenario from the automatic forecast. Your unsaved changes in the current scenario aren't kept, so save them first. To build on your changes instead, use **Save as new scenario**.

<Frame caption="Switching between the scenarios of a forecast.">
  <img src="/images/forecasts/scenario-select.jpg" alt="The Scenario selector open, with the scenarios Default and Best case, and a + button next to it." />
</Frame>

To remove the whole forecast with all its scenarios, click **Delete forecast** at the top right.
