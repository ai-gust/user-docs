This part of the documentation is for everyone working with aiGust in their day-to-day job.
Here you learn how to:

- [sign in](/guide/getting-started/log-in), and [find your way around](/guide/getting-started/navigation)
- read the [dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard) and the [financial reports](/guide/reports/overview) of an administration
- compare an administration with its peers in the [benchmarks](/guide/benchmarking/how-it-works)
- follow up on [alerts](/guide/triggers/alerts) when something needs your attention
- make [forecasts](/guide/forecasts/how-forecasts-work), and calculate [tax prepayments](/guide/forecasts/tax-prepayments) and [capital gains tax](/guide/capital-gains-tax/basics)
- prepare and hold [meetings](/guide/meetings/basics) with your clients
- [share the figures](/guide/client-portal/share) with your clients in the client portal

If you're trying to understand how to connect data sources or brand/customize/configure aiGust for your whole firm, go to the [Key user guide](/setup/welcome).

## Where to start

[Sign in](/guide/getting-started/log-in), and take a look around. Then open an administration you work on, and go through the [first steps](/guide/getting-started/first-steps). They take a few minutes, and make sure the figures, KPIs and benchmarks are right from the start.
