## Tabs instead of a menu

The client portal has no side menu. Your client opens its parts with the tabs at the top. The charts and tables look the same as in aiGust.

| Tab | What your client sees |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | The active [KPI dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard) of the administration. |
| **Meetings** | The meetings you [published](/guide/meetings/publish). Meetings you haven't published don't appear. |
| **P&L**, **Balance**, **Cashflow** | The [profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow](/guide/reports/overview), in the graphical view and the table view. |
| **Customers**, **Suppliers** | Their largest customers and suppliers, how they evolve, and the customer or supplier credit. See [Customers and suppliers](/guide/customers-suppliers/overview). |
| **Users** | The users with access to the client portal of this administration. Your client can grant and revoke access themselves. See [Let your client manage users](#let-your-client-manage-users). |

Your firm decides which tabs your clients see, and you can change that for one administration. In the screenshot above, **P&L** is turned off. See [Choose what your client sees](/guide/client-portal/share).

## Only public notes and comments

Wherever your client looks, in slides or in financial tables, they only see the notes and comments you made **public**. Internal notes and comments stay within your firm. See [Internal and public notes](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries#internal-and-public-notes) and [comments](/guide/reports/table/cell-details).

## Data settings

Your client can change less than you. Under **Data settings**, they can only switch:

- **Year-to-date view**
- **Show open periods**

See [Years, quarters and periods](/guide/reports/table/periods).

<Frame caption="The data settings of a client.">
  <img src="/images/client-portal/portal-data-settings.jpg" alt="Data settings menu in the client portal with two switches: Year-to-date view off and Show open periods on." />
</Frame>

You decide the rest, and it's fixed for your client:

- **The peer group** they're compared with. The portal says so at the top: "We compare you with …".
- **How far the figures go**, shown as "Your data is updated until …".
- **The forecast.** Your client sees an activated forecast, but can't change it or switch it off. See [Forecasts in meetings and the client portal](/guide/forecasts/use-forecasts#forecasts-in-meetings-and-the-client-portal).

By default, the portal follows your settings for the administration. You can set a different peer group or date for the client portal. See [Choose what your client sees](/guide/client-portal/share).

## Several administrations

A client with access to more than one administration, for example a holding and its operating company, switches between them with the administration button at the top right.

## Let your client manage users

With the **Users** tab, your client can give access to the client portal to others, for example a co-owner or their CFO, without asking you. They grant and revoke access in the same way as you do on the **Client access** tab of the administration. See [Give your client access](/guide/client-portal/give-access).

Your client only sees this tab if:

- the **Users** tab is turned on for the administration, and
- their role is **External user (admin)**. With **External user (read only)**, they don't see it.

## See the portal as your client does

Open the administration in aiGust, and click **Client portal** at the top of the dashboard. You then see the administration exactly as your client does, with the same tabs and settings. Click the cross at the top right to close the preview and go back to aiGust.

You can also click **Preview Client portal** on the **Client portal** tab of the administration settings.
