---
title: "Give your client access"
description: "Give someone at your client access to the client portal of an administration, or take it away again."
---

Give the people at your client access to the [client portal](/guide/client-portal/client-view) of their administration, for example the owner or the CFO. A client user only sees the administrations they have access to. Everyone at your firm with access to the administration can give access.

Each client user has a role:

- **External user (read only)** sees the client portal.
- **External user (admin)** can also give others at their company access, if your firm allows it. See [Client roles](/setup/client-portal/invite-client-users#client-roles).

## Give access

1. Open the administration, and click **Settings** at the bottom of the administration menu.
2. Open the **Client access** tab. It lists everyone with access to the client portal of this administration.
3. Click **Grant Access**.
4. Enter the **Email** of the person, and click **Next**.

aiGust checks the email address:

- **The client user already exists,** for example because they already have access to another administration. aiGust says so. Click **Next** to add this administration to the ones they can see.
- **The email address can't be used,** for example because it belongs to a user at your firm. aiGust shows: "The provided email already exists but cannot be used as an external user."
- **The email address is new.** Enter the **First name**, **Last name**, **Language** and **Role**. Choose whether to **Send a welcome mail**, and click **Next**. aiGust creates the client user, with access to this administration.

## Revoke access

To take away someone's access to this administration, click the three dots next to them and choose **Revoke access**. To revoke several at once, select them and click **Revoke Access** at the top. The client user keeps access to their other administrations.

## Edit a client user

Choose **Edit client user** in the same menu to change their name, language or role.
