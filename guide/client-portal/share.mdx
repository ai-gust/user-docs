title: "Choose what your client sees"
description: "Choose which tabs, figures and peer group your client sees in the client portal of one administration."
---

Your firm sets which tabs clients see by default. For one administration, everyone at your firm with access to it can change that, and choose how far the figures go and which peer group your client is compared with.

1. Open the administration, and click **Settings** at the bottom of the administration menu.
2. Open the **Client portal** tab.

## Other tabs for this client

Under **Page visibility**, turn off **Use tenant defaults**, and turn each tab on or off. With **Use tenant defaults** on, the administration follows your firm's settings. See [Control what clients see](/setup/client-portal/control-what-clients-see).

## Which figures your client sees

Under **Data sharing settings**:

- **Data updated until:** turn on **Use a different period than in the portal**, and choose the last period your client sees. This works like the [up-to-date-until date](/guide/concepts/financial-periods#one-date-for-your-firm-one-for-your-client), but only for your client. You can work with figures up to July, while your client only sees them up to 31 March.
- **Comparison group:** turn on **Use a different comparison group in the client portal**, and choose the [peer group](/guide/benchmarking/peer-groups) your client is compared with.

With a switch off, the client portal follows the settings of the administration in aiGust: the up-to-date-until date and the active peer group your firm uses.

The client portal shows both at the top: "We compare you with …" and "Your data is updated until …".

To check the result, click **Preview Client portal** at the top right.
