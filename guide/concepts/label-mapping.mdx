---
title: "Map accounts to labels"
description: "Review the labels aiGust suggested for an administration's accounts, confirm them and correct them where needed on the Mapping tab."
---

When an administration comes into aiGust, it suggests a [label](/guide/concepts/labels) for each account. Review those suggestions once, preferably the first time you work on the administration.

<Note>
Label KPIs such as energy costs include every suggested label, also the ones you haven't confirmed. A wrong suggestion therefore skews the administration's label KPIs, and the benchmarks of the peer groups it's part of. See [Confirmed and unconfirmed labels](/guide/concepts/labels#confirmed-and-unconfirmed-labels).
</Note>

## Open the Mapping tab

Open the administration and go to **Settings** › **Mapping**.

<Frame caption="The Mapping tab: a summary of what still needs checking, and the accounts grouped by class.">
  <img src="/images/concepts/mapping-tab.jpg" alt="Mapping tab with a Work to do banner showing 8 fairly certain, 1 uncertain case and 5 unlabeled, and below it the accounts grouped by class, each mapped to a label with a percentage" />
</Frame>

At the top, the **Work to do!** banner shows how many accounts still need checking:

- **Fairly certain:** the suggestion has a certainty of 80% or more. A quick check is usually enough.
- **Uncertain cases:** the suggestion has a certainty below 80%. Check these carefully.
- **Unlabeled:** aiGust couldn't suggest a label. Choose one yourself.

Below the banner, the accounts are grouped by class (1 to 7), and within a class by their first two digits. Each group shows whether all its accounts are verified, or how many are left to check.

Each row shows:

- **The account** from the bookkeeping, with its number and description
- **The label** it's mapped to, with its code and description
- **The certainty** of the suggestion, as a percentage
- **The status:** a green check mark for a verified label, an orange icon for a label still to check

## Check the suggestions with Resolve

The quickest way through the open suggestions is the **Resolve** wizard. It takes you through the accounts to check, one group at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the wizard">
    Click **Resolve** in the **Work to do!** banner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each group">
    The wizard shows one group per step, starting with the fairly certain mappings. For each account:
    - If the label is right, click the check mark at the end of the row, or click **Verify all** to confirm the whole group.
    - If the label is wrong, choose another one. See [Change a label](#change-a-label).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go through all steps">
    Click **Next** to continue to the next group, such as the uncertain cases and the unlabeled accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the wizard">
    Complete the last step. Only then does aiGust save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/concepts/mapping-resolve.jpg" alt="Resolve wizard titled Mapping: labels we are fairly certain about, listing 8 accounts with their suggested labels and certainty, with Verify all at the top and Back and Next at the bottom" />

## Check accounts on the Mapping tab

You can also work directly in the list on the Mapping tab, for example to check one class, or to find a specific account.

1. **Find the accounts.** Open a class, or type part of an account number or description in the search field.
2. **Verify or change the labels.** Click the check mark next to a label to verify it, or click **Verify all** to verify a whole group. **Clear** undoes the changes in that group.
3. **Save.** The bar above the list shows how many changes you made. Click **Save** to keep them, or **Clear all** to undo them all.

### Options

Click **Options** to change what the list shows:

- **Only show unverified labels:** hide the accounts you've already verified.
- **Only show used GL accounts:** hide accounts without bookings.
- **Use mapped GL account codes:** show the compliance codes from the administration's Silverfin mapping instead of the codes from the bookkeeping. See [How labels affect your reports](/guide/concepts/labels#how-labels-affect-your-reports). This option only changes the codes shown on this page, not the mapping itself. It will be removed in a future version.

## Change a label

Click the arrow next to a label to open the label picker.

<Frame caption="The label picker, with suggestions limited to labels that fit the account.">
  <img src="/images/concepts/mapping-dropdown.jpg" alt="Label picker for account 440000 showing the current label Suppliers, and under Other options three suggested labels, with the toggle Suggestions for 444000 only switched on" />
</Frame>

- **The current label** is at the top, with its certainty.
- **Other options** lists other suitable labels. By default the picker only suggests labels whose filter fits the account, for example only supplier labels for a supplier account.
- **To see all labels,** switch off **Suggestions for [account] only**, and search by code or description.

<Note>
You can map any account to any label, but labels outside the suggestions are rarely right. Choosing one can move the account to a different section of the reports. See [How labels affect your reports](/guide/concepts/labels#how-labels-affect-your-reports).
</Note>

## Map per business partner

For utilities accounts, you can choose a label per business partner instead of one label for the whole account. Many administrations book the bills of the energy supplier and those of the water company on a single utilities account. By mapping per business partner, you still get separate figures for energy and water.

An account that supports it shows a building icon next to its label. The badge on the icon shows the number of business partners. Click the icon to show the business partners, and choose a label for each one.

## Map a foreign chart of accounts

For an administration with a foreign chart of accounts, such as an English subsidiary, aiGust's suggestions are rarely useful. The account numbers don't follow the Belgian structure. Map the accounts yourself:

1. Open the label picker for each account.
2. Switch off **Suggestions for [account] only**.
3. Search for the right label by description, and select it.

Once the accounts are mapped, aiGust reports on the administration in the Belgian format.
