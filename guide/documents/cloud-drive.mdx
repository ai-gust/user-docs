The cloud drive of an administration works like Google Drive: create folders and subfolders, and upload files. Use it for documents from outside aiGust, such as depreciation schedules or meeting reports. You can then show them on a slide with the [External document](/guide/meetings/widgets/external-document) widget.

Click **Documents** in the administration menu, and choose **Cloud drive**.

## Who can see the files

The files are private. Only users at your firm with access to the administration can open and download them. Client users don't see the cloud drive in the client portal. That's different from your firm's [branding assets](/setup/branding/branding-assets), which are publicly available. Don't store confidential documents as branding assets.

## Upload files

1. Open the folder you want to upload to.
2. Click **Upload**, or drag and drop the files onto **Select files to upload** below the list.

You can upload PDF, JPG, PNG and WebP files.

Click the three dots on a file to **Download** or **Delete** it.

## Create a folder

1. Open the folder to add the new folder to.
2. Click **New folder**.
3. Enter a **Name**. To create several levels at once, separate them with a slash, for example `2025/depreciation`.
4. Click **Create**.

A new folder is only visible to you until you upload a file to it. A folder without files disappears.

Click a folder to open it. The path above the list, such as **External Documents** > **afschrijvingen**, shows where you are. Click a part of it to go back.

## Files from Silverfin

If the administration is also in your firm's [Silverfin connector](/setup/data/bookkeeping/silverfin), the cloud drive has two extra folders, marked **External sync**:

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Silverfin** | The permanent files of the administration in Silverfin. |
| **Silverfin Period** | The files in Silverfin that belong to a specific period. |

Files you already uploaded to Silverfin, you don't have to upload again.

aiGust doesn't copy these files. When you use one on a slide, aiGust fetches it from Silverfin at that moment.

You can only view and use these files in aiGust. You can't upload, download or delete them here: manage them in Silverfin.
