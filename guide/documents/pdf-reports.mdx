---
title: "PDF reports"
description: "Download and delete the PDF reports aiGust generated for an administration."
---

<Warning>
PDF reports are being reworked. You can't create new PDF reports for now. We expect them back in Q4 2026, with templates to design your own reports.
</Warning>

A PDF report is a PDF document with aiGust's financial reports, generated in aiGust. Click **Documents** in the administration menu, and choose **PDF Reports**.

The list shows the PDF reports created before, with their **Report template**, **Date**, **Language**, **Progress** and **Status**:

- **done:** the PDF is ready.
- **error:** the PDF couldn't be generated.

A **Draft report** badge next to the template shows the report is a draft.

Click the three dots on a report to **Download** or **Delete** it.
