---
title: "Documents of an administration"
description: "Upload images and PDFs to an administration, so you can use them in meetings."
---

Each administration in aiGust has its own documents: a drive where you keep files for that client, such as a depreciation table or a report made in other software. You can then show them on a meeting slide with the [External document](/guide/meetings/widgets/external-document) widget.

## Upload a document

Click **Documents** in the administration menu, and upload the file. You can upload images and PDFs, and organise them in folders.

## Documents from Silverfin

If your firm has a Silverfin connector, aiGust also shows the administration's files from Silverfin. You don't need to upload them. See [Silverfin documents](/guide/documents/silverfin-documents).
