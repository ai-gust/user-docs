---
title: "Silverfin documents"
description: "Use the files of an administration in Silverfin in aiGust, without uploading them."
---

If your firm has a [Silverfin connector](/setup/data/bookkeeping/silverfin), aiGust finds the administration's files in Silverfin. They appear in the administration's [documents](/guide/documents/document-drive), in folders marked **External sync**, such as **Silverfin** and **Silverfin Period**.

You can use these files like your own documents, for example on a meeting slide with the [External document](/guide/meetings/widgets/external-document) widget. You don't need to upload them.
