The meeting editor is where you prepare a meeting. On the **Meetings** page, click the three dots on a meeting and choose **Manage slides**. A new meeting you create with **Add** opens here too.

- **On the left,** the **Slides**: all slides of the meeting, grouped into sections.
- **On the right,** the canvas: the selected slide, with the administration's real figures.

## Buttons at the top right

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Not published** | Shows whether your client can see the meeting. See [Publish a meeting](/guide/meetings/publish). |
| **Settings** | Changes the settings of the whole meeting. See [Change the meeting settings](/guide/meetings/start-and-run#change-the-meeting-settings). |
| **Preview** | Presents the meeting from the slide you're on. |
| **Save** | Saves your changes. |
| Undo and redo | Undoes or redoes your last change. |
| Bug | *Key users only.* Takes a screenshot and logs a bug ticket, as everywhere in aiGust. See [Report a bug](/setup/support/report-a-bug). |
| Close | Closes the editor. |

Your changes aren't saved automatically. Click **Save** before you close the editor.

## Work with slides

In the **Slides** list:

- **Add a slide** with **Add slide**.
- **Change the order** by dragging a slide.
- **Click the three dots** on a slide for more options:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Move up**, **Move down** | Moves the slide. Dragging works too. |
| **Hide slide** | Hides the slide when you present, without deleting it. Use it for a slide that doesn't apply to this client. On a section slide, it hides the whole section. |
| **Make section slide** | Turns the slide into a section slide, or back into a normal slide. See [Sections](#sections). |
| **Duplicate slide** | Adds a copy of the slide. |
| **Delete slide** | Removes the slide. |
| **Open slide settings** | Opens **Slide properties**. See [Slide settings](#slide-settings). |

### Sections

The slides are grouped into sections, such as **Intro**, **Past** and **Action points**. A section starts with a section slide, and the slides below it belong to it until the next section slide. To start a new section, choose **Make section slide** on its first slide.

Each section has a number. A section can have a time period, shown as a badge next to its name, such as **2025**. When you present, each section is a numbered circle in the navigation bar, and the slides in it are small dots.

### AI summary

Click **AI summary** below the slides to add a summary slide. aiGust sends all notes of the meeting to an AI model, and turns them into a summary. See [Summarise the notes](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries#summarise-the-notes).

### The intro slide

The first slide introduces the meeting. It shows your firm's logo, the administration's name, the meeting title, and the sections with their periods. Click it to open **Intro slide settings**:

- **Show logo:** show or hide the logo.
- **Background:** **Default**, a **Color**, or a **Background image**, with a **Text color** that stays readable on it.

## Slide settings

Above the canvas, a bar holds the settings of the selected slide.

<Frame caption="The settings of a slide.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/slide-toolbar.jpg" alt="The bar above the canvas with 2025 (time period: past, present or future), By year (aggregation level: period, quarter or year), Notes (slide notes) and Layout & time period (title and layout)." />
</Frame>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Time period** | Whether the slide is about the past, the present or the future. See [Past, present and future](/guide/meetings/basics#past-present-and-future). |
| **Reporting level** | Shows the figures by period, by quarter or by year. |
| **Notes** | Opens the notes of the slide. See [Notes and action points](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries). |
| **Layout & time period** | Opens **Slide properties**. |

In **Slide properties**, you set:

- **Navigation name:** the label of the slide in the navigation bar.
- **Layout:** the zones of the slide. See [Slide layouts](/guide/meetings/slide-layouts).
- **Time period:** **Future**, **Year-to-date** or **Last closed financial year**, with the dates each one covers.
- **Reporting level:** by period, by quarter or by year.

## Fill in the zones

To change what a slide shows, click a zone on the canvas. **Zone configuration** opens:

- **Click a data zone** to choose a widget. See [Widgets](/guide/meetings/widgets/overview).
- **Click the text zone** to change the title and the text. See [Text](/guide/meetings/widgets/text).

The widgets on the canvas work like elsewhere in aiGust. You can switch the KPI type of a chart, or drill down into a quarter, also while you present.
