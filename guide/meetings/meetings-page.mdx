Click **Meeting** in the administration menu. The **Meetings** page shows a tile for each meeting of the administration, with its meeting type, the **Data updated until** date, the language, and who created it.

Turn on **Only show published meetings** to see only the meetings your client can see.

## Start an existing meeting

Each tile has a button to present the meeting. Click the arrow next to it to choose [presenter mode or fullscreen mode](/guide/meetings/quickstart#presenter-mode-or-fullscreen-mode).

When you stop presenting, aiGust remembers the meeting and the slide you were on. That meeting's tile has different buttons:

| Button | On which tile | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Start**, with a play symbol | Every other meeting | Presents the meeting from the first slide. |
| **Continue**, with a play symbol and a bar | The meeting you presented last | Goes on from the slide where you stopped. |
| **Restart**, with round arrows | The meeting you presented last | Presents the meeting again from the first slide. |

## Create a meeting

1. Click **Add**.
2. In **Create a new meeting**, check the settings aiGust proposes, such as the meeting flow and the language. See [Meeting properties](/guide/meetings/properties).
3. Click **Submit**.

The meeting opens in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor), so you can prepare it before you present it.

To create a meeting and present it right away, use the **Start** button at the top right of the administration instead. See [Quickstart a meeting](/guide/meetings/quickstart).

## Edit, duplicate or delete a meeting

Click the three dots on a tile:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Meeting details** | Changes the properties of the meeting. See [Change the properties](/guide/meetings/properties#change-the-properties). |
| **Manage slides** | Opens the meeting in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor). |
| **Duplicate** | Makes a copy of the meeting. |
| **Remove** | Deletes the meeting. |
| **Open meeting notes** | Shows all notes of the meeting. See [Take notes](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries). |
| **Export** | Downloads the meeting's definition as a JSON file. See [Export and import](#export-and-import). |
| **Publish meeting** | Makes the meeting visible to your client. See [Publish a meeting](/guide/meetings/publish). |

On the tile itself, **Notes** opens the notes too, and **Not published** publishes the meeting.

## Export and import

**Export** downloads the meeting's definition as a JSON file. Use it as a backup: **Import** at the top of the page adds it again.

Only import files you exported from aiGust. An edited file that no longer follows aiGust's format can't be imported.
