Every slide in a meeting has a layout. The layout divides the slide into zones: a text zone, data zones, or both. You can't place things freely, as in PowerPoint. It works more like a photo book: you choose a page layout, then fill in the zones.

## Choose the layout

1. Open the slide in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor).
2. Click **Layout & time period** above the canvas.
3. Under **Layout**, choose a layout.
4. Fill in the zones.

The layouts come in pairs: with a text zone on the left, or without.

| Data zones | With text zone | Without text zone |
|---|---|---|
| None | Only text | |
| One | Text and one data zone | One data zone |
| Two, side by side | Text and two data zones | Two data zones |
| Two, stacked | Text and two data zones | Two data zones |
| Four | Text and four data zones | Four data zones |
| Six | Text and six data zones | Six data zones |

When you switch to a layout without a text zone, the text of the slide disappears.

### Match the layout to the widget

You can put any widget in any data zone: aiGust doesn't check whether it fits. But not every widget works in every zone. A financial table, such as the full P&L, doesn't fit in a small card. A KPI card does. With a text zone, the data zones are smaller, so fewer widgets look good in them.

- **Use one data zone** for tables, such as the [P&L](/guide/meetings/widgets/pl) or the [Balance](/guide/meetings/widgets/balance), and for [external documents](/guide/meetings/widgets/external-document).
- **Use four or six data zones** for [KPI cards](/guide/meetings/widgets/kpi-card) and [scores](/guide/meetings/widgets/score).
- **Check the slide** before the meeting. The canvas shows the real figures, so you see right away whether everything fits.

## Titles and text

Click the text zone on the canvas to change the title, the navigation name and the text of the slide. See [Titles and text on a slide](/guide/meetings/titles-and-text).

## Data widgets

Click a data zone on the canvas to open **Zone configuration**. Under **Visual selection**, choose a widget, such as a report, a chart or a score, and set its options.

See [Widgets](/guide/meetings/widgets/overview) for every widget and its options.
