Make a meeting look like your firm's, or like your client's. You customise the intro slide, and you can add your client's logo to every slide.

## The intro slide

The first slide introduces the meeting. It shows the administration's name, the meeting title, and a timeline of the sections with their periods.

In the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor), click the intro slide to open **Intro slide settings**.

### Your firm's logo

Under **General settings**, turn **Show logo** on or off. It shows your firm's logo on the intro slide, whatever background you choose.

### The background

Under **Background**, choose one of three options:

| Option | What the intro slide looks like | Text color |
|---|---|---|
| **Default** | Your firm's main accent colour, with aiGust's pattern of blocks on top. | White or black, chosen automatically for enough contrast with your accent colour. |
| **Color** | A colour you choose. | White or black, chosen automatically for enough contrast with your colour. |
| **Background image** | An image you choose. | You choose it under **Text color**: one of the **Standard colors**, or click **Choose** under **Custom color** to pick any colour. |

### A background image

With **Background image**, you can use any image you like. aiGust scales it to fit the slide. For the best result, use an image in 16:9, the shape of the slide.

1. Choose **Background image**.
2. Under **Image**, click the image to choose another one. The list of your firm's branding assets opens.
3. Choose an image, or click **Upload** to add a new one. See [Branding assets](/setup/branding/branding-assets).
4. Under **Text color**, choose a colour that stays readable on your image.

## Your client's logo

You can also show your client's logo, on the intro slide and in the bottom left corner of every other slide.

1. Set the logo of the administration. See [The administration's logo](/guide/dashboard/administration-properties#the-administrations-logo).
2. In the meeting settings, turn on **Show administration logo**. See [Change the properties](/guide/meetings/properties#change-the-properties).

## Other ways to adapt a meeting

- **Hide slides** that don't apply to this client. Click the three dots on the slide and choose **Hide slide**. See [Work with slides](/guide/meetings/editor#work-with-slides).
- **Highlight part of a title,** hide the time period above it, or add a highlighted box below the text. See [Change the title, text and navigation](/guide/meetings/slide-layouts#change-the-title-text-and-navigation).
