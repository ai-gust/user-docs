---
title: "Publish a meeting to your client"
description: "Share a meeting with your client through the client portal."
---

Your client doesn't see a meeting until you publish it. After the meeting, publish it so your client can look back at the slides and the public notes in the [client portal](/setup/client-portal/overview).

## Publish a meeting

Do one of these:

- In the meeting editor, click **Not published** at the top right.
- On the **Meetings** page, click **Not published** on the meeting's tile, or click the three dots and choose **Publish meeting**.

The meeting then appears in the meetings overview of the client portal. On the **Meetings** page, turn on **Only show published meetings** to see which meetings your client can see.

To unpublish a meeting, click the same button again.

## What your client sees

- **Only published meetings.** Meetings you haven't published don't appear in the client portal.
- **Only public notes.** Internal notes stay hidden. See [Internal and public notes](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries#internal-and-public-notes).

Check the notes before you publish. A note marked public by mistake is visible to your client.
