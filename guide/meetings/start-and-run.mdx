You create a meeting from an administration. There are two ways to start, depending on whether you want to present right away or prepare first.

| Where | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Start** at the top right of the administration | Create a meeting and present it right away, or continue a meeting you created before. |
| **Meeting** in the administration menu | See all meetings of the administration. A meeting you create here opens in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor), so you can prepare it first. |

## Start a meeting right away

Click **Start** at the top right. What you see depends on the meetings the administration already has:

- **No meetings yet:** *Ready for your first meeting?* Click **Let's get started** to create one.
- **Meetings exist:** **Available meetings** lists them. Click **Start** next to a meeting to present it, or **Create new meeting**.
- **You were presenting before:** *Continue where you left off?* Choose **Resume meeting** to go on from where you stopped, **Restart meeting** to start from the first slide, or **Choose another meeting**.

## The Meetings page

Click **Meeting** in the administration menu. The **Meetings** page shows a tile for each meeting, with its meeting type, the **Data updated until** date, the language, and who created it. Turn on **Only show published meetings** to see only the meetings your client can see.

<Frame caption="The meetings of an administration.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/meetings-overview.jpg" alt="Meetings page of Bouwwerken Michel with Import and Add buttons, the toggle Only show published meetings, and a tile for Operational flow 28/09/2026 with Notes, Not published, meeting type Annual, data updated until 08/2026, language English, and created by B. Coelmont." />
</Frame>

Click **Add** to create a meeting. Click the three dots on a tile for more:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Meeting details** | Changes the settings of the meeting. See [Change the meeting settings](#change-the-meeting-settings). |
| **Manage slides** | Opens the meeting in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor). |
| **Duplicate** | Makes a copy of the meeting. |
| **Remove** | Deletes the meeting. |
| **Open meeting notes** | Shows all notes of the meeting. See [Notes and action points](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries). |
| **Export** | Downloads the meeting's definition as a JSON file. Use it as a backup: **Import** at the top of the page adds it again. |
| **Publish meeting** | Makes the meeting visible to your client. See [Publish a meeting](/guide/meetings/publish). |

<Frame caption="The options of a meeting.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/meeting-menu.jpg" alt="Three-dots menu of a meeting tile with Meeting details, Manage slides, Duplicate, Remove, Open meeting notes, Export and Publish meeting." />
</Frame>

Only import files you exported from aiGust. An edited file that no longer follows aiGust's format can't be imported.

### Pick up where you left off

When you stop presenting, aiGust remembers the meeting and the slide you were on. The tile of that meeting has two buttons instead of **Start**:

- **Continue:** the purple button. Goes on from the slide where you stopped.
- **Restart:** the round arrows left of it. Starts the meeting again from the first slide.

Only the meeting you presented last has these buttons. The other tiles show **Start**.

## Choose the settings

When you create a meeting, the **Create a new meeting** dialog proposes a setup based on the current settings of the administration's dashboard. Check each one:

| Setting | What it decides |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The name of the meeting. aiGust proposes the flow's name and today's date. |
| **Meeting flow** | The template the meeting starts from. aiGust proposes the flow that fits the administration best. See [Meetings and meeting flows](/guide/meetings/basics#meetings-and-meeting-flows). |
| **Language** | **nl**, **en** or **fr**. The generated texts are written in this language. |
| **Forecast** | Optional. The forecast for the future slides. |
| **Peer group** | The peer group for the benchmarks. |
| **Up-to-date until** | The last period of the figures in the meeting. Below it, you see which period counts as **Future**, **Year-to-date** and **Last closed financial year**. |

Click **Submit**.

## Texts written by AI

aiGust can write the texts on your slides. It reads what each slide shows, the widgets and their figures, and writes a text to go with it, in the language of the meeting.

- **When:** as soon as you present or preview the meeting. aiGust then fills in every empty text field. You can also generate a text earlier, in the editor.
- **Your texts stay.** Once a field has text, written by aiGust or by you, aiGust never overwrites it.
- **Titles aren't generated.** They come from the meeting flow.

See [Text](/guide/meetings/widgets/text).

## Present a meeting

Click **Start** on a meeting, or **Preview** in the editor. With the arrow next to **Start**, you choose how to present:

| Mode | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Presenter mode: notes in a separate window** | You have two screens: one for your client, with the slides, and one for you, with the notes. |
| **Fullscreen mode: slides + notes together** | You share one screen with your client. The slides and the notes are on the same screen, so only public notes are shown. |

<Frame caption="Choose how to present.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/start-modes.jpg" alt="The Start button dropdown with Presenter mode: notes in a separate window, and Fullscreen mode: slides + notes together." />
</Frame>

### Presenter mode

1. Choose **Presenter mode**. aiGust opens a second browser window.
2. Move the window with the slides to the screen your client sees.
3. Click the full-screen button at the top right of that window.
4. Keep the other window, with the notes, on your own screen.

It works much like presenter view in Google Slides or PowerPoint. In the notes window, click a slide to see its notes, and add notes during the meeting.

  <img src="/images/meetings/presenter-mode.jpg" alt="Two browser windows. On the left, the slides window with the full-screen and close buttons highlighted at the top right. On the right, the notes window with the Slides list and a public note on the Past section." />

### Fullscreen mode

In fullscreen mode, click the round button at the bottom right of the slide to open the **Slide notes**. Your client sees them too, so only public notes are shown, and every note you add is public.

<Frame caption="Slide notes in fullscreen mode.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/fullscreen-notes.jpg" alt="A slide in fullscreen mode with the Slide notes panel open at the bottom right, showing a note This is a comment and an Add comment field." />
</Frame>

### Move through the slides

The navigation bar at the top of each slide shows where you are:

- **A numbered circle** for each section.
- **A small dot** for each slide within a section.

Click a circle or a dot to jump to that slide, or use the arrows.

## Change the meeting settings

A meeting is never fixed. Before or after you present it, you can change its slides and texts in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor), and its settings under **Meeting details** or **Settings** in the editor:

- **Meeting details:** **Title**, **Language**, **Description** and **Type**. Turn on **Add slide that summarizes meeting notes** to add a slide with the notes at the end, and **Show administration logo** to show the client's logo.
- **Time period:** **Data updated until**, and the **Available forecast**.
- **Benchmark group:** the peer group.

Click **Save**.
