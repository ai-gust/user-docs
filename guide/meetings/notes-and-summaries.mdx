Each slide of a meeting has its own notes. Use them for your talking points before the meeting, for what you discuss during the meeting, and for follow-up afterwards.

## Before and after the meeting

To see and add the notes of all slides, click **Notes** on the meeting's tile on the **Meetings** page, or choose **Open meeting notes** in its menu. **Meeting notes** opens, with a tab per section, such as **Intro** and **Past**, plus **General** and **Summary**. Under each slide, click **Add note**.

Besides the tabs per section:

- **General** holds notes about the meeting as a whole, not about one slide.
- **Summary** shows the AI summary, if the meeting has one. See [Summarise the notes](#summarise-the-notes).

In the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor), click **Notes** above the canvas for the notes of the selected slide.

## During the meeting

While you present, the notes are at hand, and you can add new ones live. In presenter mode you see all notes; in fullscreen mode only the public ones. See [Take notes during the meeting](/guide/meetings/quickstart#take-notes-during-the-meeting).

## Internal and public notes

Each note is internal or public:

| Type | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Internal** | Only you and your colleagues. Use it for things you don't want to show your client, such as a remark about a late payment. |
| **Public** | Your client too, when you present and in the client portal. |

## Work with a note

Each note has a few buttons at the top right. You find them in the notes panel of the editor, and in presenter mode:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Note** | The type of the note: a regular note, or an action point. See [Action points](#action-points). |
| **Unresolved** | Marks the note as resolved once you've dealt with it. |
| **Public** | Switches the note between public and internal. |

Click **Add a reaction** to reply to a note, for example when a colleague follows up.

In fullscreen mode, you can't change these: every note you add there is a public note.

When your client opens a [published meeting](/guide/meetings/publish), they only see the public notes.

## Action points

To flag a note as an action point, click **Note** on it. The button turns purple and shows **Action point**. Click it again to make it a regular note.

Use action points for what you or your client will do after the meeting. It's a visual flag: aiGust doesn't follow up on it or send reminders.

## Summarise the notes

To go over the notes at the end of the meeting, you have two options:

| Option | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **A slide with the notes** | The notes as you wrote them. Add the [Meeting notes overview](/guide/meetings/widgets/meeting-notes-overview) widget to a slide, or turn on **Add slide that summarizes meeting notes** in the [meeting settings](/guide/meetings/properties#change-the-properties). |
| **An AI summary** | A summary written by aiGust. Click **AI summary** below the slides in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor#ai-summary). aiGust sends all notes to an AI model, and turns them into a summary. |
