A meeting in aiGust is an interactive slideshow about a single administration. You build it from the same reports, charts and KPIs you use elsewhere in aiGust, with the administration's live figures. Use it for the annual review or an intermediate review with your client.

It works much like a presentation tool: you move between slides, and you take notes. But you don't copy charts into slides. Every slide shows aiGust's own visualisations, and you can still interact with them while you present.

## Meetings and meeting flows

Every meeting starts from a **meeting flow**: a template that decides which slides a meeting has, in which order, and which widgets each slide shows. Meeting flows help your firm run its client meetings in the same way, for example:

- an annual review for management companies
- an intermediate review for operating companies

aiGust provides one meeting flow out of the box. Your key users can copy and adapt it into flows of their own. See [Meeting flows](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows).

When you create a meeting, you choose a flow. aiGust proposes the flow that fits the administration best, for example a flow made for operating companies.

The meeting is a copy of the flow. You can add, remove and change slides without changing the flow, and later changes to the flow don't change your meeting.

| | Meeting flow | Meeting |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A template | A copy of a flow, for one administration |
| **Who makes it** | Key users | Everyone with access to the administration |
| **Where** | **Meeting flows** in the firm menu | **Meeting** in the administration menu |
| **Languages** | All the languages your firm presents in | Only the language of the meeting |

For the languages, see [Language](/guide/meetings/properties#language).

## Who can do what

- **Everyone** at your firm with access to the administration can create, edit and present meetings.
- **Key users** manage the meeting flows and meeting types.
- **Your client** only sees the meetings you [publish](/guide/meetings/publish), and only the notes you mark as public.
