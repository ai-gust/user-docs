With the **Start** button, you go from the administration to a presented meeting in a few clicks. aiGust proposes a flow and the settings, and writes the texts for you.

To prepare a meeting first, create it on the **Meetings** page instead. See [Meeting overview](/guide/meetings/meetings-page).

## Use the Start button

Click **Start** at the top right of the administration.

### Create the meeting

When you create a meeting, the **Create a new meeting** dialog proposes a flow and the settings, based on the current settings of the administration's dashboard. Check the **Language**: it should be the language of your client. Then click **Submit**. For each setting, see [Meeting properties](/guide/meetings/properties).

### Texts written by AI

You don't have to write the texts on the slides. As soon as you present the meeting, aiGust fills in every empty text field. It reads what each slide shows, the widgets and their figures, and writes a text to go with it, in the language of the meeting.

- **Your texts stay.** Once a field has text, written by aiGust or by you, aiGust never overwrites it.
- **Titles aren't generated.** They come from the meeting flow.

To change a text, open the meeting in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/titles-and-text).

## Presenter mode or fullscreen mode

With the arrow next to **Start**, you choose how to present:

| Mode | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Presenter mode: notes in a separate window** | You have two screens: one for your client, with the slides, and one for you, with the notes. |
| **Fullscreen mode: slides + notes together** | You share one screen with your client. The slides and the notes are on the same screen, so only public notes are shown. |

<Frame caption="Choose how to present.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/start-modes.jpg" alt="The Start button dropdown with Presenter mode: notes in a separate window, and Fullscreen mode: slides + notes together." />
</Frame>

### Presenter mode

1. Choose **Presenter mode**. aiGust opens a second browser window.
2. Move the window with the slides to the screen your client sees.
3. Click the full-screen button at the top right of that window.
4. Keep the other window, with the notes, on your own screen.

It works much like presenter view in Google Slides or PowerPoint.

<Frame caption="Presenter mode: the slides in one window, the notes in another.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/presenter-mode.jpg" alt="Two browser windows. On the left, the slides window with the full-screen and close buttons highlighted at the top right. On the right, the notes window with the Slides list and a public note on the Past section." />
</Frame>

### Fullscreen mode

The slides fill the screen, and the notes are on the same screen. Your client sees everything you see.

### Move through the slides

The navigation bar at the top of each slide shows where you are:

- **A numbered circle** for each section.
- **A small dot** for each slide within a section.

Click a circle or a dot to jump to that slide, or use the arrows.

## Take notes during the meeting

Each slide has its own notes. Write down what you discuss with your client as you go.

- **In presenter mode,** the notes are in the notes window on your own screen. Click a slide to see its notes, and add a note under it. You see all notes, internal and public, and you choose for each note whether it's public.
- **In fullscreen mode,** click the round button at the bottom right of the slide to open the **Slide notes**. Your client sees them too, so only public notes are shown, and every note you add is public.
