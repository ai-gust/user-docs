---
title: "Meeting notes overview"
description: "Summarise the notes and action points of the meeting on a slide."
---

Shows the notes and action points taken before and during the meeting. Put it on the last slide, to go over what you agreed with your client. See [Take notes](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries).

To add such a slide automatically, turn on **Add slide that summarizes meeting notes** in the [meeting settings](/guide/meetings/properties#change-the-properties).

Internal notes stay internal: your client never sees them, also not in this widget when they open the [published meeting](/guide/meetings/publish). See [Internal and public notes](/guide/meetings/notes-and-summaries#internal-and-public-notes).

## Options

Under **Display options**, choose which **Types of notes to show**: **Regular notes**, **Action points**, or both.
