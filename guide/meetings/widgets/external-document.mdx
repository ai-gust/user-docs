title: "External document"
description: "Show a PDF or image on a slide, such as a depreciation table."
---

Shows a PDF or an image on a slide, for example a depreciation table, or a document you made in other software.

While you present, you can zoom in and out. Nothing in the document is clickable.

## Options

Under **Display options**, choose the document. Search for it, or browse the folders:

- **Your own folders,** with the files uploaded to the administration's [cloud drive](/guide/documents/cloud-drive).
- **Silverfin folders,** marked **External sync**, if the administration is in your firm's Silverfin connector. aiGust fetches these files from Silverfin when you present. See [Files from Silverfin](/guide/documents/cloud-drive#files-from-silverfin).

A large zone. A full page is hard to read in a small zone.
