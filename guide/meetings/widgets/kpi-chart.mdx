---
title: "KPI chart"
description: "Show the evolution of one KPI over time, with or without the benchmark."
---

Shows one [KPI](/guide/concepts/kpis) over time, with or without the benchmark of the peer group. It's the same chart as on the [administration dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard). See [Benchmarks in charts](/guide/benchmarking/reading-benchmarks#benchmarks-in-charts).

While you present, you can switch the [KPI type](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types), and click a year to drill down into its quarters.

## Options

Under **Display options**:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **KPI** | The KPI to show, for example **P&L > Turnover**. Click the pencil to choose another one. Turnover is selected by default. |
| KPI type | The [KPI type](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types), with the icons next to the KPI. |

The chart always shows the benchmark of the peer group. To hide it:

- **While you present,** click the eye next to a benchmark line in the table below the chart.
- **For the whole meeting,** open the [meeting settings](/guide/meetings/properties#change-the-properties), and choose **Disable benchmarking** under **Benchmark group**.

## Where it fits

A medium or large zone.
