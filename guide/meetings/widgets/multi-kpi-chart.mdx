---
title: "Multi KPI Chart"
description: "Show several KPIs on one chart, for example to compare margins."
---

Shows several [KPIs](/guide/concepts/kpis) on the same chart, without benchmarks. Use it to show how related KPIs moved together, for example the gross margin, the EBITDA margin and the net margin.

To show one KPI with its benchmark, use the [KPI chart](/guide/meetings/widgets/kpi-chart).

## Options

Under **Display options**:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The title above the chart, in **nl**, **en** and **fr**. For example *Margin evolution*. |
| **Selected KPIs** | The KPIs to show. Click **+** to add one, and the cross to remove one. |
| KPI type | The [KPI type](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types) for all KPIs, for example as a percentage of turnover. |

By default, the chart shows the gross margin, contribution margin, EBITDA and EBIT as a percentage of turnover. All KPIs use the same KPI type, so they share one scale.
