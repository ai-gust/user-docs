Shows the KPI cards of the [administration dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard), with the chart of the selected KPI below them. While you present, click a KPI card to show that KPI in the chart.

## Options

Under **Display options**, choose the dashboard to show. By default, it's **Same as portal**: the dashboard the administration uses in aiGust. You can choose another dashboard, such as one of your firm's [KPI dashboard templates](/configure/reporting/kpi-dashboard-templates).

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone.
