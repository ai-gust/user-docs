---
title: "Revenue vs Personnel Cost vs FTE"
description: "Show how turnover, personnel costs and the number of FTEs evolved over time."
---

Shows the evolution of turnover, personnel costs and the number of full-time staff (FTE) over time. Use it to discuss whether the growth of the staff keeps pace with the growth of the business. It's the same chart as on the [personnel dashboard](/guide/personnel/dashboard).

The chart needs personnel data. See [Personnel data sources](/guide/personnel/data-sources).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A medium or large zone.
