---
title: "Score"
description: "Show how the administration performed, on its own or compared with its peer group, in one score."
---

Shows the administration's score for its last closed financial year: the **Administration score**, without a benchmark, or **Performance vs benchmark**, compared with the peer group of the meeting. See [Performance scores](/guide/dashboard/scores).

## Options

Under **Display options**:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show the score compared to the benchmark** | Shows **Performance vs benchmark** instead of the **Administration score**. |
| **Show the individual KPI cards below the score gauge** | Adds the KPIs behind the score, so you can explain it. |
| **Show the score chart** | Shows the score as a gauge chart. |

## Where it fits

A small zone. It fits well next to the KPIs behind the score.
