---
title: "Taxes"
description: "Show the estimated corporate tax and the tax prepayments, based on the forecast."
---

Shows the estimated corporate tax and the tax prepayments, based on the forecast chosen in the meeting settings. It's the **Taxes** tab of the forecast. See [Tax prepayments](/guide/forecasts/tax-prepayments).

This widget needs a forecast, and only works on a slide about the future. If the meeting has no forecast, the widget isn't available. Choose a forecast in the [meeting settings](/guide/meetings/properties#change-the-properties), and set the slide's time period to **Future**. See [The past, the present and the future](/guide/meetings/properties#the-past-the-present-and-the-future).

On a slide, the widget is split into three tabs: **Estimated tax**, **Prepayments** and **Net result**.

## Options

None.

You can change the figures live during the meeting, for example to try other prepayments with your client, and save them.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Unlike the other report widgets, it also fits next to a text zone.
