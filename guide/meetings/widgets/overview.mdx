Widgets are the building blocks of a slide: a report, a chart, a score, a document. Almost everything you see elsewhere in aiGust, you can show in a meeting. Each widget shows the administration's real figures, and follows the settings of the meeting and the slide.

## Add a widget

1. Open the slide in the [meeting editor](/guide/meetings/editor).
2. Click a data zone of the [slide layout](/guide/meetings/slide-layouts). **Zone configuration** opens.
3. Under **Visual selection**, choose the widget.
4. Set its options, if it has any.

To change the text of a slide, click its text zone instead. See [Titles and text on a slide](/guide/meetings/titles-and-text).

Some widgets need a forecast, such as **Taxes**. If the meeting has no forecast, they're greyed out.

## Which figures a widget shows

Data widgets take their figures from:

- **The meeting:** the up-to-date-until date, the peer group and the forecast. See [Meeting properties](/guide/meetings/properties).
- **The slide:** its time period and reporting level. See [Slide settings](/guide/meetings/editor#slide-settings).

## Choose widgets that fit

Not every widget fits every zone. A small zone can't hold a full table. Each widget page says which zones it suits. Preview the slide in the editor to check.
