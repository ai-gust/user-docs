title: "P&L waterfall"
description: "Show how turnover turns into profit, step by step, as a waterfall chart."
---

Shows the administration's [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss) as a waterfall chart: how turnover turns into profit, step by step. See [Waterfall chart](/guide/reports/visual/waterfall).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
