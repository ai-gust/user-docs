Shows the administration's [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss) as a waterfall chart: how turnover turns into profit, step by step. See [Waterfall chart](/guide/reports/visual/waterfall).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large or medium zone. It also fits next to a text zone.
