---
title: "Cashflow"
description: "Show the cash flow on a slide as a table."
---

Shows the administration's [cash flow](/guide/reports/definitions/cash-flow) as a table: where the cash came from, and where it went. In the widget panel, it's the **Cashflow** with **Table view**.

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
