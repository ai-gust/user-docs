---
title: "Table of contents"
description: "Show the sections and slides of the meeting on a slide."
---

Shows the sections and slides of the meeting, so your client knows what's coming. Put it on one of the first slides.

It follows the [sections](/guide/meetings/editor#sections) and navigation names in the meeting editor. When you add, move or hide a slide, it updates automatically.

Every slide gets a line, under its navigation name. A slide without a title or navigation name shows as an empty line, so give each slide a name. See [Title and navigation name](/guide/meetings/titles-and-text#title-and-navigation-name).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone, or a layout of its own.
