---
title: "Cashflow waterfall"
description: "Show how the cash position changed, as a waterfall chart with a table."
---

Shows the administration's [cash flow](/guide/reports/definitions/cash-flow) as a waterfall chart, with a table built in. In the widget panel, it's the second **Cashflow** widget. See [Waterfall chart](/guide/reports/visual/waterfall).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
