---
title: "Balance graph"
description: "Show the balance sheet as blocks, with its main ratios."
---

Shows the administration's balance sheet as blocks, with its main ratios, such as liquidity and solvability. See [Balance sheet visual](/guide/reports/visual/balance-sheet-blocks).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
