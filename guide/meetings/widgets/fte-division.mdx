description: "Show how the full-time staff is divided between categories, such as labour workers and office workers."
---

Shows how the full-time staff (FTE) is divided between categories, such as labour workers and office workers. It's the same chart as on the [personnel dashboard](/guide/personnel/dashboard).

The chart needs personnel data. See [Personnel data sources](/guide/personnel/data-sources).

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A small or medium zone.
