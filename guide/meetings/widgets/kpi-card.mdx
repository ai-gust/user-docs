---
title: "KPI card"
description: "Show one KPI as a number, with its benchmark."
---

Shows one [KPI](/guide/concepts/kpis) as a number, with its change compared with last year. Like the KPI cards on the [administration dashboard](/guide/dashboard/dashboard), it can show the benchmark of the peer group. See [Benchmarks in KPI cards](/guide/benchmarking/reading-benchmarks#benchmarks-in-kpi-cards).

## Options

Under **Display options**:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **KPI** | The KPI to show, for example **P&L > Turnover**. Click the pencil to choose another one. |
| KPI type | The [KPI type](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types) of the number, with the icons next to the KPI. |
| **Show benchmark** | Shows the benchmark of the peer group below the number. |
| **Benchmark KPI type** | The KPI type of the benchmark. Only shown when **Show benchmark** is on. |

The number and the benchmark don't need the same KPI type. For turnover, for example, show the amount, and benchmark the growth. See [KPI types in benchmarking](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types-in-benchmarking).

## Where it fits

A small zone. Use a layout with four or six data zones to show several KPI cards together.
