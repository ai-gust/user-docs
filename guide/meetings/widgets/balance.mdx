---
title: "Balance"
description: "Show the balance sheet on a slide as a table."
---

Shows the administration's [balance sheet](/guide/reports/definitions/balance-sheet) as a table: what the company owns, and how it's financed.

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
