---
title: "Capital Gain Tax"
description: "Simulate the capital gains tax with your client, on a slide."
---

Shows the capital gains tax calculation of the administration. Use it to simulate with your client how much a company could gain in value, and how much tax comes with it. See [Capital gains tax](/guide/capital-gains-tax/basics).

## Options

None. You can change the simulation live during the meeting, and save it.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone.
