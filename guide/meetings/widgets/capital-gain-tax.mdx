---
title: "Capital Gain Tax"
description: "Simulate the capital gains tax with your client, on a slide."
---

Shows the capital gains tax calculation of the administration. Use it to simulate with your client how much a company could gain in value, and how much tax comes with it. See [The capital gains tax](/guide/capital-gains-tax/basics).

## Options

None. On a slide, the widget is split into two tabs to save space: **Value Evolution** and **Tax Calculation**. You can change the simulation live during the meeting, and save it. See [Calculate the capital gains tax](/guide/capital-gains-tax/calculate).

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
