---
title: "P&L"
description: "Show the profit and loss on a slide as a table."
---

Shows the administration's [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss) as a table: the lines from turnover to the result, with their figures. It's the same table as the P&L report. See [Financial table](/guide/reports/table/financial-table).

The slide's time period and reporting level decide which columns it shows.

## Options

None.

## Where it fits

A large zone. Use a layout with one data zone, without a text zone: the widget needs the space.
