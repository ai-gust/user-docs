Most slides have a text zone next to the widgets. It holds the title of the slide and, depending on the [layout](/guide/meetings/slide-layouts), a text that explains the figures. Some layouts only have a title, without text.

Click the title or the text on the canvas. **Zone configuration** opens with up to three parts: **Navigation name**, **Title** and **Text**.

In a meeting, you only change the language of that meeting. In a [meeting flow](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows#set-up-every-language), you fill in every language: **nl**, **en** and **fr**.

## Title and navigation name

A slide has two names:

- **The title,** in large letters on the slide itself. It can be a full question, such as *How did your company perform compared to the competition?*
- **The navigation name,** in the navigation bar at the top of the slides and in the list of slides on the left. While you present, it shows your client where you are in the meeting. Keep it short, such as *Score vs bench*. Long names don't fit in the navigation bar.

### Use the title as navigation name

Filling in two names, in three languages in a meeting flow, is double work. To save it, turn on **Use slide title also as navigation label**. The **Navigation name** field disappears, and the navigation bar shows the title.

This works best for short titles.

<Frame caption="With Use slide title also as navigation label turned on, the title is also the navigation name.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/title-as-navigation.jpg" alt="Zone configuration of a slide in a meeting flow, with the language buttons nl, en and fr, and only the Title Slide & navigation, marked in pink, without a Navigation name field. </Frame>

<Note>
When you turn it off again, the navigation name starts as a copy of the title. The navigation name you had before is gone. Type it again if you still need it.
</Note>

### Highlight part of the title

In **Part of the title you want to highlight**, type the words of the title to show in your accent colour, such as *the competition*. Type them exactly as in the title. They must be consecutive words.

### Show or hide the time period

Above the title, the slide shows the time period it's about, such as *Last financial year 01/01/2025 → 31/12/2025*. Your client then always knows which figures you're discussing. Turn on **Hide time period marker** to leave it out.

<Frame caption="The time period marker above the title.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/time-period-marker.jpg" alt="Slide with the time period marker Last financial year 01/01/2025 → 31/12/2025 above the title, in a pink box, with an arrow from the switch Hide time period marker in the Zone configuration, which is turned off." />
</Frame>

The time period itself comes from the slide settings. See [Slide settings](/guide/meetings/editor#slide-settings).

Titles are never generated. A meeting takes them from its [meeting flow](/configure/meetings/meeting-flows).

## Text

The text explains the figures on the slide: what they mean, and what to discuss with your client.

### Let aiGust write it

aiGust can write the text for you. It reads the widgets on the slide and their figures, and writes a text in the language of the meeting.

- **Right away:** click **AI Suggest** in the **Text** part. This only works once the widgets show their data.
- **When you present:** leave the text empty. aiGust writes it the first time you present or preview the meeting.

Once a text exists, written by aiGust or by you, aiGust never overwrites it. Click **Clear all** to empty it, and let aiGust write a new one.

In a meeting flow, **Generate text automatically** decides whether aiGust writes the text of the slide. With several languages, some can have a text and others not. aiGust then writes the missing ones when you present.

### Write it yourself

Type in **Description**. Use **−** and **+** next to it to make the text smaller or larger.

You can format the text with a few simple codes:

| Type | To get |
|---|---|
| `* ` at the start of a line | A bullet list. |
| `**bold**` | **bold** |
| `*italic*` | *italic* |
| `***bold and italic***` | ***bold and italic*** |
| `---` on a line of its own | A horizontal line. |

Headings, such as `## Title`, and links don't work. They show up as plain text.

### Add a conclusion

Below the text, turn on **Show** next to **Highlighted**, and type your conclusion. It appears in a box in your accent colour, below the text. Use it for the one or two things your client should remember from the slide.
