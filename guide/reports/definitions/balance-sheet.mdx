The standard balance sheet follows the structure of the Belgian minimum standard chart of accounts (MAR/PCMN). It's the same for every administration, so its lines can be compared and benchmarked. See [Standard definitions](/guide/reports/overview#standard-definitions).

## Structure

The balance sheet shows the balance of each account at the end of the period. Each line takes the accounts starting with the numbers shown. Total assets and total liabilities are always equal.

## Column view

By default, the balance sheet shows assets and liabilities one below the other. Under **Settings**, turn on **Column view** to show them side by side.
