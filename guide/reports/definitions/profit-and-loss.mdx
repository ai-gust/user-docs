The standard profit and loss follows the structure of the Belgian minimum standard chart of accounts (MAR/PCMN). It's the same for every administration, so its lines can be compared and benchmarked. See [Standard definitions](/guide/reports/overview#standard-definitions).

## Structure

The profit and loss adds up the bookings of each period. Each line takes the accounts starting with the numbers shown. Lines marked with = are calculated from the lines above them.

<PnlStructure />

Each line can be opened down to the individual accounts, business partners or labels. See [Financial table](/guide/reports/table/financial-table).

## Contribution margin

The gross margin shows turnover minus direct costs. Many companies also have indirect costs that grow with their activity, such as renting machinery or extra staff. The **contribution margin** takes those variable costs into account as well:

- **Variable costs** rise and fall with activity.
- **Fixed costs** stay the same, whatever the activity.
- **The contribution margin** is the gross margin minus the variable indirect costs: what's left to cover the fixed costs and make a profit.

### Show the contribution margin

Under **Settings**, turn on **Show contribution margin**.

<Note>
The contribution margin only changes the part of the profit and loss between **Gross margin** and **EBITDA**. Instead of indirect costs, other income and other costs, you see:

- **Variable costs,** **Variable other income** and **Variable other costs:** the variable part of services, directors, interim workers and personnel (61, 62), of grants and other recurring income (740–749), and of other costs (64).
- **Contribution margin** = Gross margin − Variable costs + Variable other income − Variable other costs.
- **Fixed costs,** **Fixed other income** and **Fixed other costs:** the fixed part of the same accounts. Produced fixed assets (72), grants (736–739) and other recurring income (730–735) always count as fixed.
- **EBITDA** = Contribution margin − Fixed costs + Fixed other income − Fixed other costs. The result is the same as without the contribution margin.

All other lines stay as in the [structure above](#structure).
</Note>

The gross margin and EBITDA always stay the same. That keeps them comparable across administrations for benchmarking.

### Set the fixed/variable split

For each account, you set which percentage of its costs is variable. It doesn't have to be all or nothing: rent of machinery can be 80% variable and 20% fixed. Each account shows its percentage as a badge, for example **80% var**.

To change it, click the badge. In **Split Fixed/Variable Costs**, move the slider to the variable percentage, and click **Confirm**.

To change many accounts at once, use the **Mapping** tab of the administration, under **Settings** › **Mapping**: the same place as the [label mapping](/guide/concepts/label-mapping).
