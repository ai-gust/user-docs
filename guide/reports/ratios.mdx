Besides the lines of the standard reports, aiGust calculates the financial ratios below. Like every [KPI](/guide/concepts/kpis), you can use them in advanced search, triggers, dashboards and benchmarks.

The formulas use the lines of the [profit and loss](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss), the [balance sheet](/guide/reports/definitions/balance-sheet) and the [cash flow](/guide/reports/definitions/cash-flow). Ratios per FTE use the average number of employees from the [social balance sheet](/guide/personnel/data-sources), so they're only available for administrations with social data. Ratios based on a period shorter or longer than a year are converted to a full year.

## Liquidity and solvability

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Liquidity - Current ratio** | (Accounts receivable + Financial investments + Stock & work in progress + Liquid assets + Accrued accounts (assets)) ÷ (Short term debts + Accrued accounts (liabilities)) | Number |
| **Liquidity - Quick ratio** | (Accounts receivable + Financial investments + Liquid assets + Accrued accounts (assets)) ÷ (Short term debts + Accrued accounts (liabilities)) | Number |
| **Solvability** | Equity ÷ Total liabilities | Percentage |

## Working capital

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Net working capital** | Current assets − Short term debts − Accrued accounts (liabilities) | Amount in € |
| **Working capital need** | (Stock & work in progress + Accounts receivable + Accrued accounts (assets)) − (Short term debts + Accrued accounts (liabilities)) | Amount in € |
| **Customer credit** | ((Accounts receivable &lt; 1 year − Prepayments received − VAT payable) ÷ Turnover) × days in the period | Days |
| **Supplier credit** | ((Trade debts − Prepaid suppliers − VAT receivable) ÷ (Direct costs + Services + Interim workers + Directors)) × days in the period | Days |
| **Stock rotation** | (balance of accounts 3 ÷ balance of accounts 60) × 365 | Days |
| **Cash conversion cycle** | (Stock rotation + Customer credit) − Supplier credit | Days |

## Debt and cash

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Net cash** | balance of accounts 50–58 − balance of accounts 43 | Amount in € |
| **Net debt** | balance of accounts 17, 42, 43 − balance of accounts 50–58 | Amount in € |
| **Net debt / EBITDA** | Net debt ÷ Ebitda | Amount in € |

## Added value and productivity

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Added value** | balance of accounts 70–72, 74 + balance of accounts 740 + balance of accounts 60, 61 | Number |
| **Added value / FTE** | (Added value ÷ average number of employees in FTE (social balance sheet, code 1003)) × (365 ÷ days in the period) | Number |
| **Turnover / FTE** | (Turnover ÷ average number of employees in FTE (social balance sheet, code 1003)) × (365 ÷ days in the period) | Number |
| **EBITDA / FTE** | (Ebitda ÷ average number of employees in FTE (social balance sheet, code 1003)) × (365 ÷ days in the period) | Number |
| **Costs / FTE** | (Personnel ÷ average number of employees in FTE (social balance sheet, code 1003)) × (365 ÷ days in the period) | Number |

## Valuation

| Ratio | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Valuation according to the legally provided formula** | (Ebitda × 4) + Equity | Number |
