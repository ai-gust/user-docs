Leave a comment on any figure: for yourself, for your colleagues, or for your client. Click the figure to open its [cell details](/guide/reports/table/cell-details), and open the **Comments** tab. This works in every financial table, and in the [waterfall chart](/guide/reports/visual/waterfall).

A comment belongs to one line and one period. That can be a group, such as **Services** in 2025, or a single account below it.

## A comment is a thread

Type in **Add comment** to start a new comment. You and your colleagues can then reply to it with **Add a reaction**. Under a reaction, you see how many people read it.

</Frame>

Each comment has three switches at the top right. Click one to change it:

| Switch | Options |
|---|---|
| **Note** or **Action point** | A remark, or something someone still has to do. |
| **Unresolved** or **Resolved** | Whether the comment is dealt with. See below. |
| **Internal** or **Public** | Whether your client can see it. Public comments are visible in the [client portal](/guide/client-portal/client-view). Internal comments stay within your firm. |

## Resolve a comment

When a comment is dealt with, mark it **Resolved**. It then disappears from the list and from the table, as if you deleted it. It still exists, though: turn off **Show only unresolved comments** to see it again.

## Where you see comments in the table

A cell with a comment is highlighted in your firm's brand colour, with a speech bubble. The lines above it show a small speech bubble too, so you can find comments on lines you haven't expanded.

**Example:** in the screenshot below, account 610300 Office equipment rental has a comment in 25/26. **Services** and **Fixed costs** show a speech bubble in 25/26 too.

You don't always see a comment:

- **A comment on a quarter or a period** doesn't show in the year view or the year-to-date view. Switch to the quarter view to see it.
- **A comment on an account** only shows while you group by account. When you [group by business partner](/guide/reports/table/financial-table#detail-level) instead, you still see the speech bubble on the group, but not on the lines below it.
