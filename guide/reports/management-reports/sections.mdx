---
title: "Sections"
description: "Organise a management report in sections that hold line items, formulas and other sections."
---

A section is the group in a [management report](/guide/reports/management-reports/overview) that holds other groups: line items, formulas or other sections. It doesn't hold GL accounts itself. For example, *Turnover* holds the line items *Sales* and *Work in progress*.

A section shows with a boxes icon in the definition. Click the arrow at the end of its row to expand or collapse it.

In the report, a section shows the total of everything beneath it: it adds up all its groups.

## Add a section

1. Click a group at the level where you want the section.
2. Click **Add group**.
3. Under **Group settings**, choose **Section**.
4. Enter the **Name**, in each language: **NL**, **EN** and **FR**.

See [Add a group](/guide/reports/management-reports/editor#add-a-group).

## Add groups to a section

A new section is still empty. Click the dashed **Add subgroup** placeholder under it to add its first group.

To add more groups, click a group inside the section, and click **Add group**. You can also drag a group into the section.

A section inside another section can hold sections and line items. Formulas can only be at the top level. See [Formulas](/guide/reports/management-reports/formulas).

## Settings of a top-level section

A section at the top level of the report has more settings than one inside another section:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | Plus or minus: whether a [formula](/guide/reports/management-reports/formulas) adds or subtracts the section. |
| **Use as vertical reference value** | Makes the section the line that aiGust calculates all percentages against. See [Vertical reference](/guide/reports/management-reports/editor#vertical-reference). |
| **Show this row in a distinct colour in the report** | Highlights the row in the report. |
| **Open this section automatically** | Shows the section expanded when you open the report. |
