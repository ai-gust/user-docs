The profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow all show their figures in the same financial table. Each report has two tabs: **Table view**, described on this page, and **Graphical view**. See [Waterfall chart](/guide/reports/visual/waterfall) and [Balance sheet visual](/guide/reports/visual/balance-sheet-blocks).

## Settings

Click **Settings** above the table to change what it shows.

<Frame caption="The settings of the profit and loss.">
  <img src="/images/reports/report-settings.jpg" alt="Settings menu with Year-to-date view, Show open periods, Horizontal analysis, Show contribution margin, Show absolute values, Show relative values, Group by and Notifications" />
</Frame>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Year-to-date view** | Shows the same stretch of time in every year, instead of full financial years. See [Years, quarters and periods](/guide/reports/table/periods). |
| **Show open periods** | Adds a column for the open financial year. See [Years, quarters and periods](/guide/reports/table/periods). |
| **Horizontal analysis** | Adds the growth or decline between two columns, as a badge. |
| **Show contribution margin** | *Profit and loss only.* Splits the indirect costs into variable and fixed costs. See [Contribution margin](/guide/reports/definitions/profit-and-loss#contribution-margin). |
| **Show absolute values** | Shows the amounts. |
| **Show relative values** | Shows each line as a percentage of the reference value: turnover for the profit and loss, the balance sheet total for the balance sheet. Turn on both to see amounts and percentages together. |
| **Column view** | *Balance sheet only.* Shows assets and liabilities side by side. See [Balance sheet definition](/guide/reports/definitions/balance-sheet). |
| **Group by** | Chooses what the lowest level of the table shows. See [Detail level](#detail-level). |
| **Notifications** | Colours the horizontal analysis by how important a change is. See [Notifications](#notifications). |

## Work with rows

Click the arrow next to a line to open or close the lines below it. The lowest level shows the individual accounts, business partners or labels.

Click the three dots at the end of a row for more options for that row only:

<Frame caption="The options of a row.">
  <img src="/images/reports/row-menu.jpg" alt="Row menu with Show absolute values, Show relative values, Hybrid view, Open all, Close all, Group by business partner, Group by GL account and Group by label" />
</Frame>

- **Show absolute values**, **Show relative values** or **Hybrid view** (both) for that row.
- **Open all** or **Close all** lines below it.
- **Group by business partner**, **Group by GL account** or **Group by label** for the lines below it.

## Detail level

With **Group by** in the settings, or per row, you choose what the lowest level shows:

- **GL account:** the accounts as booked in the administration's bookkeeping.
- **Label:** the accounts translated to the [aiGust chart of accounts](/guide/concepts/labels).
- **Business partner:** the customers or suppliers behind the figures.

Grouping by business partner shows which customers or suppliers make up a section, for example the five suppliers behind most of your services and other goods. Most bookkeeping software doesn't offer that view.

### Change the label of an account

At account level, click the pencil next to an account to open **Manage label**. There you can check or change the [label](/guide/concepts/labels) of the account, without going to the Mapping tab.

## Notifications

With **Notifications** on, aiGust colours the badges of the horizontal analysis to draw your attention to the changes that matter. The colours range from dark green through light green to orange and red. A white badge means the change isn't important.

The colour depends on two things:

- **How large the line is compared with the total.** A cost of 0.1% of turnover doesn't matter much, even if it doubles.
- **How large the change is.** An increase of 100% matters more than one of 1%.

Click a figure to see its notification on the **Details** tab of the [cell details](/guide/reports/table/cell-details#details). There you can acknowledge it, or mark it as a false alert.

aiGust sets default sensitivities for the colours. Your firm can adjust them. See [Firm-wide report view settings](/configure/reporting/report-view-defaults).

This colour coding is separate from [triggers and alerts](/guide/triggers/how-triggers-work).

## Sort the table

Click the three dots on a column header to sort by that column: **Sort descending** or **Sort ascending**. In aiGust, three dots always mean there's more you can do.

<Frame caption="The options of a column: sorting, and drilling down into quarters or periods.">
  <img src="/images/reports/column-menu.jpg" alt="Column menu of 2025 with Sort descending, Sort ascending, Show quarters and Show periods" />
</Frame>

Sorting only changes the order of the rows at the lowest level. The structure of the report stays the same: turnover stays at the top, and the gross margin stays below turnover and direct costs. The sorted column is highlighted in the header.

## View settings

A few settings change how every report looks, such as the column order and compact notation. Your firm sets the defaults, and you can override them for yourself. See [Firm-wide report view settings](/configure/reporting/report-view-defaults).
