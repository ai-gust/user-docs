Click any figure in the financial table to open its details in a side panel. The top of the panel shows the line and the period, for example *Services, 2025YTD (01-05/2025)*, and an **Alerts** switch, to turn the notifications for this line on or off. The panel has these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**Transactions**](#transactions) | The bookings behind the figure |
| [**Contributors**](#contributors) | Who or what makes up the figure |
| [**Evolution**](#evolution) | The figure over the years, with its benchmark |
| [**Details**](#details) | The figure compared with the previous period, in euro and as a percentage of turnover, and its notification |
| [**Benchmark**](#benchmark) | The figure compared with the peer group, and its notification |
| [**Comments**](/guide/reports/comments) | Comments by you and your colleagues |

A number on a tab shows how many items it has.

### Notifications

aiGust gives every line of the report two notification scores:

- **Compared with the previous period:** how important the change is, in amount and in percentage. You see it on the **Details** tab, and as the colour of the badges in the report. See [Notifications](/guide/reports/table/financial-table#notifications).
- **Compared with the peer group:** how far the figure is from the benchmark, for example when the company is a top performer or lags behind. You see it on the **Benchmark** tab.

The colour goes from green through orange to red. Not every line is judged the same way: for some lines, the share of turnover matters most, for others the change in amount.

Is a line not relevant for this administration? Turn off **Alerts** at the top of the panel. aiGust then no longer flags that line.

## Transactions

The **Transactions** tab lists the bookings behind the figure. For turnover in financial year 2025, for example, that's every booking on the 70 accounts in that year. Together they add up to the figure in the cell.

  <img src="/images/reports/cell-transactions.jpg" alt="Side panel for Services 2025YTD on the Transactions tab, with a business partner filter, an Export Excel button, and a list of transactions with date, period, account, business partner, description, amount and an attachment icon" />

- **Filter** the list by business partner.
- **Sort** by date, description or amount.
- **Export** the list with **Export Excel**.
- **Open the attached document,** such as the invoice, with the paperclip icon.

To search through all transactions of an administration, use **Transactions** in the administration's menu. See [Search all transactions](/guide/transactions/overview#search-all-transactions).

<Note>
Administrations that only come from a [compliance connector](/guide/concepts/financial-periods#compliance-connectors), such as Silverfin, have no transactions in aiGust.
</Note>

## Contributors

The **Contributors** tab shows who or what makes up the figure. Click services for January to May 2025, for example, and you see which suppliers account for most of those costs.

<Frame caption="The main contributors to the services, with the biggest risers and fallers.">
  <img src="/images/reports/cell-contributors.jpg" alt="Contributors tab with a donut chart of the top five business partners of 2025YTD, the Group by menu open with business partner, GL account and label, and below it tables of the most important risers and fallers compared with 2024YTD" />
</Frame>

- **Top business partners:** a chart and a table of the five biggest contributors. Click **Show more** to add more, or the eye icon to leave one out.
- **Group by:** switch between business partner, GL account and label.
- **Most important risers** and **Most important fallers:** the contributors that changed most compared with the previous period, in absolute amounts.

The same view is available as the **Top contributors** widget on the customer and supplier report, and on meeting slides.

## Evolution

The **Evolution** tab shows the figure as a chart over the years, with the [benchmark](/guide/benchmarking/reading-benchmarks) of the administration's peer group. The chart follows the view you're in, financial year or year to date, and ends with the period you clicked. Use the [KPI type selector](/guide/concepts/kpis#kpi-types) to switch between amounts, growth and percentages.

<Frame caption="The evolution of sales, January to May of each year, with the benchmark.">
  <img src="/images/reports/cell-evolution.jpg" alt="Evolution tab showing sales from 2021YTD to 2025YTD as bars with a shaded benchmark band, and a table with the company's values, the benchmark quartiles and the sample size; the benchmark for 2021YTD is not available" />
</Frame>

Every line of the profit and loss and balance sheet is a KPI, so every line has its own benchmark. **Not available** means there were too few peers for that year.

## Details

The **Details** tab compares the figure with the previous period, and shows its [notification](/guide/reports/table/financial-table#notifications).

What you can compare depends on the line. At the top, choose one of the measures:

- **In €:** the amount, for example **Turnover in €**. Every line has it.
- **As a percentage of turnover:** for example **Gross margin / Turnover as a percentage (%)**. Lines like the gross margin and the costs have it too. The change is then in percentage points.

Each measure shows its change, such as ↗ 37,5%, and a notification badge with the number of alerts, in its colour: green with a thumb up when the change is good news, orange or red with an exclamation mark when it needs your attention.

Below, a chart shows the previous period, the change and this period, with the values underneath. A sentence sums it up, for example: *Compared to the previous period, the item Gross margin dropped 3,4% (percentage points) compared to Turnover.*

- **Acknowledge:** you've seen the change. The change badge of this line, for this period, loses its colour in the report. You still see the change itself.
- **False alert:** the change isn't an alert. It's no longer flagged, as if there was no alert.

Both only apply to this line and this period.

## Benchmark

The **Benchmark** tab compares the figure with the [peer group](/guide/benchmarking/reading-benchmarks) of the administration. Under **Available benchmarks**, choose what to compare. What's available depends on the line:

- **Turnover:** its growth, the **Percentage decrease/increase of Turnover compared to the previous period**. Turnover itself depends too much on the size of the company to compare.
- **Lines like the gross margin:** the line as a percentage of turnover, and the change in that percentage compared with the previous period, in percentage points.

On the left, you see the figure of the administration. On the right, the **Benchmark group**: the median as a yellow bar, with the band from the 25th to the 75th percentile (**Q25%** to **Q75%**).

When the figure stands out from the peer group, the tab shows a notification, with a sentence such as *You are above the 75th percentile of the benchmark and are thus performing better than the majority of your peers.* It also tells you whether higher or lower is better for this KPI. As on **Details**, you can **Acknowledge** it or mark it as a **False alert**.

## Comments

The **Comments** tab shows the comments on the figure, and lets you add one. See [Work with comments](/guide/reports/comments).
