The balance sheet's **Graphical view** tab shows it as blocks, so you see its structure at a glance.

## Layout

<Frame caption="The balance sheet as blocks, with the main ratios at the top.">
  <img src="/images/reports/balance-sheet-visual.jpg" alt="Balance sheet visual with KPI cards for Liquidity current ratio, Liquidity quick ratio, Solvability, Net working capital and Working capital needs; below them, total assets split into fixed assets and current assets on the left, and total liabilities split into equity and debts on the right" />
</Frame>

- **Total assets** are on the left, split into fixed assets and current assets.
- **Total liabilities** are on the right, split into equity and debts.

Both sides are always equal in total.

## Drill down

Click a block to see what it's made of. Click current assets, for example, to see stock and work in progress, accounts receivable and liquid assets.

<Frame caption="Current assets and liabilities, drilled down one level.">
  <img src="/images/reports/balance-sheet-visual-drilldown.jpg" alt="Balance sheet visual drilled down: current assets split into stock and work in progress, accounts receivable and liquid assets; equity shown as reserves; debts split into long-term and short-term debts" />
</Frame>

- **Back** goes up one level.
- **Reset** goes back to the top level.
- **The arrows** at the top right switch between years.

## Ratios

KPI cards at the top show the main balance sheet ratios:

- **Liquidity - Current ratio**
- **Liquidity - Quick ratio**
- **Solvability**
- **Net working capital**
- **Working capital needs**

Click a card to see how the ratio is calculated for this administration. A panel shows the value, a short explanation and the formula with its components. The chart then shows the blocks used in the calculation.

<Frame caption="The quick ratio: assets without stock divided by short-term debts and accounts payable.">
  <img src="/images/reports/balance-sheet-ratio.jpg" alt="Balance sheet visual with the Liquidity quick ratio card selected; a panel shows the value 0.83, the range 0.75 to 1.25, an explanation, and the calculation of active without stock divided by short-term debts plus accounts payable; the chart shows both amounts as blocks" />
</Frame>

The range below the value is the normal range for that ratio, for example 0.75 to 1.25 for the liquidity ratios, or 20% to 35% for solvability. Below the range is weak, above it is strong. The [Administration score](/guide/dashboard/scores#administration-score) uses the same ranges.

For the definitions of all ratios, see the [ratio reference](/guide/reports/ratios).
