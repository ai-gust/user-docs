A meeting in aiGust is a structured client conversation built around a live agenda, real-time financial figures, and a shared record that both you and your client can refer back to. Rather than juggling a slide deck, a spreadsheet, and a note-taking app at the same time, aiGust brings all three together in a single, purpose-built workspace designed for accounting professionals.

## Why aiGust Meetings Instead of a Generic Tool

Generic meeting tools — video calls, shared documents, calendar apps — were built for general-purpose collaboration. They leave you copying numbers from your accounting software, formatting slides by hand, and chasing up action items over email. aiGust meetings are different because the financial data is already there.

## The Meeting Lifecycle

Every aiGust meeting moves through four stages. Understanding the lifecycle helps you know what to do before, during, and after each client conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    Open the Meetings section, select a client, and create a new meeting. At this stage you choose the meeting type, assign a flow, and set a date. The meeting record is saved immediately so colleagues can see it in the client's timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare">
    Before the meeting, review the agenda, verify that the financial data blocks are pulling the correct figures, and add any client-specific notes to each section. You can also pre-populate discussion prompts or attach supporting documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run">
    Open the meeting on the day. Walk through each section of the flow with your client, referencing live data, capturing notes, and logging action items as you go. The editor auto-saves continuously so nothing is lost if you need to switch tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete">
    Mark the meeting as complete when the conversation ends. aiGust locks the financial snapshot so the record accurately reflects the numbers at the time of the meeting, then lets you share the summary with your client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can return to a meeting in the **Prepare** stage as many times as you like before the meeting date. Use this time to refine the agenda after you receive updated financials.
</Tip>

## Where to Find Meetings

Meetings live in the **Meetings** section of the aiGust sidebar. From there you have two views:

- **All Meetings** — a chronological list of every meeting across all clients, filterable by status, meeting type, or assignee.
- **Client Timeline** — open any client record and switch to the **Meetings** tab to see only that client's history.

You can also access upcoming meetings from the **Dashboard**, where aiGust surfaces the next scheduled meeting for each of your active clients.

<Note>
  If you cannot see the Meetings section in the sidebar, your firm administrator may need to enable the module for your account. Contact your aiGust admin or reach out to support.
</Note>

## Types of Meetings

aiGust ships with three default meeting types. Each type comes with a suggested flow, although you can swap in any custom flow you have built.

| Meeting Type | Typical Cadence | Default Flow |
|---|---|---|
| **Quarterly Review** | Every three months | P&L summary, cash flow, KPIs, action item review |
| **Annual Review** | Once per year | Full-year performance, tax position, goals for next year |
| **Ad-hoc** | As needed | Blank canvas — build the agenda on the fly |

<Tip>
  Use the **Ad-hoc** type whenever a client calls with a specific question — for example, a cash flow concern or a potential acquisition. Logging it as an aiGust meeting keeps the conversation on record and lets you attach supporting figures.
</Tip>

You can also create your own meeting types in **Settings → Meeting Types** if your firm has recurring conversations that do not fit the defaults — for example, a monthly bookkeeping catch-up or an onboarding session for new clients.
