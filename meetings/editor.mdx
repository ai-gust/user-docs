The aiGust meeting editor is a rich-text workspace that lives inside every meeting section. It lets you combine formatted prose, live financial data blocks, and action items into a single, structured record — no copy-pasting figures from another tab, no switching to a separate note-taking app. Everything you write and every data block you insert is tied to the meeting and stored against the client record automatically.

## What the Editor Contains

When you open any section of a meeting, the editor occupies the lower half of the screen below the section's data blocks. It provides:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rich Text Formatting" icon="text-size">
    Headings, paragraphs, bullet lists, numbered lists, bold, italic, and inline code — the same formatting controls you expect from a modern document editor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Financial Data Blocks" icon="chart-bar">
    Embeddable widgets — P&L snippets, KPI cards, bar charts, and cash flow summaries — that pull live figures from the client's connected data source.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Action Items" icon="circle-check">
    Inline action item entries with assignee and due date, visible across the whole meeting and synced to aiGust's task tracker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-save and Versioning" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Every keystroke is saved continuously. A version history lets you step back through earlier drafts of the meeting notes at any time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Insert Financial Data Blocks

You can insert a data block anywhere in the editor — not just in the pre-configured positions set by the flow. This is useful when a client raises a figure mid-conversation that you want to surface immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Position Your Cursor">
    Click in the editor at the point where you want the data block to appear. Place it after a heading or at the end of a paragraph to keep the notes readable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Block Menu">
    Press **/** (forward slash) to open the block insertion menu, or click the **+** icon that appears in the left margin when you hover over an empty line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Block Type">
    Type a keyword or scroll the menu to find the block you need:

    | Block Type | Best Used For |
    |---|---|
    | **P&L Snippet** | Showing revenue, expenses, and net profit for a selected period |
    | **KPI Card** | Highlighting a single metric with period-over-period movement |
    | **Bar Chart** | Visualising a monthly or quarterly trend |
    | **Cash Flow Summary** | Presenting operating, investing, and financing cash flows |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Block">
    A configuration panel slides in from the right. Set the period, comparison baseline, and any line-item filters, then click **Insert**. The block appears in the editor immediately, populated with live data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you insert a data block during a meeting and the figures look unexpected, click the **⟳ Refresh** icon on the block to force a data sync. If the issue persists, check the client's data source connection in **Settings → Data Sources**.
</Tip>

## Format Text, Headings, and Lists

The editor's formatting toolbar appears at the top of the editor pane whenever the editor is active. You can also use keyboard shortcuts for the most common formats.

**Headings and paragraphs:**
- Highlight text and click **H2** or **H3** in the toolbar to apply a heading style. Use H2 for major topics within a section and H3 for sub-points.
- Press **Enter** at the end of a heading line to return to normal paragraph text automatically.

**Lists:**
- Click the bullet list icon (or press `Ctrl+Shift+8` / `Cmd+Shift+8`) to start an unordered list. Use bullet lists for observations, risks, or anything without a strict order.
- Click the numbered list icon (or press `Ctrl+Shift+7` / `Cmd+Shift+7`) for ordered lists. Use numbered lists for sequential steps or ranked priorities.

**Emphasis:**
- `Ctrl+B` / `Cmd+B` — **bold** for key figures, names, or decisions.
- `Ctrl+I` / `Cmd+I` — *italic* for qualifications or caveats.

<Note>
  Avoid over-formatting meeting notes. Bold the most important figure or decision in each section rather than bolding multiple phrases — this makes the post-meeting summary easier to scan at a glance.
</Note>

## Add Action Items and Assign Them

Action items can be created from within the editor inline, keeping them contextually attached to the section where they were raised.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Action Item Block">
    Press **/** and select **Action Item**, or click **Add Action Item** in the floating toolbar above the editor. An action item entry form appears inline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the Task">
    Type a clear, concise description of what needs to be done. Start with a verb — for example, *"Send updated cash flow forecast"* or *"Confirm Q3 budget with operations team."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign and Set a Due Date">
    Click the **Assignee** field and search for a colleague or select **Client** to assign the task to the client. Then click **Due Date** and choose a date from the calendar. Both fields are optional, but setting them ensures the task appears in the assignee's task list and triggers a reminder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Action Item">
    Press **Enter** or click outside the form. The action item appears as a checkbox in the editor and is added to the meeting's action item panel on the right side of the screen. Assignees receive an in-app notification immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review all action items in the right-hand panel before you click **Complete Meeting**. Confirm each one has an assignee and due date — unassigned action items are easy to overlook after the meeting ends.
</Tip>

## Save and View Version History

The editor saves your notes automatically and continuously while you type. There is no manual save button. A small **Saved** indicator in the top-right corner of the editor confirms that the latest content has been persisted.

To view or restore an earlier version:

1. Click the **⋯** menu in the editor toolbar and select **Version History**.
2. A panel opens on the right showing a timestamped list of saves. Each entry shows the author and the time of the save.
3. Click any version to preview it in read-only mode. The main editor dims to indicate you are viewing a historical version.
4. Click **Restore This Version** to replace the current editor content with the selected version, or click **Close** to return to the current version without making any changes.

<Note>
  Version history is retained for 90 days. After that, only the current (latest) version of the meeting notes is stored. If your firm requires a longer retention period, contact aiGust support to discuss your plan options.
</Note>

## Export Meeting Notes to PDF

You can export the notes for any meeting section — or the full meeting — to a PDF at any time, whether the meeting is in progress or complete.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Export Menu">
    Click the **⋯** menu in the editor toolbar (or in the meeting header for a full-meeting export) and select **Export to PDF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose What to Include">
    A dialog appears with the following options:

    - **All sections** or a specific section.
    - **Include data blocks** — renders charts and tables as static images in the PDF.
    - **Include action items** — appends the full action item list at the end of the document.
    - **Include client branding** — adds your firm's logo and the client's name to the header. This option is available if your firm has uploaded a logo in **Settings → Branding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and Download">
    Click **Export**. aiGust generates the PDF and downloads it to your browser's default download folder. The export typically completes in a few seconds. For meetings with many large charts, allow up to 30 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the PDF export as a leave-behind for clients who prefer a printed copy of the meeting summary. Pair it with the **Share Summary** email for clients who want both a digital link and a document they can file.
</Tip>
