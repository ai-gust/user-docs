Running a client meeting in aiGust keeps your agenda, live financial data, and note-taking in one place so you can stay focused on the conversation rather than switching between tools. This guide walks you through every step, from selecting a client to marking the meeting complete and sharing the summary.

## Before You Start

Make sure you have done the following before the meeting begins:

- The client's financial data source is connected and synced. Check the client record for a green **Data Synced** indicator.
- You have assigned a flow to the meeting during the prepare stage. If not, you can assign one at launch.
- Any documents or supporting files you want to reference are attached to the meeting in the **Documents** tab.

<Tip>
  Open the meeting in aiGust a few minutes early to verify that data blocks are loading correctly. If a block shows a loading error, navigate to **Settings → Data Sources** to trigger a manual sync before your client joins.
</Tip>

## Step-by-Step: Running the Meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Client and Open the Meeting">
    In the aiGust sidebar, navigate to **Meetings**. Find the meeting in your upcoming list — you can filter by date or search by client name. Click the meeting to open it. If you are starting an unplanned conversation, click **New Meeting**, select the client from the dropdown, choose **Ad-hoc** as the meeting type, and click **Start Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or Confirm a Flow">
    If the meeting already has a flow assigned from the prepare stage, aiGust loads the agenda automatically. If no flow has been assigned yet, a prompt appears asking you to select one from your flow library. Choose the flow that best matches the conversation and click **Apply Flow**.

    <Note>
      You can switch flows after a meeting has started, but doing so clears any notes you have already entered in the current agenda sections. Confirm the flow before the conversation begins to avoid losing work.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk Through Each Section">
    The meeting opens in presentation mode, showing the first section of the flow. Use the **Next Section** and **Previous Section** buttons — or press the right and left arrow keys — to move through the agenda. Each section displays:

    - The section title and any pre-written discussion prompts.
    - Live financial data blocks populated with the client's current figures.
    - A notes area for capturing what was discussed.

    Talk through the data with your client. Data blocks are interactive — click a figure to expand it into a detailed breakdown, or hover over a chart bar to see the underlying value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Notes in Each Section">
    Click anywhere in the notes area beneath a section's data blocks to start typing. The editor supports rich text — use the formatting toolbar to add bullet points, bold key figures, or insert a heading to separate topics within a section. Your notes auto-save every few seconds, so there is no save button to click.

    <Tip>
      Write notes in the past tense as the conversation progresses — for example, *"Client confirmed revenue target for Q3 remains £450k."* This makes the post-meeting summary immediately readable without editing.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log Action Items">
    When you or your client commits to a task, click **Add Action Item** in the toolbar at the top of any section. Fill in:

    - **Description** — what needs to be done.
    - **Assignee** — you, a colleague, or the client.
    - **Due date** — when it should be completed.

    Action items appear in a running list in the right-hand panel throughout the meeting so you can see everything that has been committed to at a glance. They are also visible to assignees in their aiGust task list as soon as you save them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the Meeting Summary">
    When you have worked through all sections, click **Share Summary** in the top-right corner. aiGust compiles the meeting notes, the data snapshots, and the action item list into a formatted summary document. A preview opens for you to review before sending.

    Choose how to share:

    - **Email to client** — aiGust sends the summary directly to the client's email address on record. You can customise the subject line and add a short message.
    - **Copy link** — generates a secure, read-only link you can paste into any communication.
    - **Download PDF** — produces a branded PDF you can attach to an email manually.

    <Note>
      The financial data in the summary is a snapshot taken at the moment you click **Share Summary**. It will not update if figures change later, ensuring the client always receives a consistent record of the meeting.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the Meeting as Complete">
    Click **Complete Meeting** in the top-right corner. aiGust:

    - Locks the meeting record so the notes and data snapshot cannot be edited accidentally.
    - Updates the client's meeting timeline with the completed status.
    - Notifies assignees of any action items that have been created.

    The meeting remains fully readable in the client's timeline — only editing is restricted. If you need to add a correction after completing, use the **Add Addendum** option, which appends a timestamped note without altering the original record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the Meeting: Quick Reference

| Action | How to Do It |
|---|---|
| Move to next section | **Next Section** button or → key |
| Expand a data figure | Click the figure in any data block |
| Add an action item | **Add Action Item** in the section toolbar |
| Check all action items | Action items panel on the right |
| Take a full-screen data view | Click the **⤢** icon on any chart or table |
| Pause and resume | Meetings never time out — close the tab and reopen to continue |

<Tip>
  If your client is joining remotely, share your screen and display the aiGust meeting view. The clean section-by-section layout is easy for clients to follow on a shared screen without exposing the rest of your aiGust workspace.
</Tip>
