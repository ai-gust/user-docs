This guide is for everyone who wants to integrate with aiGust — software vendors connecting their product, integration partners exchanging data, and developers building against the aiGust API.

## Ways to integrate

### API integration

Read data out of aiGust, create users, or embed an aiGust functionality in your own product. A key user of the firm creates an integration in the aiGust portal, and your software uses its client ID and secret to call the API.

- [Authentication](/integrate/authentication): get credentials and access tokens.
- [Use the aiGust API](/integrate/api): base URL and basics. The full API is documented in the [interactive API reference](https://api.prod.ai-gust.io/api/redoc) (ReDoc).
- [Provision client users](/integrate/provision-client-users): give your clients access to aiGust from your own portal.

Questions? Contact us at [support@ai-gust.io](mailto:support@ai-gust.io).

### External data uploads

Want to share data from your software to aiGust — for example bookings, invoices or master data? Contact us at [integrations@ai-gust.io](mailto:integrations@ai-gust.io).

Once you are set up, [these pages](/integrate/ext-upload-overview) take you through the complete upload flow. The credentials come with your upload connector, and the tokens work as for any [API integration](/integrate/authentication).
