The aiGust interface is designed around the way accounting firms actually work — you move fluidly between firm-level oversight and the granular detail of individual client files. Once you understand the layout, switching contexts, pulling up reports, and preparing for client meetings becomes second nature.

## Main Navigation Areas

The interface is divided into three persistent navigation elements that stay visible regardless of where you are in the platform.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sidebar" icon="sidebar" href="/concepts/navigating-aigust">
    The left-hand sidebar is your primary navigation panel. It lists all major sections — Dashboard, Reports, Insights, Meetings, and Settings — and updates its options depending on whether you are at the firm level or inside an administration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reports" icon="chart-bar" href="/reports/dashboard">
    Access all financial reports — profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, and the KPI dashboard — from the Reports section of the sidebar when inside an administration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Firm Settings" icon="building" href="/firm-setup/onboarding">
    Manage users, branding, connectors, and firm-wide preferences from Firm Settings, accessible via the top bar or the bottom of the sidebar.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Firm-Level vs Administration-Level Context

aiGust operates at two distinct levels, and knowing which one you are in determines what data you see.

- **Firm level** — This is the default view when you log in. Here you see an overview of your entire client portfolio: aggregated alerts, administration health scores, and a list of all client files. Actions taken at this level (such as applying labels or managing team members) affect the firm as a whole.
- **Administration level** — When you open a specific client file, you enter administration context. The sidebar updates to show sections relevant to that client: their KPIs, financial reports, meeting notes, and settings. A breadcrumb at the top of the page always shows you which administration you have open.

<Note>
  The breadcrumb trail in the top bar displays **Firm › Client Name** when you are inside an administration. Click the firm name in the breadcrumb to return to the firm-level portfolio view at any time.
</Note>

## Switching Between Administrations

You can move between client files without returning to the portfolio list each time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the administration switcher">
    Click the client name shown in the breadcrumb or the administration selector in the top bar. A panel slides out listing all administrations linked to the current firm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the administration">
    Type the client name or company registration number into the search field at the top of the panel. The list filters as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the administration">
    Click the administration you want to open. aiGust loads that client's context immediately, keeping you on the equivalent page where possible (for example, if you were on the Reports page, you land on that client's Reports page).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Search and Filter Functionality

The global search bar in the top bar lets you find content across both firm-level and administration-level data.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Global Search">
    Press `/` from any page to focus the global search field, or click the magnifying-glass icon in the top bar. Search results are grouped by type — **Administrations**, **Reports**, **Meetings**, and **Settings** — so you can jump directly to the right place without navigating manually.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Portfolio Filters">
    On the firm-level portfolio page, use the filter bar above the administration list to narrow results by label, sector, company type, or sync status. You can stack multiple filters and save the combination as a named view for future use.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In-Page Filters">
    Inside reports and insight screens, a secondary filter toolbar lets you scope the data to a specific period, compare against benchmarks, or isolate particular account categories. These filters apply only to the current view and reset when you navigate away.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Keyboard Shortcuts and Quick-Access Tips

Learning a handful of shortcuts dramatically speeds up day-to-day work in aiGust.

<Accordion title="Keyboard shortcuts">
  | Shortcut | Action |
  |---|---|
  | `/` | Focus global search |
  | `G` then `D` | Go to Dashboard |
  | `G` then `R` | Go to Reports |
  | `G` then `M` | Go to Meetings |
  | `G` then `S` | Go to Settings |
  | `Esc` | Close any open panel or modal |
  | `?` | Open the keyboard shortcut reference overlay |
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Pin your most-visited administrations by hovering over them in the portfolio list and clicking the star icon. Pinned clients appear at the top of the administration switcher panel, saving you from searching every time.
</Tip>
