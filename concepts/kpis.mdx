Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are the numerical heartbeat of every administration in aiGust. Rather than requiring you to build formulas or pull figures manually from a bookkeeping system, aiGust reads your client's synced data and calculates a standard set of financial metrics automatically — then surfaces them in a clear, period-aware dashboard you can act on and share.

## KPIs aiGust Tracks

aiGust calculates KPIs across four primary financial dimensions. Each metric is derived directly from the chart of accounts mapped during the bookkeeping data sync.

<Accordion title="Revenue & Growth">
  | KPI | What It Measures |
  |---|---|
  | **Total Revenue** | Gross income from all trading activities before any deductions |
  | **Revenue Growth** | Percentage change in revenue compared to the prior period |
  | **Recurring Revenue** | Portion of revenue from repeating contracts or subscriptions, where identifiable |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Profitability">
  | KPI | What It Measures |
  |---|---|
  | **Gross Profit** | Revenue minus direct cost of sales |
  | **Gross Margin %** | Gross profit expressed as a percentage of revenue |
  | **EBITDA** | Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation |
  | **EBITDA Margin %** | EBITDA as a percentage of revenue |
  | **Net Profit** | Profit after all operating expenses, interest, and tax |
  | **Net Profit Margin %** | Net profit as a percentage of revenue |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Liquidity & Cash">
  | KPI | What It Measures |
  |---|---|
  | **Cash Balance** | Total cash and cash-equivalent balances at period end |
  | **Operating Cash Flow** | Net cash generated by trading activities |
  | **Debtor Days** | Average number of days clients take to pay invoices |
  | **Creditor Days** | Average number of days the business takes to pay suppliers |
  | **Current Ratio** | Current assets divided by current liabilities |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Overhead & Efficiency">
  | KPI | What It Measures |
  |---|---|
  | **Overhead Ratio** | Total overheads as a percentage of revenue |
  | **Payroll Ratio** | Staff costs as a percentage of revenue |
  | **Revenue per Employee** | Total revenue divided by headcount (requires headcount entry) |
</Accordion>

## How KPIs Are Calculated

aiGust pulls figures directly from the synced bookkeeping data — no manual input is required for standard metrics. The calculation flow works as follows:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data sync">
    When you connect a bookkeeping source (such as Xero, QuickBooks, or Exact), aiGust imports the chart of accounts, transactions, and period balances on an automated schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account mapping">
    aiGust maps imported account codes to its internal schema. Standard accounts map automatically; any unrecognised codes are flagged for manual review in the **Mapping** section of Administration Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Formula application">
    Once mapping is confirmed, aiGust applies its KPI formulas to the mapped balances for each financial period. Formulas follow standard accounting conventions and are documented in the KPI reference in the Help Centre.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Benchmark overlay">
    Calculated KPIs are compared against the peer group defined by the administration's sector and company type, and the result is displayed as a benchmark indicator alongside each metric.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  KPI values update automatically after each data sync. The timestamp of the last sync is shown in the top-right corner of the KPI dashboard. If figures look outdated, trigger a manual sync from **Settings › Data Sources**.
</Note>

## Viewing KPIs on the Dashboard

The KPI dashboard is the first screen you see when you open an administration.

- The **summary row** at the top shows five headline metrics: Revenue, Gross Margin %, EBITDA, Net Profit, and Cash Balance. Each tile shows the current period value, the change versus the prior period, and a colour-coded trend indicator.
- Below the summary row, the **KPI grid** displays the full set of tracked metrics, grouped by dimension. Each card shows the value, the benchmark position (above, within, or below peer range), and a sparkline of the trailing twelve months.
- Click any KPI card to open a **detail panel** with a full period history, benchmark band chart, and the option to annotate the metric for a client meeting.

## Customising Which KPIs Are Shown

Not every KPI is relevant to every client. aiGust lets you tailor the dashboard to show only what matters for a given administration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter customisation mode">
    On the KPI dashboard, click the **Customise** button in the top-right corner. The grid enters edit mode and a panel of available KPIs slides in from the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or remove KPIs">
    Tick or untick KPIs in the panel to add or remove them from the dashboard. Drag cards within the grid to reorder them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a default view">
    Toggle **Set as default view** to save this layout as the default for this administration. Other team members with access to the administration will see your saved layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Done** to exit customisation mode. Your layout is saved immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also create KPI **presets** at the firm level — a named collection of KPIs you can apply to multiple administrations at once. This is useful when you want a consistent view across all clients in a particular sector or portfolio segment. Find presets under **Firm Settings › KPI Presets**.
</Tip>

## Comparing KPIs Across Periods

Period comparison is one of the most-used features in the KPI dashboard. aiGust supports several comparison modes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Prior Period">
    The default comparison mode shows each KPI against the immediately preceding period of the same length. For a monthly view, that means the prior calendar month. For a full-year view, it means the prior financial year.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Prior Year (Same Period)">
    Select **Prior Year** from the comparison dropdown to benchmark the current period against the same period twelve months ago. This is the most useful comparison for businesses with strong seasonality.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom Range">
    Choose **Custom** to define any two date ranges manually. aiGust calculates KPIs for both ranges side by side, letting you compare, for example, a post-restructure quarter against the same quarter before the change.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Budget vs Actual">
    If a budget has been imported for the administration, select **Budget** as the comparison target. Each KPI displays actual versus budget, with the variance shown in both absolute and percentage terms.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
