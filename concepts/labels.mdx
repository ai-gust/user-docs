As your client portfolio grows, finding and working with the right subset of administrations gets harder. Labels give you a flexible, free-form tagging system that sits on top of the built-in sector and company-type classifications. You decide what categories matter to your firm — and aiGust makes it instant to filter down to exactly the clients you need.

## What Labels Are and Why They Are Useful

A label is a short, coloured tag you create at the firm level and assign to one or more administrations. Labels are entirely custom — you define the name and colour, and they carry whatever meaning you assign to them.

Unlike sectors and company types, which feed into benchmarking calculations, labels are purely organisational. They do not affect how aiGust processes or displays financial data; they simply help you and your team navigate and prioritise your workload.

Common uses include:

- Marking administrations by **service tier** (e.g., *Premium*, *Standard*, *Advisory-Only*)
- Grouping clients by **responsible partner or manager** (e.g., *Team: Sarah*, *Team: Marcus*)
- Tracking **onboarding status** (e.g., *Onboarding*, *Data Review*, *Live*)
- Identifying **review cycles** (e.g., *Quarterly Review*, *Annual Only*)
- Flagging **special conditions** (e.g., *In Restructure*, *Pre-Sale*, *Grant Recipient*)

## How to Create a Label

Labels are created and managed from Firm Settings, making them available across the entire portfolio.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Firm Settings">
    In the top bar, click your firm name or the settings cog, then select **Firm Settings**. In the left-hand menu, click **Labels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new label">
    Click **+ New Label**. A creation form appears with two fields: **Name** and **Colour**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name">
    Type a short, descriptive name — ideally one to three words. Label names must be unique within the firm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a colour">
    Select a colour from the palette. Colours are displayed alongside the label name throughout the interface, so choose one that is visually distinct from your other labels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Label**. The label is now available to assign to any administration in the firm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only firm administrators can create, rename, or delete labels. All users with access to the firm can assign and remove labels on individual administrations.
</Note>

## Assigning Labels to Administrations

You can assign labels to a single administration or to multiple administrations in bulk.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Single Administration">
    Open the administration and click **Settings › Administration Details**. Scroll to the **Labels** field, click it, and select one or more labels from the dropdown. Your changes save automatically. Alternatively, hover over any administration in the portfolio list and click the label icon that appears — a quick-assign panel opens without leaving the list view.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bulk Assignment">
    From the firm-level portfolio page, tick the checkboxes next to the administrations you want to label. In the bulk actions bar that appears at the bottom of the screen, click **Assign Labels**. Choose the labels to add (or remove) and click **Apply**. The change is applied to all selected administrations simultaneously.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  An administration can carry multiple labels at once. There is no limit on how many labels you assign to a single client, so feel free to combine categories — for example, tagging a client as both *Premium* and *Quarterly Review* and *Team: Sarah*.
</Tip>

## Filtering the Administration List by Label

Once labels are assigned, filtering your portfolio is straightforward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the portfolio view">
    Navigate to the firm-level portfolio page if you are not already there. You will see the full list of administrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the filter bar">
    Click **Filters** above the administration list. A filter row expands with dropdowns for Label, Sector, Company Type, and Sync Status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more labels">
    Click the **Label** dropdown and tick the labels you want to filter by. The portfolio list updates immediately to show only administrations carrying at least one of the selected labels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Combine with other filters">
    Add additional filters — for example, filter by *Label: Quarterly Review* and *Sector: Retail* simultaneously to find all retail clients due for a quarterly meeting. Filters stack with AND logic by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as a view (optional)">
    Click **Save View**, give the filtered view a name, and click **Save**. The saved view appears in the left-hand sidebar of the portfolio page for one-click access in future sessions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
